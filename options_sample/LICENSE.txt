1. License scope
----------------

Choose a license at checkout according to the number of people who will
have access to the data:

  Single user    - one designated person.
  Team           - up to 10 people.
  Organization   - any number of people within one legal entity.

A person counts as a user if they can access the data files or a database
loaded from them. Someone who only reads reports or views charts or other
permitted results does not count as a user of the underlying data.

The license covers the person or legal entity for whom the purchase is
made. Employees, consultants and collaborators who can access the data
count toward the selected license's user limit. Working on the same project
does not extend a Single user license to other people.

For an organizational purchase, covered users are its employees and
consultants engaged to work on its behalf, within the selected user limit.
Parent companies, subsidiaries and other group entities need their own
licenses. An Organization license removes the user limit within one legal
entity; it does not cover an entire corporate group.

Free samples may be downloaded and evaluated without purchasing a license,
subject to the redistribution restrictions in section 3.

3. Redistribution restrictions
------------------------------

You may not sell, publish, share, sublicense or otherwise redistribute data
files, in whole or in part, to anyone outside your license scope. You may
not release derived outputs from which a substantial part of the data can
be reconstructed in usable form.

A substantial part is assessed by the usable data a recipient can recover,
rather than by a fixed row count or percentage of the archive. A chart of a
strategy's returns is a permitted result. A published table of daily
closing prices for a thousand tickers redistributes usable market data,
even if it represents a small fraction of the archive.

You may show limited example rows to explain research or an issue in a
paper, post, support request or bug report. Examples must not be
accumulated or organized into a collection that substitutes for the
underlying data.

Download links and access credentials may only be shared with users covered
by your license.

-------------------

You may train machine-learning models on the data, including models used
commercially.

If you publish a model or offer it as a service, users must not be able to
recover a substantial part of the underlying data through it. Predictions,
scores and classifications are permitted outputs, provided they comply with
section 3. A model that reproduces historical quotes on request is subject
to the same redistribution restrictions as other ways of supplying the
data.
