The honest picture
The strategies are well tested on 2018–2026 data, but nothing is proven live yet. Two of three evaluations failed, both on one ordinary full stop at a thin cushion, which happens in about half of all evaluations even in the backtest. The bigger risk in the plan is timing: in 13–31% of simulated histories, a bad November–December fails every evaluation and nothing is funded by year end.

What we decided on October 30

Accounts

  • No more new 50Ks. #2 runs to its end.
  • Every new account is a 150K No-Activation ($249, no activation fee).
  • Replace #3 with #4 on Sunday Nov 1 (catches the Sunday-evening Bridge trade).
  • From Nov 13: all job pay goes to 150Ks, one purchase a day, never several the same day.

Trading rules

  • Full size always: skipping or shrinking trades on a thin cushion tested worse.
  • Stop buying if the drift alarm fires (live clearly below backtest).
  • Mid-December gate: 40–70 live trades at ≥75% of backtest before scaling further.
  • Personal account opens once Apex pays for its own replacements (~March with the job).

The pipeline

StepWhat happens
1 · Write the rule firstRules and pass/fail gates are written down before results (pre-registration); no tuning after a failure
2 · Backtest honestly1-minute data 2018–2026, full costs, longs and shorts separately, by year, placebo controls
3 · Independent checkA separate checker rebuilds the rule from its written spec; MultiCharts must reproduce the trades
4 · Add or replaceTested on the account simulators as an addition and as a replacement; kept only if it helps the plan
5 · LiveApex incubation, compared trade by trade with the backtest (drift alarm), then the personal account
Why live will be lower than the backtest
The plan's strategies were chosen on the same 2018–26 data they're scored on, and published market effects lose about a quarter of their returns out of sample (more after publication). The plan is sized to work at 75% of the backtest; every projection below shows both.

5 · Funding model

Apex: why 150K accounts

An evaluation is a paid test: reach the profit target without touching the trailing floor. Passing gives a funded account that pays out in capped steps, then closes.

50K evaluation150K No-Activation
Price~$59 + $130 activation on a pass$249, no activation
Target / trailing floor+$3,000 / $2,000+$9,000 / $4,000
Pass rate (backtest)~50%~40%
Funded payouts, year 1 / 2 years~$10.2k / $12.3k~$18.1k / $20.3k
Funded account blown~5%~1%
Cost per funded account~$250~$600

Apex caps you at about 20 accounts, so account slots run out before money does. Each 150K slot earns ~65% more and rarely blows once funded, which is why every new account is a 150K.

Thin cushions: tested twice, keep full size

Losses = failed + unfinished evaluations (an unfinished one costs a new fee). Skipping trades lowers failures but the account stalls and runs out of days.

Read a failed evaluation correctly
About half of all evaluations fail even in the backtest. Two failures out of three happens about 40% of the time at these rates. Judge the strategies on live trades vs backtest, not on accounts lost.

Why a slow start, then a lot
A funded 150K needs +$6,600 and five $350+ days before its first payout: that takes ~2½–3½ months. After that, payouts arrive every few weeks until the account's ~$20.5k is paid. So January–February bring little or no trading income; March–December bring most of 2027.

7 · Personal account

Apex first, personal second

$249 in an evaluation that passes earns ~$14–20k; $249 in a personal account earns ~$200 a year. So money goes to Apex until it is at its account cap and pays for its own replacements. The personal account matters most from 2028: it has no payout cap.

With the job in 2027*

  • ~March: start setting aside the paycheck + ~25% of payouts
  • ~April–May: open with starter set A (~$11.5k)
  • ~Summer: core 16 pieces (~$26k)
  • 2027 contribution ~$5–10k

Job ends Dec 24

  • ~June: set aside ~25–50% of payouts
  • ~July–August: open with set A
  • ~Autumn: core 16 pieces
  • 2027 contribution ~$2–5k

Conditions: the mid-December gate passed, and Apex keeps funding its own slots. If payouts dip, Apex replacements get the money first.

What would make us stop
A drift alarm on a core strategy, live results clearly below 75% of the backtest at the mid-December gate, or nothing funded by mid-January. Any one of these pauses new purchases until the cause is understood.
