# Strategy Mechanics: Expiration Open Short (EOS)

**Strategy**: `Expiration Open Short` (`NQExpirationOpenShort` / Task 06)  
**Instrument**: CME E-mini Nasdaq-100 Futures (`NQ`) / Micro E-mini (`MNQ`)  
**Timeframe**: 5-Minute Bars  
**Direction**: Shorts Only  
**Target / Stop**: Fixed Time Exit at `10:00 CT` / Dynamic Stop at `Entry + 0.30 × ATR`  
**Execution Time**: 3rd Friday of the month at `08:35 CT` (next bar after 08:30 close)  
**Trade Frequency**: Exactly 1 trade per month (~12 trades per year)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Architecture

`Expiration Open Short (EOS)` is a specialized calendar-driven institutional flow strategy designed to exploit the structural morning liquidation pressure generated by options market maker delta-hedging on monthly options expiration (OPEX) days.

```
                 EXPIRATION OPEN SHORT ARCHITECTURE
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Calendar Trigger: Monthly OPEX 3rd Friday (Day-of-Month 15 to 21)   │
│ 2. Holiday Rule: If Friday is closed (Good Friday), SKIP (No trade)    │
│ 3. Entry Signal: 08:30 CT 5m Bar Close                                 │
│ 4. Execution: Sell Short 1 Contract next bar at MARKET (08:35 CT)      │
│ 5. Risk Control: Stop Loss at Entry + (0.30 × ATR-15) (~30-50 pts)     │
│ 6. Profit Capture: Systematic Time Exit at 10:00 CT 5m Bar Close       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. The Core Economic Engine: Why It Works

EOS does not look at charts, moving averages, or trend indicators. It is an unadulterated **market microstructure flow trade** driven by three structural market realities:

### A. Monthly Options Dealer Delta-Hedge Unwinding
* The third Friday of every month is the primary expiration for monthly index and single-stock options (AM and PM settlements).
* Market makers and institutional option dealers hold massive inventory across thousands of strike prices. Throughout the month, dealers dynamically hedge their options exposure using underlying equities and CME index futures (`NQ` and `ES`).
* On the Friday morning of expiration, as deep out-of-the-money and near-the-money options approach expiration, their deltas collapse rapidly toward zero (peak theta decay).
* Dealers who were long futures to hedge customer call buying or short put exposure are forced to **liquidate and unwind their long futures hedges**. This unleashes a concentrated wave of automated market-on-open and market-order selling across the 08:30 to 10:00 CT window.

### B. Institutional Portfolio Rebalancing & Rollover
* Pension funds, mutual funds, and multi-asset asset managers utilize monthly OPEX to rebalance equity weights and roll structured collar/overwriting hedges.
* Historically, portfolio managers harvest gains and rebalance cash positions during the opening 90 minutes of OPEX Friday, creating a persistent negative return skew between 08:30 and 10:00 CT.

### C. European Institutional Settlement Handover
* European markets close between 10:00 and 10:30 CT (16:00–16:30 London time). 
* The downward pressure from global portfolio rebalancing is concentrated strictly before 10:00 CT. Exiting at 10:00 CT captures the core meat of the unwind while exiting before afternoon short-covering rallies begin.

## 3. The Anatomy of Failure: Why and How It Loses

Because EOS blindly shorts the market at 08:35 CT once a month without regard for the prevailing trend, it fails under very specific macroeconomic and order-flow conditions:

```
Price
  ▲
  │                                    [Bullish Trend Day Runaway]
  │                                             ▲
  │                                      ┌──────┴──────┐
  │  ── Stop Loss (+0.30 ATR) ────────── ├─────────────┤ ── Hit Stop Loss (-$800)
  │                                      │             │
  │                                ┌─────┴─────┐       │
  │  ── Short Entry (08:35 CT) ─── ├───────────┤ ──────┼──────────────────
  │                                │ 5m Bar    │       │
  │                                │ (08:35)   │       │
  └────────────────────────────────┴───────────┴───────┴─────────────────► Time
```

### 1. Macro Economic Surprises (CPI / Fed / Payrolls)
If a major macroeconomic release prints at 07:30 CT on an OPEX Friday (e.g., unexpectedly cool CPI inflation, dovish central bank commentary, or strong retail sales) that ignites a broad-market risk-on rally:
* Macro algorithmic buy orders and global asset allocators deploy aggressive buying programs.
* This systemic macro buying volume dwarfs the localized dealer hedge unwinds.
* NQ surges +100 to +250 points, blasting straight through the 0.30 ATR stop loss within 10 to 20 minutes of entry.

### 2. Pre-Market Overnight Exhaustion ("Unwind Already Happened")
* If Asian and European sessions experienced intense overnight selling leading up to the 08:30 CT open, dealers may have already unwound the bulk of their delta hedges during overnight Globex trading.
* By 08:30 CT, NQ is already down -1.5% to -2.5% and extremely oversold.
* Instead of continuing lower, the cash open triggers a sharp "short-covering relief bounce," causing EOS to sell into the exact bottom of the session and trigger its stop loss.

### 3. Positive Gamma Pinning (Sticky Strike Traps)
* If there is an exceptionally massive concentration of Open Interest (OI) at a specific round strike (e.g., 20,000 Strike Pin), market makers become long gamma.
* Long gamma requires dealers to buy dips and sell rips, suppressing volatility and pinning price tightly to the strike.
* If NQ drifts even slightly above the strike, dealer buying forces price higher just enough to clip the tight 0.30 ATR stop before settling.

## 5. Live Execution & Apex Prop Account Management Rules

### A. Sizing & Trailing Drawdown Protection
* With an average stop of 0.30 × ATR (~35–50 pts on NQ = \$700–\$1,000 per full contract), a single loss on a full NQ contract consumes 35% to 50% of the \$2,000 Apex trailing limit.
* **Combine / Evaluation Rule**:
  - Always execute EOS with **2 to 4 MNQ micros** (\$140 to \$280 max risk) until account cushion reaches **\$2,500+**.
  - On funded accounts with full cushion, 1 NQ contract is appropriate.

### B. Execution Precision at 08:35 CT
* At 08:30:00 CT, the US cash equity market opens. The first 5 minutes (08:30 to 08:35 CT) exhibit the highest spread volatility and widest bid-ask disparity of the day.
* **Do not enter at 08:30:00**. The strategy specifically waits for the **08:30–08:35 CT 5-minute bar to close**, submitting the short order at **08:35:00 CT**. This avoids the chaotic 08:30:00 opening tick auction slippage.

### C. MultiCharts PowerLanguage Implementation
```pascal
// Exact Calendar Condition for 3rd Friday
condition1 = DayOfWeek(Date) = 6; // Friday
condition2 = DayOfMonth(Date) >= 15 and DayOfMonth(Date) <= 21; // 3rd Friday window
condition3 = Time = 0835; // Close of 08:30-08:35 bar

if condition1 and condition2 and condition3 and MarketPosition = 0 then begin
    SellShort ("EOS_Enter") next bar at market;
end;

if MarketPosition = -1 then begin
    // Stop Loss at Entry + 0.30 * Daily ATR
    SetStopLoss(0.30 * AvgTrueRange(15) * BigPointValue);
    
    // Time Exit at 10:00 CT
    if Time >= 1000 then begin
        BuyToCover ("EOS_TimeExit") next bar at market;
    end;
end;
```
