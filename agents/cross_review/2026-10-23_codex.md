## 2. Kusho/C03: roll adjustment reaches P&L but not the indicators

**Claim.** `checks/candidates/kusho/REVIEW.md:34` ranks C03 C and concludes that its ATR redesign did not help,
based on `analysis/kusho_test.py` as a reconstruction of the supplied native code.

**Position and evidence.** With default `RawRollHandling=true`, C03 subtracts `CumGap` before OHLC enters its
EMA, ATR and swing calculations (`C03.txt:436–465`). v9 likewise stores `Close - CumGap` for its DOWN regime

or bracket distances. This discrepancy remains even assuming matching input data, as the user requested.

A static check plus an invented +20-point splice demonstrates that the two calculations can produce different EMA/ATR
and DOWN flags; it does not measure historical frequency or profitability. Proof and source hashes are in the linked script/output.

**Practical consequence.** Exact-source Kusho/C03 rankings and associated account comparisons remain provisional.
This does not establish that either strategy improves the plan.

**Smallest deciding check / owner.** Claude compares native and Python adjusted indicator/signal values at a fixed roll
transition, including both RawRollHandling settings. Codex verifies the comparison. If signals differ, register a corrected
replay before new returns, keeping entry/exit choices and account gates frozen. **Unresolved; transformation mismatch confirmed.**

**Claim.** `tools/checks/Apex_Account_Check.txt:5` says a blank AccountName uses the chart's broker account.

**Position and evidence.** Line 12 actually calls `GetAccount(1)`. MultiCharts documents this as retrieving an indexed
entry from the broker account list, not the selected chart account. Its separate `GetAccountID` keyword describes
selected strategy-account retrieval; suitability in an indicator still needs checking. Primary references:
[account-list example](https://www.multicharts.cn/post?idno=106) and
[GetAccountID](https://www.multicharts.com/trading-software/index.php?title=GetAccountID).
Short vendor excerpts and the exact proposed indicator's source/blob reference are preserved under this review's
`risk/` inputs (`MULTICHARTS_ACCOUNT_API_PROVENANCE.md:13,15,30,38–40`).

**Practical consequence.** Two charts assigned to different accounts may both print the first account, falsely validating
the account balance feed for future cushion sizing. This is a **proposal/readiness defect**; no deployed sizing error is established.

**Smallest deciding check / owner.** Claude makes account identity explicit in the proposal and, on an isolated test
setup, verifies two exact account names and different expected balances against the intended chart assignments.
