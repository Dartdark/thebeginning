## 1. Participants and cause

Investors paying for volatility insurance may sustain a volatility risk premium. Inverse ETP exposure collects
some premium while bearing nonlinear crisis risk. No forced order, deadline or dollar flow is measured.
VIX cannot be held/delivered; convergence is risky exposure, not a cash-arbitrage lock.

## 2. Direction, size and clock

Source: front-VX/spot-VIX percentage basis >+8% buys XIV, below +6% closes; below −8% buys VXX, above −6%
closes. Optional capital fraction hedged by SH/SPY. Threshold strictness, relative-basis denominator, expiry
roll and order sequence must be confirmed against source code before an exact reproduction. No stop is defined.
Settlement/spot feature availability must precede decision/fill. ETP close cannot retrospectively execute a
signal that needs later confirmed VX settlement. A causal later fill needs its own frozen protocol.

## 3. Transmission

The assets are VIX ETPs and equity ETFs, not NQ/ES futures. Source average holding/change interval is 18 days.
No Apex day-trade equivalence follows. Inverse daily ETP payoff is not simply shorting a long ETP for many days.

## 4. Independent data

Owned spot VIX, VIXY and SPY date/schema inventories were inspected without returns. No complete source-era
XIV/VXX/SH panel identified. Prior dated VX acquisition exists in Calvados's manifest; raw scratch panel is
absent locally, not universally unavailable. Source vendor offers original synthetic packages for purchase;
modern free synthetic products are not exact replacements. Bounded historical TXT fetches failed. No private
API key, product purchase or authentication was used. Market/indicative and pre/postlaunch eras must stay separate.

## 5. Costs and intraday gaps

The source uses 0.20% fee and 0.15% spread on each buy/sell. That does not establish executable close prices,
current liquidity or survival. Short/borrow costs would arise in a short-VIXY adaptation, absent from holding
XIV; do not silently apply NQ costs or assume the same return. Source rule holds nights/weekends, so intraday
plan occupancy and before-breach offsets are not established.

## 6. Power

106 historical position changes are not 106 independent net round trips at identical exposure. No 2018–22
design event count, return SD, or current product sequence is established. Do not construct a perpetual XIV
or switch to a surviving product after inspecting outcomes just to achieve a minimum trade count.
Historical Sharpe2.11 is not independently measured design power or multiple-testing-adjusted evidence.

## 7. Previous tests and falsifiers

North Star/VIXY sleeve (C) and Calvados naked-VX rejection are related carry exposures, not exact replications.
The new source is distinct by basis normalization, hysteresis and ETP payoffs; it does not erase those trials.
Data/implementation falsifiers: same-close unavailable input, unverified roll, substituted product reset,
missing synthetic vintage or omitted acceleration/redemption. Claims must survive causal fills and later eras.

## 8. Failure/diversification and decision

Short-vol losses cluster with equity crises; a linear equity hedge does not remove nonlinear volatility jumps.
Long-vol carry can bleed while waiting for a crisis. Product termination, gaps, issuer exposure and trading
halts are separate from measured daily SD. No useful capital or account-breach protection is established.
