## 1. Who trades, and what forces or causes the trade?

Hypothesis: information or positioning reflected around the London cash open predicts later NQ drift, especially
when the US opening hour agrees. The rule observes NQ prices only, not European stock orders, mandates or flows.
No identified participant is demonstrably forced to trade NQ in the predicted direction later that morning.
This is a cross-session momentum hypothesis, not a verified forced-flow mechanism. The exact rule has no published
effect size in the supplied material. Price persistence is the prediction to test, not evidence of its cause.
