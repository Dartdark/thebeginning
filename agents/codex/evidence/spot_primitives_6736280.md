# SSRN6736280: DON'T TEST — taxonomy without a new strategy hypothesis

Summary: Read all 18 pages of Aktaş's May 2026 *The Two Primitives of Spot Market Strategy*.
It explicitly supplies no new empirical evidence, and no full entry/exit/risk/regime algorithm.
Its categorical exhaustion, autocorrelation, cashflow and arbitrage claims need qualifications.
Retain qualified definitions; do not invent another indicator test from this upload.

Source: [uploaded document and manifest](../inputs/spot_primitives_6736280/manifest.json).
Review: [five questions and audit](../results/spot_primitives_6736280_review.md).
Return trials: **0**. Constructed mathematical examples are not market-return trials.

## 1. Participant and mechanism

Literature discusses overreaction, underreaction and institutional flows, but this paper identifies no
new participant obligation, forecastable magnitude/deadline or source-specific mispricing to test.
Classifying a trade as momentum/reversion does not establish an economic edge.

## 2. Direction, size and information clock

No chosen reference estimator, lookback, threshold, regime state or receipt/decision/fill clock is supplied.
“Price is high/low” is not an executable direction/size specification. No causal regime algorithm.

## 3. Contract transmission

Conceptual spot taxonomy; no new NQ/MNQ demand or intraday evidence. Cited time-series momentum research
includes futures/forwards but long monthly horizons and multiple instruments, not this project's single-market setup.

## 4. Data feasibility and incremental information

Owned futures OHLC can implement many previously tested price rules, but cannot supply the missing hypothesis.
More data do not repair a logical exhaustion claim. No free-data acquisition or data-shopping entry is justified.
Author's unpublished Turkish-equity thesis and forthcoming regime work are references, not outcomes supplied here.

## 5. Costs and book gap

No source turnover, fees, spread, stops or executable gaps to screen. Strategy labels do not establish
unoccupied book hours, favorable liquidity role or incremental return per unit of account risk.

## 6. Power

No specified event universe or outcomes, so no meaningful sample-size/power calculation. Toy probability and
cashflow examples test implications only; they establish no empirical frequency, Sharpe or profit factor.

## 7. Earlier tests and falsifiers

Existing EMA/RSI/relative-value tests and [the requested Sentinel MNQ failure](../results/sentinel_mnq_20261108.md)
remain. The current paper supplies no new definition/evidence to revive them. Correlation does not prove a
stationary tradeable spread; ex-post regime descriptions do not make a switcher causal.

## 8. Failure, liquidity and diversification

Trend/reversion labels do not determine payoff skew, stop-gap risk, liquidity provision or tail protection.
Losses, shared book exposures and timely offsets require concrete rules and chronological execution/account
evidence. Pure simultaneous arbitrage differs from inventory held for future convergence. Retain the distinction
in the existing execution library, without claiming a new executable opportunity or a new policy.

## Decision

**DON'T TEST.** Completed conceptual review, not a data-blocked strategy. No preregistration/market returns,
promising-strategy queue entry, new data request, account stage or live change. A genuinely new mechanism and
complete rule would require its own evidence/preregistration; this paper is not authorization to invent one.
