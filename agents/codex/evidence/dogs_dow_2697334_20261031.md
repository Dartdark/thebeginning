# Dogs of the Dow conceptual review: DON'T TEST from this source alone

Summary: Annual high-yield Dow-stock selection is a recognizable value hypothesis.
This narrative review presents mixed cited evidence but no new empirical net edge.
No forced futures flow, current portfolio benefit or new source-supported timing rule is identified.
Reject advancement; retain accounting lessons only. Zero new return trials.

Source: Ghouse/Ahmad, SSRN2697334, full uploaded conceptual paper.

## 1. Who trades and why?
Investors choose high-yield stocks voluntarily. No observed forced order, deadline-driven concession or
identified counterparty supplies an incremental futures edge. Value/recovery is proposed, not identified causally.

## 2. Direction, size and timing
Long ten highest-yield contemporaneous Dow constituents; annual review. Exact yield convention, rank timestamp,
weighting, cash/reinvestment and execution rules are not fixed by a new tested implementation in this source.

## 3. Direct contract
Individual equities. No direct NQ/ES futures transmission or permitted intraday adaptation is established.

## 4. New information and feasibility
No new data or return evidence. A later justified replication would require as-of membership, available dividend
declarations, actual prices and complete corporate-action/delisting/cash accounting. No speculative acquisition
project is warranted before a stronger source passes this evidence gate.

## 5. Costs and exposure gaps
Source gives no quantified gross edge to compare with costs. Annual equity holdings share broad equity downside;
neither intraday occupancy improvement nor personal diversification is measured. Apex homes are ineligible as written.

## 6. Power
No original sample or effect/variance estimate to support a power calculation. Annual rebalance snapshots and ten
correlated stock holdings are not independent strategy-validation observations. Do not invent a numerical hurdle.

## 7. Prior work and falsifiers
No exact entry found in checked tier/watch/queue searches. The source itself reports positive and negative cited
studies and after-cost failures. A future separately justified test must establish incremental net performance
versus risk-matched equity/value baselines; lack of such performance refutes the alpha claim.

## 8. Failure and diversification
Yield traps, cuts, sector concentration, slow recovery and equity crashes can create clustered losses.
Portfolio turnover and corporate actions affect executable net return. No stop, cash-risk allocation or offset
arriving before breach is evidenced. Ten names do not guarantee broad diversification.

## Decision
DON'T TEST from this conceptual paper alone. Not a new promising-queue item; not a universal rejection of
dividend investing. Retain the review and E09 accounting lesson; revisit only on stronger independently justified evidence.
