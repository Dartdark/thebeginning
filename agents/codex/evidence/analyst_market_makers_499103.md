# Analyst/dealer pre-upgrade activity: DON'T TEST as a public directional strategy

Summary: Source links affiliated dealer quote activity with future upgrades in1999–2000; aggregate effect weakens in2001.
Event-conditioned results and imputed inventory profits do not identify a tradable public detector or actual dealer P&L.
[Full review/five answers](../results/analyst_market_makers_499103_review.md); zero economic trials.

1. **Who/why:** dealers/clients affiliated with analysts may have information about recommendation changes. Source associations
   do not prove access, principal inventory, wrongdoing or a modern causal effect. No observable forced NQ/ES flow established.
2. **Direction/size/timing:** event weeks are selected using later upgrade/downgrade labels. Need a public predecision rule across
   all eligible days, actual release/receipt clocks and attainable fills; days−1 to+4 cannot be treated as purely post-release returns.
   Quote-count share×volume is not actual signed dealer size. Same-sample significant-firm selection adds a hindsight stage.
3. **Transmission:** single-stock dealer/analyst relationships, not index futures participant identity or portfolio-level net demand.
   Stable1999–2002 stock selection and period-specific routing/regulation constrain transferability.
4. **Inputs:** historical participant top quotes/updates, complete trade records, MPID/firm/subsidiary vintages, First Call original
   release times and all-event/non-event universes required. Owned NQ OHLCV does not provide them; no matching filenames found.
   No public detector is specified, so no paid acquisition or price proxy is initiated merely to manufacture a backtest.
5. **Costs/gaps:** source profits impute quote shares and future returns without a native cash/queue/inventory ledger. No attainable
   net spread, borrow/carry/impact, margin or chronological account gap/offset is established.
6. **Power:**3271999–2000 upgrade cases are event-conditioned, overlapping/dependent stock/firm observations, not independent
   forward trades. Numerous firm/stock/lag tests and same-sample dummies require selection adjustment. No current2018–22 power gate.
7. **Prior/falsifiers:** exact-ID/title/author-topic check found no prior matching review. Related order-flow and retail-proxy
   failures remain closed. The2001 aggregate weakening is retained; a future detector must include false positives/non-events and
   an independent past-only evaluation. It must fail closed if event labels or unavailable quote fields are required at entry.
8. **Failure/diversification:** quotes can be customer agency, revised/cancelled, hidden or routed anonymously. Midpoint-signed trades
   assigned to bid/ask participants are imputed, not identified fills. No specified entry/exit/stop, liquidity role, leverage bound,
   common-loss or pre-breach offset evidence supports any current account placement.

**Decision:** research/execution reference only. No promising-strategy/data-watch queue entry, return trial, account comparison,
MultiCharts implementation or portfolio allocation. A separate public-release/participant study needs one as-of quote/event mapping
and an independently justified all-observation protocol before any economic test.
