# Evidence gate — MAE entry timing, SSRN 7063478

Summary: DON'T TEST a new market strategy from this proprietary, same-sample execution study.
MAE can diagnose idealized limit eligibility; it does not establish actual fills, path risk or net new alpha.
Seven invented mechanical checks are separate from market returns. New return trials: zero.

1. **Actor / reason to trade:** proposed pullback conviction/adverse-selection filtering. No measured forced
   participant, order flow or new risk premium identified; price improvement on filled old-exit tickets is mechanical.
2. **Known direction/size/time:** known parent signals could generate orders causally, but the proprietary Nifty
   rules, raw tickets, exact cancellation clock and child risk state are unavailable. Typical hold differs from actual exit.
3. **Owned-market transmission:** own ES/NQ prices/ledgers can support a different justified replay. Source Nifty,
   hourly rules and 6.02 bp costs do not transfer automatically to native ES/NQ integer positions or fees.
4. **Separation from prior work:** E05 price-limit substitution and E06 fast timing already studied. A 0.25% offset
   is not identical to those policies, but no independent new evidence justifies a new cross-book threshold search.
5. **Gross/cost gate:** Table 1 reports after a fixed cost assumption but supplies no raw fill/cost ledger. MAE touch
   does not establish trade-through, queue/partial fills or complete held paths. Skipped trades need not be winners.
6. **Power/selection:** 3,908 original and 2,448 selected trades are author counts. Dependence, unknown parent trials,
   eight nonzero offsets and side-specific/interpolated views prevent a local power or multiplicity PASS. Reordering
   selected returns does not create out-of-sample evidence. No historical source replication attempted.
7. **Earlier tests:** fixed limit entries generally weaker; marginal EOS +$0.70 per signal retained honestly.
   Recent five-minute-return study had no passing profile. Neither is falsely presented as the exact present policy.

**Liquidity role:** resting limits may save entry spread/slippage but face adverse selection and uncertain fill.
The MAE rule alone does not identify available liquidity, queue priority or a rebound confirmation.
**Common losses / executable risk:** a fill during a persistent decline can overlap existing book losses. Changed
brackets, late fills, overlapping intents and gap handling can change exposure. Whole-trade MAE is not child MAE.
**Timely offset:** source Sharpe/drawdown based on selected returns does not show protection before an intraday
account breach. No account/payout/portfolio result is inferred from it.

**Disposition: DON'T TEST.** Preserve supporting execution reference and original selection history; no new TODO
candidate, paid/free data chase, return reservation or dependent account stage earned. No paused program resumed.
