## 1. Participants and mechanism

Liquidity takers split orders; liquidity providers replenish. Paper evidence says persistent flow and decaying impact
can offset each other, not that a profitable fade is guaranteed. Hypothesis: a price displacement unexplained by the
usual current/lagged flow relation, followed by actual observed repair, identifies temporary overshoot. No forced order
quantity, dealer inventory or informed/uninformed identity is measured. Permanent information repricing falsifies it.
