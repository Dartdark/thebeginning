## Seven evidence questions

1. **Who trades and why?** Informed strategic traders with private fundamental information and random liquidity
   traders, in a stylized equilibrium. No observable mandated institution or calendar trade is identified.
2. **Direction/size/time before entry?** Insider direction depends on private fundamental minus price; speed is
   an ex-ante technology choice. No external forecast, dollar-flow estimate, real clock or NQ volume comparison.
3. **Direct contract transmission?** One abstract risky asset; no calibrated NQ/ES/micro mechanism or observed hedge.
4. **Independent data?** Need identified participant speeds/re-entry, information arrival/quality and quotes/orders
   for an execution study. The proposed equity empirical design additionally needs holdings, prospectus labels,
   prices and firm fundamentals with correct vintages. Our inspected minute summaries do not identify these states.
5. **Costs and gaps?** Speed cost is a theoretical parameter, not our commission/spread/latency estimate. No expected
   intraday move, fixed parent order or gap to exploit. Occupancy-based event selection is not justified.
6. **Power?** No source trading opportunities, holding periods or per-trade variance exist to count. Do not infer
   independent trade count or t-stat from simulated precision. A power estimate would require inventing a rule.
7. **Prior work/falsifiers?** Exact SSRN/title absent in local ranked/review files at inspection; related strategic
   microstructure theory is already retained. Model assumption changes reverse qualitative conclusions. Any future
   empirical proposal needs observable type/clock definitions and falsifiers before new return testing.

## Failure, liquidity role and account implications

Private-value knowledge, risk neutrality, normal liquidity noise and overt type counts are material assumptions.
Adverse selection and repeated informed participation can defeat a naive “wait” policy. Our actual orders' passive
or aggressive role is unspecified by this source; no contingent hedge or offset before an Apex breach is established.
No stop, target, account size or daily-flat rule is supplied. No placement or sizing inference is earned.

## Decision

**DON'T TEST** as an alpha strategy. This is not a promising source-defined rule merely waiting for data.
Retain as execution theory; reopen only for an independently justified measurement question with a dated native
sample and then a separately scoped experiment. The formula audit is model arithmetic, zero economic definitions.
