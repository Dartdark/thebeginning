# Corrected RL strategy build: TEST as explicitly requested exploratory adaptations

User request: "build the strategy based off what u found", then "do both. and lmk eachs performance".
This authorizes a corrected daily-stock implementation and a separately identified intraday-futures adaptation.
It does not make the previous legacy engine valid, authorize live changes or establish a forced-flow mechanism.

Source and previous evidence: SSRN3690996, [review](../results/rl_ensemble_3690996_20261030.md),
five admission fixture groups and public source/data hashes. These new protocols are ours, not exact paper replication.

Mechanism: learned portfolio/position allocation from past prices, position/cash state and risk controls. Equity
participation and simple trend/risk overlays may explain any gains. The explicit request earns an exploratory test;
no independent forced participant or alpha premise is asserted. Fixed simple controls must test incremental value.

Stock data: acquired, hash-verified author-public 2009–2020 raw OHLC and split-factor panel. DD and RTX are excluded
before returns because their merger/spin-off lineage/cash accounting is unresolved. Remaining static 28-name universe
and factor-derived split accounting remain conditional data assumptions, not a certified point-in-time total-return panel.
Dividends are unavailable, so no total-return claim. Primary design dates 2018–2019; reserved historical validation 2020.
The available source cannot support the normal 2018–2022 / 2023+ validation; this limited source-data experiment is disclosed.

Futures data: owned NQ one-minute grid, MNQ $2/point, tick .25. Historical 2017 training/warm-up; design 2018–2022;
2023 onward only after the frozen design gate. Five-minute decisions use prior/current completed bars and next-minute
open execution proxies. Flat before the afternoon session ends, explicit entry-bar stops and missing-session census.

Information clock: close t observations produce share/contract targets; fills first occur after t, at the next session
open (stocks) or next minute open (futures). No backward fill, future scaler, future covariance or future quantile.
Fitted policies see only transitions ending before fit cutoff; preceding-quarter selection ends before the trade quarter.

Costs: stocks .1% commission plus .05% adverse price per side; integer shares and fee-inclusive cash. MNQ .62 commission
per side plus one adverse tick per fill; 1 MNQ primary, 2 MNQ 150K-size diagnostic if earned. No source share fee is reused
as a futures commission. The economic result may fail simply because turnover costs exceed gross edge.

Power: stock design has about 500 daily observations and futures about 1,250 sessions; training steps are not independent
observations. Incremental t>=2 requires mean incremental daily return about 2*SD/sqrt(n). Unknown true advantage and
multiple prior looks prevent an a priori power guarantee. Primary seed fixed, no after-result architecture/seed search.

Failure/diversification: long-stock equity beta and shared technical inputs; futures churn, trend reversals and gaps.
Stops/turbulence do not guarantee a fill or Apex survival. Current-book contribution, common losses and offsets before
a floor breach remain unmeasured until earned account tests. All four Apex homes reject the stock basket as written.
Futures adaptation must pass its own design gate; no transfer of the paper's Sharpe or stock results is allowed.

Decision: build code, verify mechanics, commit exact code/config before historical returns, then run the frozen design
screen. Stop dependent validation/placement on a failed gate, while delivering both complete research implementations.
