## 1. Who trades, and why?
Benchmark-tracking and rebalancing investors have a reason to transact near official closing prices.
Liquidity providers can absorb this, often before a late observer can act; no guaranteed residual is established.
The source evidence motivates checking observable auction demand, not calling a preceding price move forced flow.
Older auction schedules and populations require current venue/schema verification.

## 2. Direction, size and timing known before entry
Pre-close pressure needs a published signed imbalance available after realistic receipt lag and before order
submission. Post-close reversal needs the final auction print and pre-close midpoint, then a LATER executable quote.
The print cannot both define the signal and supply its own entry fill. Use date-aware ET/CT conversion and actual
early-close times. Flow dollars, hedge fraction, entry, stop and exit are not frozen because the input gate fails.
No final-day quantity, future auction denominator or retroactive update is eligible as a pre-close feature.

## 3. Does the flow reach the traded contract?
SPY–ES and QQQ–NQ are plausible direct hedging routes, already in our shared research.
Cash-stock cross-sectional pressure can cancel at index level. Observing ETF deviations is not proof that
NQ/ES futures have unabsorbed demand. Stock/ETF implementation is personal-only unless a justified futures rule
subsequently passes the full process. No substitution of futures tape delta for auction imbalance.

## 5. Costs and actual intraday gaps
Existing price-only MOC and post-close curvature families were already explored, and trade in the plan's late-day
neighborhood. Do not select events based on book occupancy. Exact current-roster overlap and event occupancy
remain unmeasured because no eligible candidate events exist. No diversification or protective effect is claimed.
Later costs must use native MNQ/MES commission, spread, order delay, slippage and gap handling; stock baskets
add borrow/financing, auction fees, missed fills and inventory. Academic deviations are not net returns.
A source-led3–5day stock holding period can be researched for personal capital, not inserted into an Apex plan.

## 6. Power before returns
Prior QQQ evidence counted1,249 normal-close design-calendar opportunities in2018–22; this is an existing
calendar census, NOT observed imbalance events. With assumed20%event frequency, n≈249; at assumed$300/NQ trade
SD, t2 needs about$38.02 net or$55.27 gross including$17.25 NQ round-trip base costs. With only124events the
corresponding planning figures are about$53.88/$71.13. These assumptions do not measure an effect or establish
micro costs; clustering/warm-up reduces effective n. Two Arca days or one Nasdaq day cannot support that screen.
The late-entry QQQ parent also includes latency, making the hurdle more demanding. No returns/targets inspected.

## 7. Already tested and falsifiers
TIER_LIST MOC follow/order-flow, WATCHLIST10/12/20, existing SPY/QQQ evidence and closing_auction_curvature.md
are prior work, not new trials to reset. Index reconstitution next-morning futures reversal also already failed.
Falsifiers for a future registered real-signal rule: no increment over price/calendar, no remaining move after
receipt and spread, unstable quantity definitions, no transmission to the traded contract, or effect concentrated
in unavailable fills. Failure is a stop; it does not license side flipping, clock shifting or a tight-spread rescue.
