## 8. Failure/diversification and decision

Marketable orders pay costs, do not earn the dealer's customer spread. EUR rallies, central-bank surprises,
liquidity loss and persistent trends can cause large adverse paths; fixed time is not a protective stop.

before the hoped-for offset; only later chronological integer-size account replay can establish any benefit.
No stop/target is part of this cheap definition. Future executable design requires a separately frozen stop,
entry-minute protection, gap handling, complete path/contract audit and native cost specification.
