## 7. Priors and falsifiers

## 8. Failure and diversification

Long stocks share equity downside; selection can concentrate sectors. Earnings gaps bypass a weekly loss check.
No intraday margin/offset or current personal-book benefit is verified. A later actual-size account comparison is earned
only after strategy validation, not by narrative diversification.
