# Listing-switch execution study: DON'T TEST

Summary: The paper supports execution measurement, not a prospective futures trading rule.
Announcement CAR is insignificant and effective-spread tables do not consistently support the narrative.

## 1. Participant and mechanism

The source proposes dealer preferencing and cost-sensitive flow migration between lit/dark stock trading.
These are inferred motives, not observed forced NQ orders. Execution price/immediacy tradeoffs do not identify
an investor's positive net return. Dealer revenue must cover adverse selection, inventory and operating costs.

## 2. Direction, size and clock

No trading direction, notional, entry/exit or size specified. Listing announcement and effective switch are
different dates. Five-minute future midpoint is an outcome label unavailable at order decision, not an input.
Daily volume-weighted spreads also mature after their constituent observations.

## 3. Traded-contract link

Nasdaq stock listing is not Nasdaq-100 inclusion or index rebalancing. No demonstrated futures-demand
transmission. CME NQ data do not replicate NYSE/Nasdaq securities or equity dark-pool eligibility/queues.

## 5. Net edge, intraday gaps and account fit

Spread coefficients and volume shifts are not gross strategy profits. There is no cost-adjusted trade or
account gap to evaluate; own native commission settings remain unchanged. No BASE/ADD/REPLACE/EMPTY stage
earned in either evaluation, funded plan or personal portfolio.

## 6. Power

Stock-day regression rows are dependent; 51 nonmissing-return events plotted in Figure 2 are not evidence of
a viable futures sample. Authors find no significant announcement CAR. No candidate trading mean/SD or 2018–22
power can be computed without inventing a rule, so stop before returns.

## 7. Deduplication and falsifiers

No exact ID/title full review found in checked tier/watch/queue/results/scout sources. Execution metrics
overlap E01/E10/E56 and the prior Nasdaq concentration review. Positive large-firm dark spread change and
negative pooled lit effective-spread change contradict broad improvement/deterioration claims respectively.
No coefficient-sum significance asserted without covariance; no new side/window/filter rescue.

## 8. Failure and diversification

No prospective order policy, queue priority or filled inventory is specified. Hence liquidity role, missed
fills, clustered losses, common account exposures and offset-before-breach are unmeasured. A smaller displayed
spread or higher price discovery does not supply an independent hedge or account protection.

**Decision: DON'T TEST as directional alpha.** Research diagnostic code is feasible with real quote/fill inputs;
profitability is not established. Save the supporting reference. No lease/reservation, trial-budget change,
portfolio allocation, external message or live installation.
