# WASDE futures/options activity: NEEDS DATA AND SPECIFICATION

Summary: Source reports a specific pre-release futures/options volume relationship with subsequent crop returns.
No net executable strategy is established; data and event/contract clocks block replication.
Source: Zhang, SSRN 4841490, full uploaded 28-page PDF; [review](../results/wasde_volume_4841490_review.md).
Return trials: 0. No strategy preregistration or historical return screen admitted.

| Evidence question | Source-supported answer / unresolved gate |
|---|---|
| 1. Who trades and why? | Producers adjust crop hedges; informed speculation is proposed. Unsigned volume does not establish trader identity, forced net demand or causal predictability. |
| 2. Direction, size and timing known beforehand? | Author's fitted signs are futures volume positive, options volume negative; ending-stock revisions negative at +5 days. No dollar flow estimate, frozen trading threshold or executable event clock. Forecast revisions are known only after publication. |
| 3. Does it reach NQ/ES? | No demonstrated direct transmission. The source concerns agricultural futures/options; same-crop futures are the natural research instruments. |
| 4. New data beyond price? | Contract-matched pre-release futures/options volume, option expiration/strike aggregation, dated front/deferred prices and as-published crop forecasts. Owned filename census found no matching panel. USDA/CME public checks returned proxy tunnel 403; product availability and cost unverified. See saved gate receipt. |
| 5. Costs and actual plan gaps? | No measured gross trading edge, native costs, fill convention or intraday hours. Source holds 5–20 trading days; no Apex occupancy slot can make that legal as written. Personal portfolio diversification is unmeasured. |
| 6. Power? | Monthly events and pooled dependent commodities. No verified 2018–22 eligible count or executable payoff SD; hence no credible numeric t>=2 hurdle yet. Author coefficient t-statistics cannot replace a trading power census. Stop before returns. |
| 7. Already tested? | Identifier/title search found no prior review. Existing TODO's ZC/ZS/WASDE data request overlaps the market, not a completed options-volume test. Do not relabel a prior generic price/volume idea as this replication. |
| 8. Failure/diversification? | Timing leakage, seasonal/contract confounding, unstable coefficients, event gaps/limits and correlated crop losses could invalidate it. Marketable/passive role and any offset before account breach are unspecified. No hedge benefit or acceptable sizing is established. |

**Decision: NEEDS DATA AND SPECIFICATION.** One dated report/contract input-and-clock reconciliation, then
no-return coverage/cost/power checks, is the smallest useful next step when genuinely new inputs arrive.
No forecast-consensus proxy, intraday adaptation or fitted full-sample threshold is authorized by this sheet.
