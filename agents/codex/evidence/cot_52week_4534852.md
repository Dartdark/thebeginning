# COT behavior at 52-week extremes: DON'T TEST

No causal executable rule, cost-adjusted return or forced quantity/deadline is provided.

## 1. Participants and cause

Commercial physical hedgers and non-commercial speculators/CTAs react differently to prices. Hedging,
attention and anchoring are proposed explanations; no actual compelled order or outsider's advantage is measured.
Futures-side commercial losses can accompany successful physical hedges; do not copy one leg as an alpha signal.

## 2. Direction, size and time

The general position response is contrarian for commercials and continuation for non-commercials. Response
slopes moderate at highs and strengthen at lows. This is not an ex-ante directional return rule. No dollar
order size is established. Weekly Tuesday snapshots normally become public Friday 15:30ET, subject to delays.
Record actual available_at/receipt, decision and first executable fill; do not backdate to Tuesday or a bar open.

## 3. Transmission

Direct commodity positions in 12 markets; no stock-index futures in the source. Weekly aggregate inventories
do not identify intraday marketable orders, physical hedges or a deadline. No NQ/ES or micro transmission is proved.

## 4. Independent inputs and feasibility

Owned `data/cot/deacot2017.zip` through 2026 and CL/GC prices could support a separately justified later inquiry.
They do not reproduce the source's 1992–2022 12-market sample, Datastream roll series, curve/control inputs,
first-release vintages or historical receipt times. Prior CL intake remains closed at its frozen identity gate;
this review does not reinterpret its code/name pair. Exact high/low construction and roll treatment need clarity.
No unavailable input was filled by a proxy or chosen after seeing returns.

## 5. Costs and account gaps

The source gives no entry, stop, exit or execution-cost bridge. A weekly association cannot establish an
intraday Apex edge or actual pre-breach diversification. No tradeable gross move exists to compare with native
CL/MCL/GC costs. Weekly personal-account holdings require roll/cash/margin and whole-contract accounting.

## 6. Power

Table 8 has 1,528 regression observations per market; those are not 1,528 independent strategy trades.
No frozen signal or design-only event frequency/holding-period SD exists to evaluate strategy power.
An isolated silver 5% interaction amid many comparisons is not a promotion gate or a reason to select that
market now. No artificial pooled event count or inferred t-statistic is used.

## 7. Previous tests and falsifiers

No exact SSRN/title prior hit. Existing COT Dealer Rank day trades/filter and COT reversal-index tests already
fail to improve NQ/ES; price-anchor monthly propagation is D. This paper supplies no new net-return result
that reopens those definitions. Oil/gold position-to-return Granger p-values are insignificant in both groups;
the paper explicitly finds little timing ability. Behavioral regression significance is insufficient for TEST.

## 8. Failure and diversification

Market efficiency, reaction rather than prediction, publication delay, stale/revised data and contract rolls can
destroy an inferred edge. A marketable outsider entry consumes liquidity; a commercial classification does not
establish passive execution. Trend reversals, inventory shocks and macro exposure could produce clustered
losses; joint-account paths and useful offsets are unmeasured. No protective order or size has been selected.

**DON'T TEST from this source alone.** Retain the COT timing/measurement lesson in E44. A new independent causal
hypothesis would need a separate evidence sheet, publication-aware baseline, committed rule/code and the normal
design-first gates. No new queue entry, return screen, account simulation, rank or live action is warranted.
