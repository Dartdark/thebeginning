## 1. Participant and cause

The source studies the seller-side tax on Indian non-agricultural commodity futures, effective July 1, 2013.
Higher transaction cost may deter marginal traders. It identifies no forced directional purchase/sale at a
repeatable trade clock and measures no trader's net profit from supplying that flow.

## 2. Direction, size and timing

No prospective trade sign, quantity, decision, first fill, holding period, protective exit or liquidation clock.
The event dummy is descriptive; coefficients fitted through 2016 cannot become 2013 trading information.

## 3. Contract transmission

MCX gold and CME GC/MGC are different venues, currencies and cost regimes. This is not evidence of an executable
GC/NQ/ES forced-flow signal. The historical Indian seller tax must not be imported into CME cost settings.

## 5. Costs, gaps and account fit

selection. Evaluation, funded and personal placement: no candidate; no account simulation earned.

## 6. Power

One historical policy change is not a recurring 2018–22 entry opportunity. The 1,616 descriptive observations
and repeated nested windows do not create independent trades. Effective trading count/mean/SD/power are
undefined until a genuine new hypothesis exists; no return inspection to manufacture them.

## 7. Deduplication and falsifiers

No exact ID/title review found in checked Codex/Claude tier/watch/queue or Codex results/records. Cost and
participation methods overlap E09/E10/E12. The published alpha5 p-value and persistence sum already fail the

## 8. Failure, liquidity role and diversification

A tax/turnover observation predicts no order role, clustering, offset timing or protection before an account
floor breach. These are unmeasured, not low-risk. No stop/size/hedge is invented. A risk/execution reference
does not establish uncorrelated account profit.

**Decision: DON'T TEST.** Save review/reference and continue only when a separately evidenced question earns its
own gates. No reservation/lease or trial consumed; no live, allocation or paused-program change.
