## 1. Who trades, and what causes the trade?

Cash-session participants rebalance and incorporate overnight news around 08:30 CT. We hypothesize the first
30-minute drive distinguishes continuing overnight demand from an overnight-pressure reversal that survives
cash participation. No dataset here identifies forced participants or signed institutional demand. This is an
explicitly speculative interaction of existing price ingredients, not a verified forced-flow mechanism.

## 2. Direction, size and timing known before entry

At the 09:00-labelled close, use the 17:01 reopen to 08:29 overnight direction, ONB's 23:01–08:30 range,
08:31-labelled cash-open price, first15-minute range, first30-minute drive and continuous15-minute ADX.
Enter 09:01-labelled OPEN; same-bar-known features do not filter earlier fills. Size starts at 1 native MNQ in
any earned later account comparison. Features, labels and order clocks are frozen in the plan.

## 3. Does the information reach the traded contract?

All inputs and trades are NQ itself. No transmission proxy is needed. Price confirmation is not evidence of
unabsorbed flow: both candidates may merely select existing morning momentum with fewer opportunities.

## 5. Costs and actual intraday gaps

NQ round trip $7.25 plus one tick per market/stop fill ($17.25 total); MNQ $1.25 plus $1 total slippage.
The morning window overlaps ONB, FC and VB; this is not an empty-hour strategy. Existing occupancy evidence is
in the ingredient reports. Do not choose events from plan-flat dates. Same-window parent controls concentration.

## 6. Power before returns

At most roughly 1,250 design sessions before roll-calendar/missing-data exclusions. Hypothesized selection
frequency 10–25% implies ~100–300 events; actual counts are saved before return replay. Assuming $2,000 NQ
trade SD, t2 needs ~$231–400 net per trade plus $17.25 gross costs. This SD/frequency is an assumption,
not a measured result or promotion hurdle. Min30 trades and t2 cheap gates do not establish robust selection.

## 7. Already tested and falsifiers

Original FC/ONB/T ranked; generic overnight-to-intraday reversal and OR variants have prior failures in TIER_LIST.
The declared new interaction is first30-minute confirmation of continuation versus overnight reversal, under one
common FC v2 exit framework. No new historical independence is claimed. Reject if it only raises mean by removing
profitable trades, fails paired total-profit/risk controls, or cannot cover costs and its fixed execution stress.

## 8. Failure, liquidity and diversification

Marketable entries and stop exits consume liquidity; wider spreads and news gaps can exceed modeled ticks.
Continuation whipsaws after an early breakout; reversal loses when overnight repricing persists. Both overlap
ONB/VB/FC, and a counter-signed strategy can still lose later on the same day. No offset before an Apex floor
breach is assumed. A 100-point stop means ~$202.25 modeled MNQ loss, with gap risk above it; an account's
available cushion, concurrency and intraday marks still need later chronological replay. One trade per day,
no simultaneous legs or cross-account hedge. Flat 14:59-labelled open, 11:31 on source early-close days.
