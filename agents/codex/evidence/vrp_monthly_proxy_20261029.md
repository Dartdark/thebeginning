# Monthly VRP proxy: TEST (bounded feasibility adaptation)

Summary: User authorized an actual adapted test after the source review.
Positive monthly equity forecasting evidence supplies a risk-compensation mechanism, not forced orders.
Use existing VIX/ES inputs with explicit source/roll/holding restrictions; no source-parity claim.
Only the committed two-candidate screen may run; data/economic failure stops further returns.

Sources: [original review](../results/variance_risk_premium_review_20261029.md), Zhou2018 SSRN1783624,
[author page](https://sites.google.com/site/haozhouspersonalhomepage/),
[official older Fed data](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econresdata/researchdata/feds200711_1.html).
New return trials before preregistration:0. Source finding: monthly equity VRP positively forecasts next-month
excess returns (Table1 t5.20), with strongest fit near three months; not independently reproduced here.

## 1. Who trades and why?
Variance-insurance buyers and risk-bearing investors price uncertainty. There is no identified mandatory
order or deadline/dollar-flow estimate. The test is a user-authorized conditional equity-risk-premium adaptation,
not a claimed scheduled ES imbalance. High expected return can accompany severe interim losses.

## 2. Direction/size/timing before entry
Positive/high IV-minus-trailing-RV predicts higher future equity return. Freeze one MES-equivalent, known
month-end state, next-month first cash open, next-month exit. Exact clocks and two thresholds are in the plan.
Dollar demand/volume comparison unavailable; no forced-trade explanation is invented.

## 3. Does it reach the traded market?
ES tracks SPX equity risk; using ES RV and ES opens is still a proxy for the source SPX process. Exclude target
quarterly roll months by calendar. Feature roll heuristic is causal at month-end but vendor identity unverified.

## 4. Inputs and incremental information
Existing VIX daily and ES minute2017–26 files; hash inputs and audit missing endpoints. Option IV provides
information beyond past returns: compare identical-universe IV-only, RV-only and constant-equity controls.
Author advertises monthly data throughDec2024, but its Drive download was denied by network policy; web preview
does not expose values. Older official Fed table endsDec2008 and cannot fill2018–22. No purchase or login.
data/SPY_daily.csv is excluded: its values are index/futures-scale, not verified SPY. No daily-volatility
substitution, inferred author values or silent index/ETF interchange. Native MES fills/fees not independently verified.

## 5. Costs and plan occupancy
Assume MES$1.24commission+$2.50slippage RT=$3.74; four-tick stress$11.24. Gross must exceed$7.48/month,
before stronger net/filter gates. Long monthly overnight exposure is personal-only; it cannot exploit an Apex
intraday gap and overlaps the personal equity book. No occupancy-based selection of market events.

## 6. Power
At most40 target months in2018–22 (eight/year), before feature warm-up/missing inputs. Half selected gives
about20 trades; t2 requires mean0.447*monthly SD. For assumed3% monthly ES return SD and illustrative3000 ES,
one-MES SD~$450 and required mean~$201/month plus costs. These are planning assumptions, not observed moments.
Small sample and crisis clustering can make the screen fail; daily marks are not independent monthly trades.
Require30 common-known target months/15 selected; no expanding the trial if power or economic gates fail.

## 7. Already tested and falsifiers
Prior VIX regime tilts, implied-vol exit scaling and failed implied-variance budget breach are different rules;
do not restart them. The monthly source relation is unverified. Reject this adaptation if it cannot beat constant
exposure at fixed costs and matched random drops, removes profitable months, or loses significance/payout fit.
Five economic definitions total: two candidates, three nonselectable controls; at most two candidate variants.

## 8. Failure/diversification
Risk-premium forecasts may fail after publication or depend on crises; proxy measurement may dominate signal.
Orders are marketable at scheduled native opens; no passive spread saving assumed. Monthly holding can lose
during equity crashes and overnight gaps; no stop or guaranteed maximum loss. Missing paths limit margin-risk
certification. Existing personal long equities share losses; no hedge or offset-before-breach established.
No allocation/size increase is justified by a passing gross screen; account and implementation gates remain.

## Decision
TEST this explicit proxy, not exact author replication. Follow [the frozen plan](../plans/vrp_monthly_proxy_20261029.md)
and stop at its first failed gate. A different later source-parity replication remains separately identifiable.
