# Post-announcement volume persistence: DON'T TEST as directional alpha

Summary: The source explains later elevated volume after U.S. news in European index futures.
It does not supply a return forecast, executable cost saving or trade rule. A numerical diagnostic is feasible.
Exact empirical replication needs licensed transactions and professional forecast vintages absent locally.
Zero economic return trials; keep in execution references, not the promising-alpha queue.

Source and page references: [full review](../results/volume_persistence_7446599.md), uploaded SSRN 7446599.

## Evidence questions

1. **Who trades and why?** Repositioning, execution splitting and inventory transfer are proposed explanations.
   No identified forced actor or directional obligation is estimated; explanations are not causally separated.
2. **Direction/size/time ex ante?** Release time is scheduled, but initial activity is known only after the initial
   observed minutes. Outcome volume over the next 120 minutes has no source-defined signed price prediction or
   forced-dollar-flow estimate. Full-sample timing and same-day volume-selected contracts cannot become live inputs.
3. **Direct contract link?** The study measures FTSE/DAX/CAC futures directly. No verified NQ/ES transmission,
   microcontract behavior, exposure gap or net trade expectancy is established.
4. **Independent data?** Initial volume scale and professional forecast dispersion are distinct from price momentum,
   but the latter requires pre-release vintages. European native transactions/contracts and quote/fill records are
   absent locally; data statement says raw inputs commercially licensed. Minute OHLCV cannot identify exact tick-rule
   flow or spreads/depth. No incomplete proxy panel was substituted.
5. **Costs/gaps?** Source explicitly separates activity from costs/liquidity resilience. No expected gross price move,
   usable intraday gap, parent-order execution rule or native cost bridge. No plan-occupancy-conditioned selection.
6. **Power?** Source counts are volume-event regressions, not strategy trades. Without a signed rule/holding period,
   per-trade SD and net hurdle cannot be estimated responsibly. Three simultaneous markets are not three independent
   macro events. A new codeable measurement does not justify inventing an alpha power calculation.
7. **Prior work and falsifiers?** No exact SSRN/title duplicate found in local rank/watchlist/review files at inspection.
   Related activity/volatility and order-flow lessons already retained. Future execution research must be falsified by
   incremental cost/markout evidence beyond baseline activity, not supported solely by significant volume coefficients.

## Failure and account/diversification implications

High activity can coexist with better depth or adverse selection; forecast ability may shift with venue/session changes.
There is no specified passive/aggressive order, stop/target or capital exposure, and no measured conditional loss
diversification against the current book. No offset before an Apex breach or safe sizing is established.

## Decision

**DON'T TEST** as source-defined alpha. Measurement-tool feasibility: yes, scoped arithmetic kernel built and
synthetically checked, with date/availability/contract validation left explicit upstream. Reconsider only for a separate
execution problem with audited event/quote/fill data; no strategy code or plan placement is earned.
