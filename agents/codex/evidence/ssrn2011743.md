# Fibonacci leveraged index trading: DON'T TEST from this source

Summary: The paper reports large leveraged returns but leaves essential rules discretionary.
Exact source replication needs a deterministic specification and a trade ledger.
No independent forced-flow input or demonstrated Fibonacci-specific advantage is established.
No new parameter choices or proxy-market tests are justified. Return trials: zero.

Source: Doguc and Erdogan, March 2006, SSRN 2011743; uploaded full PDF archived at
`agents/codex/inputs/ssrn2011743/source.pdf`. Full review: `../results/ssrn2011743.md`.

## 1. Who trades, and what forces the trade?
The paper invokes trends, psychology and panic, not an identified mandate, hedge
or scheduled flow. Direction is inferred from price patterns; forced order size is unknown.

## 2. Direction, size and timing known before entry
Daily close breakouts/retracements determine proposed positions. Pivot confirmation,
ellipse placement and regime labels lack reproducible as-of rules. A confirmed
closing signal does not establish a fill at that same close. Information clock unresolved.

## 3. Transmission to our contracts
DJI30 and ISE100 spot-index examples do not establish net demand in NQ/ES. Multi-day
medium-term holdings also do not meet Apex's daily flatten requirement.

## 4. Data and independent information
Price-only Fibonacci/Dow patterns; no independent flow measure. Required source
index histories, executable instruments/rolls, marked ledger and any protective
options specifications are not supplied. Our continuous futures bars do not resolve
missing algorithms or reproduce historical index trading costs.

## 5. Costs and plan gaps
Paper assumes 10x margin leverage and 0.2% costs per contract, with ample closing
liquidity. Cost interpretation, financing/rolls and option premiums need clarification.
No intraday window or incremental net edge is established; occupancy testing is unearned.

## 6. Power
No trade ledger or usable trade count/return dispersion. Effective sample size and
required mean for t>=2 cannot be estimated responsibly; calendar years are not trades.

## 7. Prior work and falsifiers
No exact ID/author/Fibonacci match found in the searched tier/watch/review files.
Generic trend/reversal claims need risk-matched momentum and non-Fibonacci controls;
this source supplies neither. New algorithms would constitute a different strategy.

## 8. Failure and diversification
Source acknowledges sideways-market losses. Pyramiding raises exposure and one
countertrend branch has no stop. Annual balance growth cannot establish pre-breach
diversification or an executable maximum loss. Margin reserves are not a loss guarantee.

## Decision
DON'T TEST now. Exact replication is specification-blocked; no compelling independent
evidence warrants inventing rules or adding a promising-strategy queue item. Reconsider
only if deterministic as-of rules and a reconcilable ledger become available.
