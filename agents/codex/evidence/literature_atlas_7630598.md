# Trading Literature Atlas: DON'T TEST as a standalone strategy

Summary: Retain a source-navigation and research-design reference. No specified economic trading rule or independently tested edge. Source's bridges are proposals, not findings. No data purchase, strategy trial or portfolio change.
Source: [focused review](../results/literature_atlas_7630598_review.md), SSRN7630598, October18,2026. New return trials: zero.

## Participant and cause

No single forced participant, quantity or deadline is established for a NQ/ES trade. The survey discusses possible mechanisms in other papers; no cited mechanism is independently verified here.

## Direction, size and clock

No complete entry/side/quantity/stop/exit protocol. Timestamped features, label maturity, native lots, costs and execution rules would need a separately justified primary-source hypothesis. Do not invent them to turn the atlas into a strategy.

## Transmission and independent inputs

Cross-disciplinary tools do not establish CME demand. The original cited study, historical availability, contract identity, quotes/depth and native execution would determine data feasibility for any future specific proposal. Current minute bars do not supply event-level book paths or option surfaces.

## Costs and power

No eligible event census or measured gross edge. Library counts are not trades or independent market observations. Forecast/classification gains cannot replace executable net returns; no account model is earned.

## Previous evidence and implementation

Existing research controls already require causal features, fold-local fitting, trial counts, baseline/removed-trade controls, costs and account comparisons. Existing rejected generic flow/pattern/regime definitions stay closed. Experimental tools are technically feasible; profitability is unproved and a standalone rule is absent.

## Failure, liquidity and diversification

No chosen order type or measured liquidity role. Timestamp errors, simulator exploitation, changing regimes, shared gates, unknown fills and short-horizon costs can invalidate proposals. No measured common-loss reduction or timely offset before account breach exists.

## Decision

Keep the execution/reference lessons; do not nominate a strategy or acquire a speculative dataset on this source alone. At an already-authorized verification, compare data/signal/quantity/fill records against the existing controls. No new economic plan or stage is opened.
