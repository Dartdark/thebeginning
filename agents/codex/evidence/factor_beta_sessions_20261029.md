# U.S. factor-beta session baskets: NEEDS DATA
Summary: The source reports lagged-beta stock-return differences by session, not an executable futures edge.
U.S. gross conditional slopes justify a no-return feasibility audit; costs, borrow and fills remain unverified.
No new return trials or trading allocation. [Source review](../results/factor_beta_sessions_review_20261029.md).

Sources: Ye, Jiang and Luo, SSRN 4138715, uploaded March 15, 2023 version; provenance in review.
Return trials: **0**. This is not a TEST verdict or a strategy preregistration.

## 1. Who trades and why?
Investor clientele may create and partly correct factor-related mispricing at the open/close.
Supporting proxies are observational. No forced purchase or guaranteed arbitrage is established.

## 2. Direction, size and timing known before entry
Candidate for later audit: U.S. stocks ranked on prior-month estimated market beta; measure high-minus-low
baskets separately overnight and intraday. Source slopes favor high beta overnight and low beta intraday.
No basket size, threshold, financing or execution choice is frozen here; choosing one later is a new design.
Features must be available before orders; prior-month return fits differ from full-sample post-ranking chart betas.
Actual auction deadlines/receipts/fills and capacity require verification.

## 3. Transmission to traded instrument
This is a stock-basket relationship. No demonstrated net ES/NQ demand or valid two-futures substitute.
Potential venue is later personal-account stock research, subject to feasible universe and account permissions;
it is not a candidate for immediate addition to the current futures stack.

## 4. Data feasibility
| Input | Source lead | Unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Historical U.S. universe, returns/open-close, delistings | CRSP as in source | Owned access/cost, as-of universe, adjustments |
| Daily factors and characteristics | Source's FF5 definitions; Chinese variants require CSMAR/Stambaugh inputs | Publication lags/revisions, factor construction |
| Executions and short implementation | Current broker/venue records, borrow availability and prices | Costs, auctions, short recalls, actual fills and integer sizes |

No external series acquired or availability/licence claim verified. Same-window market/calendar and simple
market-beta-only controls must precede complex factor combinations.

## 5. Costs and occupancy
Gross regression bp per unit beta is not achievable basket P&L. Need attained exposures, turnover, spread,
commissions, borrow, financing and market impact; missed auction fills remain visible.
No event-matched current occupancy map or incremental account-risk claim was computed.
Do not select test dates by whether existing plans are flat.

## 6. Power
Effective sample depends on sessions, common-factor dependence and overlapping beta estimates, not the number
of stock-day observations. Count eligible 2018–22 days after universe/borrow/warm-up checks first.
Net mean hurdle for t≈2 is approximately 2 times session-P&L SD / sqrt(effective n); neither feasible SD nor
net mean is established. Stop at NEEDS DATA until the cost/power gate is possible.

## 7. Existing work and falsifiers
No exact source match in inspected ranks/queue. General overnight futures, cross-asset momentum and NQ/ES
Relative Strength are related context, not stock-basket replications.
Falsifiers: past-only exposures fail to preserve the source sign, simple controls explain the spread,
or attainable gross returns are consumed by implementation. Do not rescue a failure with hindsight factor mixing.

## 8. Failure, risk and diversification
| Question | Ex-ante concern / evidence needed |
|---|---|
| What stops it? | Clientele convergence, crowding, different auction mechanics; verify current data |
| Liquidity role? | Auction/marketable basket orders versus passive orders must be specified; adverse selection and missed fills |
| Loss clustering? | Factor crashes, borrow recalls, gaps and correlated basket liquidation |
| Shared losses? | Equity beta/growth exposure may overlap VB/ONB/RS and personal futures; unmeasured |
| Timely offset? | No protection established; need chronological aggregate cash/exposure replay, not annual correlation |

No stop/time exit or size selected while data-blocked. Stops do not bound gap losses.
A later TEST sheet must specify executable risk, order/exit clocks, data failure handling and limits before returns.
Compare addition/replacement/empty slots only after a passing cheap design screen and independent review.

## Decision
**NEEDS DATA** — no-return data/cost audit first. All new return tests require a committed plan with at most
five variants; already-seen recent history is not fresh holdout. A queue entry is not authorization to trade.
