# Dastarac, SSRN 3947960: DON'T TEST as a portfolio strategy

Summary: a symmetric-information equilibrium model explains futures demand, lower welfare and basis under imperfect competition.
It does not supply an empirical return rule or a calibrated outsider execution/arbitrage strategy.
Collective strategic pressures cancel; private inventory utility is not our trading alpha.
Observed raw basis lacks executable matched-leg, carry and information-clock evidence.
Keep the execution lesson; no return stage is earned for any current portfolio.

Source: Hugues Dastarac, Banque de France WP 841, October 2021, supplied 82-page PDF.
[Review](../results/strategic_futures_3947960_20261030.md).
Return trials, proxy variants, gross screens and account simulations: **0**.

1. **Participant and force:** strategic inventory holders choose when to trade to manage price impact and utility.
   This is endogenous discretion, with opposing effects in equilibrium, not a source-defined observable forced flow.
2. **Direction, size, clock:** the model defines dates 0/1/2 rather than an actual calendar window. We do not observe
   its trader inventories, risk aversion, demand slopes or date-0 expectation of customer flow. Realized later flow
   cannot substitute for that expectation. No known dollar amount, market-share estimate or outsider order side.
3. **Traded contract:** the modeled derivative settles on date-1 spot price before the asset's terminal payoff.
   NQ/ES/CL/GC/ZN continuous bars alone do not identify matched cash/futures legs or model participants.
4. **Owned data:** repository inventory contains futures OHLCV, COT, Treasury auction dates and order-flow material,
   not sufficient matched cash securities/baskets, bid/ask quotes, funding/borrow, CTD/conversion factors or
   participant-linked predecision inventory/customer-flow expectations. No identifiable empirical dataset is offered
   by the paper. No acquisition or substitute-proxy return search was warranted.
5. **Cost and actual gap:** the theoretical basis and welfare change have no demonstrated native-dollar edge after
   both-leg spread, fees, funding, carry, partial fills and settlement cash. Margin is explicitly omitted in the model.
   No actual independent intraday slot or protected execution protocol is specified.
6. **Power:** no fixed executable signal/event universe or empirical mean/variance is provided. Sample count,
   statistical power and gross-versus-cost hurdle are unavailable; no invented returns or t-statistics.
7. **Duplicate/falsifiers:** exact identifier/title/author search found no duplicate. Distinct from Muermann–Shore's
   hidden physical monopolist model. Opposing pressure, different risk preferences, endogenous response to an outside
   arbitrageur or instrument/carry mismatch could invalidate a naive trading translation.
8. **Failure/diversification:** undefined without an executable rule. Price-impact savings can be offset by later price
   exposure; leg and financing mismatches can create losses before any hedge settles. No demonstrated stop, time
   exit, concurrency cap, common-loss pattern or offset-before-Apex-breach. No current-book benefit is inferred.

Decision: **DON'T TEST**. If a separate concrete execution/basis proposal earns investigation later, the smallest next
step is a no-return audit of matched instruments, pretrade availability and both-leg executable cost/carry accounting.
It would need a new evidence sheet and committed plan before economic outcomes.
