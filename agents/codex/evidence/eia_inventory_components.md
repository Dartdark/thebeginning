## 1. Participant and cause

Oil producers, refiners, inventory holders and financial participants respond to public supply/demand
information. Inventory component changes could distinguish genuine total-stock tightening from crude
moving through refineries into product stocks. No compulsory order, mandate, actual demand shock or delayed
arbitrage is observed. Reported changes can be anticipated by API/consensus, seasonal or already priced.

## 2. Direction, scale and clock

Potential later prediction: falling combined crude/product stocks imply tighter reported balances and rising
stocks the reverse. This is not an actual-vs-consensus surprise or a guaranteed price direction. No economic
rule/threshold/entry clock is frozen yet. Standard Highlights publication policy is10:30ET; historical delays,
multiple releases and actual receipt remain separate checks. No dollar flow/hedge fraction is established.

## 3. Transmission

WTI CL is directly exposed to crude balances, while gasoline/distillate stocks affect refinery demand and
product margins. Regional inventories, SPR transfers, imports, refinery outages and demand seasonality can
confound a simple aggregate. A one-contract standalone CL trade is a possible later test; no basket or existing
strategy ticket would determine its entry. Native MCL costs/parity and listing history need separate treatment.

clocks and an insufficient source panel. Performance falsifiers belong in a later committed economic plan.
