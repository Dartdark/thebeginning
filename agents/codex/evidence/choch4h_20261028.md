# Four-hour CHoCH — TEST as a bounded user-requested baseline

User requests a simple 4h change of character, close entry, previous swing stop and 1:1 target; explicitly requests both one- and two-candle pivot confirmation. This is a price-pattern hypothesis, not an established forced-flow effect. The prediction is that an established sequence of higher highs/lows (lower highs/lows) breaking its latest confirmed opposing swing starts a reversal that reaches one risk unit before losing one risk unit. Price alone does not identify forced participants or flow size.

NQ minute day/overnight exports and shared Grid24/execution/metrics are available. Use only completed bars and confirmed pivots, no centered-series values before confirmation. No new price data or returns examined before registration. Similar 6h fractal breakout research exists in Twin Continuation; this test is a reversal of established structure with a fixed swing stop and 1:1 target, not a claim of discovering all swing trading anew.

Main falsifiers: after-cost expectancy no greater than zero, weak design t, dependence on one side/year, same-close fill optimism, missing-minute/roll ambiguity, or no improvement over base and empty-slot controls. One MNQ cost scenario on NQ prices is a proxy, including pre-MNQ-launch 2018; no claim of actual historical micro fills. Weekend/multiday bracket holds make the literal strategy unsuitable for Apex as written. Current book overlap and tail risk remain unmeasured until a candidate clears the cheap design gate. Do not add filters or retune after a failure. Closed Hunt/t6 programs remain untouched.
