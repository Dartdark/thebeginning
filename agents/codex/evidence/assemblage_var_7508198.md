# Assemblage VAR: DON'T TEST as a standalone trading strategy

Summary: Supervised aggregation of climate constituents into a macro VAR is a useful research method.
Structural responses are not trading returns; no futures rule or timed participant obligation is supplied.
Free transformed panels/code acquired and inspected; source/parity cautions saved. No return trial or allocation.
Source: [review](../results/assemblage_var_7508198_review.md), SSRN7508198, draft September22,2026.

## Participant, cause and transmission

Storms/heat/floods may reduce real activity and housing, but no identified trader must buy/sell NQ/ES at a specified time. Climate-to-activity effects do not establish incremental information beyond market expectations. No tradable flow sign/size measured.

## Direction, size and timing

Monthly structural innovations, recursive ordering and indicator weights are not an order rule. Original publication/receipt vintages and causal estimation needed for any proposed future trading use; full-sample fitted indices cannot become historical signals. No stop, target, deadline, order type or integer size selected.

## Inputs and feasibility

Pinned public code and transformed ACI/NOAA panels acquired free. Counterexample verifies that sorting zero-mean constituents does not preserve every rank mean; actual code imposes a constraint. CV, penalties and gap treatment require parity care. Retrospective analysis inputs suffice for methods inspection, not real-time signal certification.

## Cost, power and previous work

No eligible trading-event census, price-return study or executable gross edge. Library/data row counts are not trade counts. Existing generic indicator/flow searches remain closed; methodology does not supply an independent economic hypothesis to reopen them.

## Failure, liquidity and portfolio risk

Revision/receipt errors, full-history preprocessing/weights, nonunique correlated components, optimizer/tuning uncertainty and translating different index shocks into one trading direction can defeat an adaptation. No execution liquidity role, loss clustering, diversification or offset before account breach measured.

## Decision

Retain a research-tool reference and source audit, not a promising-strategy/data-blocked entry. An independently motivated future hypothesis must pass its own evidence, data-clock, preregistration and chronological net-return gates. No trading implementation or economic test earned here.
