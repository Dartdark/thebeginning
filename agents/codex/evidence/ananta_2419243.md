# ANANTA: NEEDS DATA AND SOURCE-SPECIFICATION CLARIFICATION

Source: Nicolas Georges, April 2014 first draft, SSRN2419243; uploaded full 20-page PDF.
Review: `../results/ananta_2419243_review.md`. Zero economic trials.

1. **Who trades and why?** Proposed FX repricing as relative expected monetary policy
   changes. The paper does not identify mandated orders, measured flow or an arbitrage
   ensuring profit. This is a cross-market forecasting hypothesis, not established flow.
2. **Ex-ante direction, size and time?** The verbal rule buys rising relative-rate
   currencies; p12 pseudocode appears reversed. Uses two-year swap differentials versus
   2/15-day averages, median magnitude selection and net country scores, rebalanced
   18:00 Europe/London and 17:00 America/New_York. Publication/fill lag, precise rolling
   window, percentile scope, zero denominator and gross-normalization rules need resolution.
   No demonstrated tradable flow size or volume comparison is available.
3. **Does it reach our markets?** Original is ten OTC xxxUSD legs across eleven currencies,
   not NQ/ES. Some FX futures could implement a separately specified subset; dropping
   countries, changing basis or forcing intraday flatness would be a new strategy.
4. **Separating information?** Synchronized country swap-rate changes could be distinct
   from FX price momentum. Need original-vintage two-year swap quotes and executable FX
   bid/ask at both clocks for eleven currencies, with benchmark-transition metadata.
   Owned continuous 6E bars and daily macro data do not provide these fields.
5. **Costs and a real gap?** Author claims net 3.5/5 bp per dollar traded. These are not
   independently verified, current broker costs, or an NQ per-trade edge. Spot/futures
   basis, financing, two daily rebalances, USD residual and whole-contract rounding matter.
   No current-book cost or occupancy comparison is earned before source/data admission.
6. **Power?** Eleven years of daily marks are reported, but no raw returns or independent
   holdout. Fifty-five pair sub-signals are dependent, not fifty-five independent trades.
   Cannot compute net-effect power or t from plotted cumulative curves. First require
   actual timestamped coverage and turnover; do not invent performance inputs.
7. **Already tested?** No SSRN2419243/ANANTA match in owned tier/watchlist/review files.
   Related generic FX and cross-asset momentum failures do not replicate this signal.
   No current tier or allocation is earned by the author's historical claims.
