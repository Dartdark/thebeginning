## 1. Actor and reason

The paper proposes short-horizon reversal in liquidity-consuming trend entries/stop exits, not a new forced
directional order. A cheap reversal can improve a slower signal's execution without supporting a standalone
high-turnover strategy. This is an empirical execution question, not proof of liquidity provision or causal alpha.
Its transfer to futures, scheduled flows and existing mean-reversion/limit strategies is expressly unproven.

## 2. Direction, size and information clocks

Use each original parent stream's recorded side, entry and exit requests as a fixed **virtual parent**. Its
state/levels stay fixed; actual child execution may differ. This is a separately specified shadow execution
wrapper, not a rerun that pretends altered fills leave an independently traded parent's state unchanged.
Counter signal = one complete five-minute **close-to-close** return against entry direction; bar color is not
the paper's definition. It must end strictly after the source event. Fill at the next observed minute OPEN,
with one adverse native tick. No guaranteed improvement, fill at a just-observed close or hindsight-best pullback.
The stop/signal request is an event at its recorded clock, never an earlier feature. Deferred liquidation waits
for the first favorable five-minute return; time/holiday flatten deadlines override it. Broker/account floors
are never delayed. A stop deferral changes realized downside even if its trigger level is unchanged.

## 6. Power and gates

Design only2018-04-02–2022-12-30;2017 warms generated parents. At least30 common complete tickets; zero unresolved
source/candidate tickets for a passing parent. With effective n, paired net effect needs t at least the conservative
repository+project multiple-testing threshold, and DSR>=.95. A cheap pass also needs positive total/stressed net,

population is not power for the parent's sparse trade opportunities. Already-seen2023+ is not a fresh holdout.

## 8. Failure, liquidity role and account timing

Delayed entry can miss the profitable first minutes; the first opposite bar can still leave price worse.
Stop deferral can magnify/cluster losses or overlap other equity-beta pieces. Market entry after a pullback is
still liquidity consuming. Additional exposure may breach an account floor before a later rebound/offset.
No annual correlation, closed-ticket daily curve or one-micro result establishes account protection.
