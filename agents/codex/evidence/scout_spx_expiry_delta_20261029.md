## 1. Who trades and why?

The proposed actor is an SPXW dealer hedging expiring cash-settled options. At expiry option delta disappears,
potentially leaving an offsetting hedge to remove. This differs from gamma-driven rebalancing as the index moves.
The source estimates signed positions from market-maker buy/sell records accumulated from series inception.
Estimated net option delta need not equal an obligation to trade precisely the matching ES hedge: other products,
cross-book offsets, existing hedges and dealer discretion matter. The source's directional group evidence is author evidence.

## 2. Direction, size and information clock

Positive expiring dealer option delta implies a short hedge; removing it implies buying. Negative delta implies selling.
