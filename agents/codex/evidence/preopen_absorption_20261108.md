## 1. Participants and mechanism

Aggressive overnight buyers/sellers may meet patient opposite-side liquidity. New cash-opening participation
may expose remaining temporary pressure. We hypothesize that strong directional trading with stalled price
progress identifies a more profitable fade. No participant identities, inventory or forced hedge mandate are
observed; persistent informed flow and continuing impact are competing explanations. These predictions can fail.

## 7. Prior tests and falsifiers

inventory fades failed. Price-only FC/ONB/T handoff also failed2018–22. H03/H04intraday/flow programs remain closed;

## 8. Failure, liquidity and diversification

Our marketable entries/stops demand liquidity; the absorption label does not turn them into passive orders.
Price continuation after news and gaps near the opening can overwhelm a reversal stop. T/EOS and ONB/VB may
share losses during fast repricing; later-offset profits may arrive after an intraday account-floor breach.
One position/day, no cross-account hedge. No safety/portfolio benefit from historical correlation alone.
