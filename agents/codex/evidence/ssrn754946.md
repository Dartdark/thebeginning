# FX floor/screen price discovery: DON'T TEST a directional strategy

Summary: The paper measures historical venue liquidity and price discovery, without a trading rule.
Its contemporaneous estimates and retrospective volatility buckets are not pretrade signals.
Owned single-market minute bars cannot reproduce its venue-separated 30-second histories.
Retain the methods/execution cautions; zero strategy-return trials.

Source: Ates and Wang, May 2005, SSRN754946, user-uploaded full PDF archived in
`agents/codex/inputs/ssrn754946/source.pdf`; review `../results/ssrn754946.md`.

## 1. Who is forced to trade?
No prespecified forced-flow event. The paper studies traders choosing venues and
associations between relative liquidity/activity and price-discovery estimates.

## 2. Direction, size and information clock
No buy/sell direction, parent quantity, entry threshold or liquidation rule. Daily
model estimates use that day's observations; high/low volatility classification
uses each contract-period empirical distribution. Those outputs are descriptive
unless a separate past-only, as-of protocol is specified before a new test.

## 3. Transmission to NQ/ES
Identical FX futures across two mechanisms in 2003–04 are not NQ/ES directional
demand. Nor do identical contracts justify assuming the same relationships between
mini/micro contracts with distinct sizes, quotes and participant mixes.

## 4. Data gate
Study uses CFTC time-and-sales and CTR, separated by floor/Globex, Jan 2003–Mar 2004;
30-second previous-tick samples. Inspected owned 6E data has one-minute OHLCV and
filenames spanning 2017–26, not the paired historical venue data or executable quotes.
No source raw data is attached. A current lead/lag study would be a different hypothesis.

## 5. Costs and occupancy
Historical reversal-based spread estimates are not current quoted or all-in costs.
Price-discovery shares do not establish a positive move after spread, fees and latency.
No defined intraday opportunity exists for an occupancy or account test.

## 6. Power
No strategy opportunity count, per-trade dispersion or net payoff distribution;
sample days and statistical price-discovery estimates are not independent profitable trades.
No responsible t>=2 profitability hurdle can be computed from the source.

## 7. Existing work / falsifiers
No exact SSRN/title/author duplicate found in the searched records. Related E10
liquidity and E11 information-clock cautions already apply. A separately proposed
lead/lag rule must survive stale-quote, sampling, common-shock and net-cost controls.

## 8. Failure and diversification
Leadership changes with the venue/institutional regime. Same-day fitting, carried
stale prints, correlated innovations and broad information-share bounds can mislead
an executable forecast interpretation. Neither hedging nor pre-breach diversification is shown.

## Decision
DON'T TEST a trading strategy from this source. Price-discovery diagnostics are codable
with appropriate data; any investable rule requires a separately justified, preregistered
economic mechanism and actual executable paired inputs. No queue or plan change earned.
