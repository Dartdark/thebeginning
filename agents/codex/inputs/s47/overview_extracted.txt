Equity path and drawdown




The trade story
The idea: Europe sets the direction first. S47 measures how far NQ travels around the London cash open — 15
minutes before to 30 minutes after 08:00 London — in units of the overnight range. If that push is big enough it waits
out New York's first hour, then trades the same way from 09:31 CT with a stop sized to the overnight range, and
closes at 15:00 CT. v2 also requires New York's own first hour (08:30 → 09:30 CT) to point the same way: the European
push confirmed by the US open, in the same market.

Rules
 Volatility unit                   ATR_ON = average overnight range (17:01 → 08:30 CT) of the previous 20 trading days, at least 15
                                   usable.

 London signal                     s = (close at London 08:30 − close at London 07:45) ÷ ATR_ON — normally 02:30 and 01:45 CT.
                                   Long if s > +0.0625, short if s < −0.0625.

 v2 only                           Trade only if 09:30 CT close − last close at or before 08:30 CT has the same sign as s.

 Entry / exits                     Market at the 09:31 bar · stop 0.5 × ATR_ON from the bar after entry · exit at the 15:00 CT close · no
                                   target · one trade a day.

 Skipped days                      Quarterly roll days, early-close holidays, unusable overnight sessions.

After 2024 — does the regime still show up?
For v2, yes: $505.00 in 2024 (weak but positive), $5,845.50 in 2025 and $2,115.00 in 2026 to 2026-10-05. Plain S47 lost
−⁠$2,106.00 in 2024, then made $4,219.00 in 2025 and $6,313.00 in 2026 to 2026-10-16 (1 MNQ). The calm-market
weakness seen in 2017 and 2019 is the thing to watch.
