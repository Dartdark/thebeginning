The trade story
The idea comes from auction reading: a bullish 15-minute bar that closes above its 50-bar average with only a small
lower wick shows buyers in control. The strategy leaves a buy limit at that bar's open for one bar — the retest — and,
if filled, rides it with a structure stop under the latest swing low, a 3R target and an early break-even. Trades still open
run into the evening and are closed from 21:00 CT; a position open on Friday is held over the weekend. v9 adds the
mirror-image short, taken only on down-trend days.

Rules
 Long signal                       15-minute bar stamped 09:00–13:59 CT: close > open, close > EMA(50) of closes, lower wick (open
                                   − low) ≤ 70 ticks.

 Entry                             Buy limit at the signal bar's open, valid for the next bar only. One position at a time.

 Exits                             Stop at the most recent fractal swing low of bars 2–10 back if that risk is ≤ 500 ticks, target 3R, and
                                   the stop moves to entry + 5 ticks once price has moved 0.5R in favour. Otherwise a 200-tick stop
                                   and a 200-tick target. Market exit from the first bar at or after 21:00 CT.

 v9 only: short                    The mirror (close < open, close < EMA(50), upper wick ≤ 70 ticks, sell limit at the open,
                                   swing-high stop, 3R, break-even at entry − 5 ticks) on down-trend days only: the prior
                                   session's 15:00 close is below the average of the last 50 15:00 closes and that average is
                                   below its value 5 sessions earlier.
