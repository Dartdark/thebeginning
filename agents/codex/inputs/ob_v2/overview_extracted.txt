The trade story
Where NQ opens inside its overnight range shows which side the night favoured. An open in the top third of the
overnight range allows only longs that day, an open in the bottom third only shorts, and anything in between is a
no-trade day. After the first 15 minutes, the strategy takes the first 15-minute close beyond that opening range in
the allowed direction, but only when the market is trending (ADX) and the breakout bar is not an exhaustion bar.
Stop and target scale with daily volatility. v2 adds one condition: recent volatility must be in a normal range
compared with the last 100 days, which skips the most unusual markets.

Rules
Day's direction                Open at 08:30 CT inside the overnight range (midnight → 08:30 CT; the date change resets it):
                               top third (≥ 0.67) = longs only, bottom third (≤ 0.33) = shorts only, otherwise no trade.

Opening range                  08:30–08:45 CT high and low. Day skipped if that range is more than 2 × its recent (20-day)
                               average.

Entry                          First 15-minute close beyond the opening range in the allowed direction, 08:45–12:00 CT, with
                               ADX(16) ≥ 20 and the bar's range ≤ 2.5 × the average of the prior 20 bars; market order on the
                               next bar. One position at a time, up to 4 trades a day.

Exits                          Stop 0.30 × ATR from the signal close, where ATR = the 15-day average true range of the
                               08:30–15:00 session; target 3 × the stop; market exit at 14:30 CT.

v2 regime filter               Trade only when that 15-day ATR ÷ the 100-day ATR is between 0.6 and 1.2.
