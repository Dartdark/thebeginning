The trade story
Every morning it watches the first 30 minutes of the New York session (08:30–09:00 CT). If that window closes up
or down and the order flow inside it was unusually one-sided the same way compared with its own last 60 days,
it enters that direction at 09:01 and holds into the afternoon with a wide stop, a break-even step and a loose trail
— no target. Most days give back a little; the trend days pay for everything.
Rules
 Opening range                     1-minute bars stamped 08:31–09:00 CT.

 Direction                         Sign of the range's close minus its open.

 Flow filter                       Per bar Volume × (Close − Open) ÷ (High − Low), summed over the range ÷ range volume;
                                   scored as a z-score against the previous 60 days (≥ 20 needed). Trade only if direction and z
                                   agree beyond ±1.0.

 Entry                             Market at the 09:01 bar.

 Exits                             Stop 100 points · after +66 points the stop moves to entry + 1 point · 150-point trail from the
                                   best price · no target · market exit from 14:58 CT.

How it was validated: the first 2B rule — stop at the opening-range extreme, break-even step, no trail — was
fitted on 2016–2021 (455 trades, PF 1.51, net $7,570.50), checked on 2022–2023 (PF 1.72, net $7,560) and run
once, frozen, on the Feb 2024 → 2026-06-01 holdout: 211 trades, PF 1.42, net $7,106.50, max drawdown
$2,320.50 (1 MNQ). That holdout was later re-read to reject variants and then retired. Today's exits — a fixed 100-
point stop and a 150-point trail — were set on 2026-07-19 on the full sample. They give up some profit for a
smaller drawdown (about $20,640.50 / $1,611 against the first rule's ~$21,900 / $2,320.50 per MNQ) and were
registered as a forward challenger. The 2023 → column is therefore a later-period check of today's rules, not a
frozen out-of-sample run.

After 2024 — does the regime still show up?
So far yes: $2,752.50 in 2024, $3,841.00 in 2025 and $1,468.00 in 2026 to 2026-10-16 (1 MNQ). In the 2024–2026
part of the full run, 239 trades averaged $32.10 with a 66.5% win rate and PF 1.50. One thing has changed: the
fixed 100-point stop was 2.2 × daily ATR in 2017 and is about 0.23 × ATR in 2026 — tighter, in volatility terms,
than anything in the development years.
