03 / Mechanism

How the noise floor trades

What the signal does, where the money comes from, and whether the v2 stop is a finding or a fit.

Rules as coded

  1. Noise bandσ(t) is the mean of |close(t) ÷ open(09:30) − 1| over the last 14 full sessions at the same minute. Upper = max(open, prior close) × (1 + σ); lower = min(open, prior close) × (1 − σ).
  2. Half-hour checksOnly on the bars stamped 10:00, 10:30 … 15:30. Long when close > upper and > VWAP; short when close < lower and < VWAP. An opposite signal reverses.
  3. TrailAt the same checks, exit a long when close < max(upper, VWAP) and a short when close > min(lower, VWAP).
  4. Protective stop (v2)Entry ∓ 0.4 × σ(t) × signal close, live from the fill bar and filled intrabar.
  5. Flat dailyMarket exit off the 16:00 bar (fills 16:01 open). Exchange holidays, early closes and two mourning days are skipped.

LucidFlex first passage on MNQ

Rules from third-party summaries dated Aug–Sep 2026 — confirm on Lucid's site. 50K: $3,000 target, $2,000 EOD-trailing MLL locking at +$100, 50% evaluation consistency, no daily limit, 40 micros max. 150K: $9,000 / $4,500 / 100 micros. Funded: 5 qualifying days per cycle, payout 50% of profit capped at $2,000 / $3,000, 90% split. MNQ costs assumed $2 RT + 1 tick/side, which takes 24% of the gross edge per contract (12% on NQ). Breach is tested on the realized intraday path, not just the close. History = an evaluation started on every session with a year of runway; bootstrap = 4,000 block-resampled years.

Highlighted rows are the recommended sizes. The evaluation wants 2× the funded size: at 1 MNQ a 50K eval takes most of a year; at 3+ MNQ the $2,000 trailing limit breaks it. Funded, only 1 MNQ (50K) or 2 MNQ (150K) survives a typical year, because payouts pull the balance back toward the locked limit. Every account on this signal breaches on the same days — more accounts is more of the same bet, not diversification.

05 / Verdict

Trade it small, after three checks

What holds

The edge and the stop are both real

The replica reproduces the header within 2%. The v2 stop halves drawdown (−$40.6K → −$22.3K) while adding profit; it sits on a 0.35–0.6 plateau, survives walk-forward with a 5% haircut and wins by 51% at equal risk. No Monte Carlo engine produces a losing nine-year path.

What worries

Thin lately, and tail-driven

PF is 1.22 since July 2024 and 1.11 without April 2025. 43.5% of all profit sits in 10 positions, so execution has to be perfect on exactly the days that are hardest to trade — fast opens and headline rallies. Shorts are lumpy and depend on crash years.

Size
1 NQ per $200K

Plan on the IID P95 drawdown of about −$40K per NQ, not the backtest's −$22K, and keep it under 20% of risk capital: 1 MNQ per $20K. On LucidFlex: evaluate at 2 MNQ, trade funded at 1 MNQ on a 50K (≈$1.7K expected payouts in a year); on a 150K evaluate at 4 and fund at 2 (≈$3.9K).

Before live

Three checks

  1. Tick-validate the stop. Run it in MultiCharts on tick data or a 1-tick bar magnifier. 56% of positions exit on the stop and 132 inside the entry minute; this report is replica-only and the real number will differ.
  2. Back-adjusted series. This data is unadjusted, so prior close crosses a roll about four times a year, shifting one side of the band on those days.
  3. Correlation with the NQ quartet. Check daily P&L correlation against the strategies already on the Lucid engine before adding it; its value there is as a diversifier.
