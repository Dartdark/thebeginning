// ABSTRACT
THE CASE.
Every regular session the first 5-minute candle on NQ
(08:30-08:35 Chicago) picks the side. The trade is taken only if
price is already at least 1.5 bar-ATRs beyond the Supertrend
line on that side. Stop at 0.1 × the 14-day average daily range
(ADR), break-even after 0.5 ADR, exit at 15:00, up to three
re-entries the same way. One trade in five wins; the winners are
five times the losers.
On 1 NQ mini with the Valeris size: development (2017 to
October 2022) 1,446 trades, $201,362, t 5.16. OOS 1 (to
December 2023) $45,027, t 1.75 against 1.96; OOS 2 (2024 to
September 2026) $103,748, t 2.25 against 3.92. On 1 micro
since 26/10/2022: $14,519, worst drawdown $2,030
(30/06-17/09/2026).
Prop bootstrap (Matteo's method, fixed size, last 30% of days):
evaluation within 30 days 17.4% on LucidFlex 50K, 17.7% on
25K, 36.2% on Apex 50K, far from the 60% target. Long only

// THE PAPER, IN FULL
HYPOTHESIS.
Zarattini, C., Aziz, A. (2023). Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable? Evidence of Sustainable Long-term Profits from Opening
Range Breakout (ORB) Day Trading Strategy vs. Benchmark in the US Stock Market. SSRN working paper 4416622.
// WHAT THE AUTHORS TEST
The paper starts from a well-known fact: most people who day
trade lose money. The authors ask whether a simple, fully
mechanical rule can do better, and test the oldest intraday idea
there is, the opening range breakout, on QQQ (the Nasdaq-100
ETF) with 5-minute bars from January 2016 to February 2023, a
period that includes the 2020 crash and the 2022 bear market.
The rule. Look at the first 5-minute candle of the New York
session (09:30-09:35). If it went up, buy at the start of the second
candle; if it went down, sell short. The stop sits at the other end of
the first candle (its low for a long, its high for a short). The target is
ten times that risk (10R). Whatever is still open is closed at the end
of the day. One trade a day at most, sized so that a stop costs 1%
of the account, with leverage capped at four times (the US
day-trading limit), commission included.
// WHY IT SHOULD WORK
The open concentrates information. Overnight news, earnings
and the orders that piled up since the previous close all reach the
market in the first minutes. The first candle is the market's first vote
on that imbalance.
The imbalance persists. Large investors cannot fill big orders at
once: they work them through the day. If the first minutes show
buyers in control, the same flow tends to keep coming, and the day
keeps moving the same way more often than chance would say.
This is intraday momentum.
The payoff is asymmetric. A stop at the edge of the first candle is
small; the 10R target, or the close, lets a trend day run. The rule
can be wrong on most days and still make money, because a few
trend days pay for many small losses. And it leaves nothing to
judgement, which is where day traders usually fail.

// THE RAW PAPER RULE ON NQ
Side from the first candle, entry at 08:35, exit at 15:00 (the
cash close), no stop: on NQ in 2017-10/2022 it made 1,424
trades, $52,439 on 1 mini, 51% winners, t 0.57, worst
drawdown $101,276. Positive, enough for step 1 of Alpha
(average trade at least half the NQ threshold), but nowhere
near significant on its own.
The paper gives the idea and the side; the optimisation gave everything else. What carries the result is the
asymmetry the authors describe: one trade in five wins, and the winners are five times the losers.

// THE RULE, STEP BY STEP
HOW IT WORKS.
Chicago (exchange) time, 5-minute bars stamped at their close: the 08:35 bar runs from 08:30 to 08:35. One position at a time, everything
closed the same day. ADR = average high-low range of the last 14 regular sessions (08:35-15:00); ATR = average true range. Both are
yesterday's values, fixed for the whole day.
1 · FIRST CANDLE At 08:35, compare the close of the 08:35 bar with the close of the 08:30 bar. Up: the day is long. Down: the day
is short. Unchanged: no trade today.
2 · FILTER Supertrend with 10 bars and multiplier 2, on every 5-minute bar, night session included. Distance z = (close -
Supertrend line) × side ÷ ATR(14) of the 5-minute bars. Trade only if z ≥ 1.5: price must already be running in
the candle's direction, well clear of the line.
3 · ENTRY Market order at the close of the 08:35 bar. Never on holidays or half-days; the first 14 regular sessions of the
chart are skipped (ATR and ADR need them).
4 · STOP 0.1 × ADR from the entry price, active from the next bar. In 2026 that is 21 to 70 NQ points, median 39 (about
$78 per micro). If a bar opens beyond the stop, the exit is at the open.
5 · BREAK-EVEN When price has moved 0.5 ADR in favour (using the highs or lows after the entry bar), from the next bar the
stop moves to the entry price.
6 · TIME EXIT A target at 5 ADR exists but was never touched in 2017-2026. Otherwise the base trade closes at the close of
the 15:00 bar, the cash close.
7 · RE-ENTRIES After any exit, wait 4 bars, then re-enter the same side at the close of the bar where the close crosses the
level: for a long day, yesterday's close - 0.25 × daily ATR(14), crossed upwards; for a short day, yesterday's
close + 0.25 ATR, crossed downwards. Same stop and break-even from the new price; re-entries close at
15:30 (never after 15:40). At most 4 trades a day: the base and three re-entries.
8 · SIZE Valeris sizes dynamically: weight = (median ADR of the last 250 sessions ÷ ADR)0.5, between 0.25 and 4, so
more contracts when the market is quiet. The prop numbers here use a fixed size (input TagliaDinamica = 0):
same trades, constant contracts.
In MultiCharts the side can be restricted with the input Lato: 0 both sides (as optimised), 1 long only, -1 short only (section 06). The rule uses
only open, high, low and close of NQ: no volume, no other market.

The stop follows volatility: median 4 points in 2017, 19 in 2020, 21 in 2023, 39 in 2026 (from 21 to 70). One NQ point is $2 on the micro and $20 on
the mini. The worst single trade out of sample, −$162.55 on 1 micro (17/04/2025), came from an April 2025 ADR of about 810 points.

pass = price ÷ pass rate, plus the $140 activation fee on Apex. Only the first payout counts; later payouts, resets and discounts are ignored, and
the trader share is 90% on LucidFlex. It is a sketch of the order of magnitude, with the favourable final 30%: around zero for the two-sided
strategy, negative for the long-only version.
On these accounts the strategy alone is roughly a break-even bet per attempt, and only on the strongest
stretch of data. Its value, if any, is as one more independent leg of a pack.
