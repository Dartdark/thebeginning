1 What the strategies do
Two builds of the same NQ day-hold entry. The BASE build is the entry and the timed exit alone. The FILTERED build
is the same entry and exit with two conditions on the day that must hold before the signal is allowed to fire. Neither
build has a stop, a target or a trail.

1.1 The signal
The reference bar is the first five-minute bar of the New York session, the one that closes at 08:35 Chicago time. At
that bar's close the signal compares the close to a 12-bar EMA of five-minute closes. Close above the EMA buys,
close below sells short, at that bar's close. One signal per calendar day.

The trade is a bet that the direction of the opening five minutes persists into the close. It is held for the whole
session whatever happens in between.

1.2 The exit
The position is closed at the close of the first bar at or after 15:00 Chicago time, the 16:00 New York cash close.
SetExitOnClose is set as a backstop, so on a half day where the 15:00 bar never prints the session end closes the
trade instead.

1.3 The filters (FILTERED build only)
Both conditions are measured in units of the average day-session range, the mean high-low of the last ADRLength
day sessions from 08:30 to 15:00. A range unit is used instead of points so that a setting means the same at NQ
7,000 as at NQ 25,000.

  Reference bar strength. The first five-minute bar's own range must be at least RefBarMinRange of the average
  day range. The filter asks for a real opening move before the direction of that move is trusted.
  Volatility regime. The 20-session average day range must be at least VolRegimeRatio times the 100-session
  average. The entry only trades when intraday ranges are expanding against their own recent history.

A day that fails either test takes no trade, and there is no second attempt that day.

 The regime filter needs history. No trade is taken until VolRegimeLongLen sessions (100 by default, about five
 months) have been stored. Backtests of the FILTERED build start later than the BASE build on the same data;
 compare the two from the same date.
2 Setting it up
Both signals run on one data series and need nothing beyond the inputs. The chart they expect:

Group          NQ opening-direction day hold

Instrument     NQ continuous contract (@NQ)

Data 1         5 Min, 24-hour Globex session

Data 2         none

Time zone      CT. Defaults are set for an Exchange (Chicago) chart.

Step 1. Build the chart. NQ, five-minute bars, full Globex session, chart time zone Exchange (Chicago). The
signal reads clock times, so a chart in another time zone shifts the reference bar and the exit without any error.

Step 2. Bars back. Auto-detect is enough; nothing looks back further than the EMA.
Step 3. Load the signal. Compile NQ_First5_EMA_DayHold_BASE or NQ_First5_EMA_DayHold_FILTERED, add one of
them to the chart, and check that the input list matches section 3.
Step 4. Costs and fills. Set commission and slippage in the strategy properties. Bar Magnifier is not needed,
since every order fills at a bar close.

 Changing the bar size changes the reference bar. The signal is defined on the first five-minute bar. On
 one-minute bars SignalTime 0835 is the fifth bar of the session and the EMA is a different average; on fifteen-
 minute bars the reference bar does not exist. Keep the chart at five minutes.
3 The inputs
These are the defaults in both builds. The BASE build has the first six; the FILTERED build adds the rest.

3.1 Entry and exit (both builds)
INPUT                DEFAULT    WHAT IT DOES

EMALength            12         Length of the EMA the reference bar close is compared to.

SignalTime           0835       Close time of the reference bar, Chicago time.

ExitTime             1500       The first bar closing at or after this time exits at its close. 1500 CT is the 16:00 ET cash
                                close.

AllowLongs           true       false disables long entries.

AllowShorts          true       false disables short entries.

NumContracts         1          Contracts per entry.


3.2 Filters (FILTERED build)
INPUT                DEFAULT    WHAT IT DOES

SessionOpen          0830       Day-session open, Chicago time. Bars after this and up to ExitTime build the day range.

RefBarMinRange       0.15       Minimum range of the reference bar as a fraction of the average day range. 0 switches
                                the filter off.

VolRegimeRatio       1.10       The 20-session average day range must be at least this multiple of the 100-session
                                average.

ADRLength            20         Sessions in the short average day range.

VolRegimeLongLen     100        Sessions in the long average day range. Also the warm-up length.

VolRegimeRatio is the input that decides how many days trade: raising it admits fewer days, all of them in higher-
volatility stretches. RefBarMinRange removes the days where the opening bar itself was too small to carry a
direction.
