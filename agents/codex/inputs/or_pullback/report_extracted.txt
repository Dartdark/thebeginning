01 / HYPOTHESIS & RULES
Wait for the break to be defended
Initiative at the open, conﬁrmed by a pullback that fails to re-enter the range.
A break of the ﬁrst ﬁfteen minutes' range shows one side taking initiative. Buying the break itself often buys the top
of the impulse. Waiting for the ﬁrst counter-colour 5-minute candle that still closes outside the range enters at a
better price — and only after the market has shown it is defending the break. The 15-minute range, 5-minute
conﬁrmation and ﬁxed bracket were chosen in a structured search over opening-range variants on 2021–23 data,
then frozen.
Execution assumptions. Entry at the signal bar's close; exits on 1-minute bars; 1 point per MNQ round trip (≈
commission + one tick of slippage each side). Fixed size, no averaging, no scaling.
Opening range = high and low of 09:30–09:45 New York time.01
Long: armed once a 5-minute bar closes above the range high. Trigger = the ﬁrst later red bar that still closes
above it. Enter at that close.
02
Short: mirror image — armed by a close below the range low; trigger = the ﬁrst later green bar still closing
below it.
03
One trigger per side per day; a trigger blocked by a guard still uses up that side.04
Bracket: stop 50 points, target 100 points from entry. If both are touched in the same minute, the stop is
assumed ﬁrst.
05
Time exit: ﬂat at 15:55. Guards: one position at a time, max 2 trades and 2 losing trades per day, entries on
bars closing 09:35–15:30.

04 / PROP USE · LIMITS · SETUP
How to run it
Thin per-trade edge, steady path, regime-dependent.
In a prop evaluation. Alone at 2 MNQ under 50K
LucidFlex-style rules ($3k target, $2k end-of-day trailing
drawdown, 50% consistency), pass within 60 days: 41%
(2021–23), 35% (2024–25), 44% (holdout). Better as one
component of a diversiﬁed set of strategies than as a
stand-alone evaluation strategy.
Switch-oﬀ rule. Track the rolling 6-month net proﬁt ÷
max drawdown; size to zero below half its backtest norm
and resume when it recovers. Historically this cut the
2010–16 losses by about three-quarters, at the cost of a
third of 2021–26 proﬁt.
Limits.
Regime dependence — section 03.
Thin edge — +$12.6 per MNQ ≈ 6 points; two
extra ticks of slippage a side remove a sixth of it.
Selection — the rules came from a search; the
holdout sits inside the same 2021+ regime.
Fixed points — 50/100 is tuned to 2021–26
prices; PriceScaled keeps the proportion.
MultiCharts. Signal PB_F9b_ORPullback.txt. NQ or MNQ, 5-minute bars, bar-close stamps, intra-bar order
generation oﬀ, Bar Magniﬁer 1 minute. Chart time zone = Exchange; ChartIsCentral = true for Chicago-
time feeds, false for New-York-stamped bars. Set commission and slippage in Strategy Properties.
Data: NQ continuous 1-minute bars 2010-06 – 2026-07; ES 2010–2026; GC 2017–2026. Win rate = trades with positive net P&L. Proﬁt factor =
gross wins ÷ gross losses. Max DD = peak-to-trough closed-trade equity. Research only — not trading advice.
