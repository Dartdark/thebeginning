THE IDEA


Gold earns at night.
Blose, Gondhalekar and Kort (2018) find it across
COMEX gold futures, spot gold, mining stocks and
gold funds. Their reading: opening prices are set
too high and drift lower during the session.

WHAT I TAKE AWAY


Four lessons.
1   Count every trial.
    About 7.3 million configurations were counted.
    Corrected for them, the development t of 5.10 is not
    significant, so both windows were exploratory tests.



2   One pass is not validation.
    Seven variants went through both windows: one
    cleared the first threshold, none the second.



3   Know what you are harvesting.
    On the Nasdaq-100 the edge may simply be the
    equity overnight premium, not the gold anomaly.



4   Test the edges.
    Sensitivity checks rated it fragile: one small step
    away from the chosen settings, it loses money.

THE VERDICT


Not confirmed.
It cleared the first out-of-sample threshold, not the
second. It stays positive, with thinner margins.

After millions of trials, a t of 5 is a reason to verify the result,
not a reason to believe it.


The next clean test: data after
