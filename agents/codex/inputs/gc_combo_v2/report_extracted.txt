GC_COMBO_v2        ·   10:00 BLEND + STRETCH SHORT                ·    @GC FUTURES



Hypothesis:
Tested rule: at 10:00 ET, add five fixed-sign votes (morning price, against morning taker flow, GLD creations, big crude move,
price again on dollar-decoupled days). Sum ≥ +2 buy, ≤ −2 sell, next bar; exit the 13:30 settlement bar, no stop. Separately,
short when price closes 0.5 ATR under session VWAP; stop 0.505 ATR, target 2R. One position at a time. It passed a sealed out-
of-sample read. The mechanism is still an open case.

Why it should exist: no single gold flow pays the round turn on its own. Each vote is a small, sourced edge — the New York morning move continues, aggressive
morning takers get faded, GLD authorised participants must buy or sell bullion after creations and redemptions, a large crude shock moves inflation hedges, and
on days gold ignores the dollar its own flow dominates. Stacked, the cost is paid once. The stretch short is different: a downside break of VWAP in gold is
liquidation, a cascade, not an overreaction.

What this note proves: the frozen rule survived a sealed 2025–26 read on three pre-declared marks, and MultiCharts reconciles trade-for-trade with an independent
Python replay. What it does not prove: that each story behind each vote is the true cause, or why 10:00 New York and not the London auction. Treat the votes as
the hypothesis and the frozen rule as the result.
