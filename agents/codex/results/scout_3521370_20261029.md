## Four questions

1. **About:** Krohn, Mueller and Whelan, *Foreign Exchange Fixings and Returns around the Clock*, SSRN3521370,
   published Journal of Finance79(1),2024, pp541–578; online2023-12-18. Intraday currency behavior around benchmarks.
2. **Findings — authors:** TableI reports EUR pre-ECB drift−3.53bp/day(t−7.25),1999–2019; TableIII also finds
   patterns in futures. SectionV.C/TableVI identifies a shift to14:00Frankfurt after2016. SectionVI.E/TableX finds
   negative cost-adjusted client trading returns despite positive dealer liquidity-provider returns.
3. **Why money:** Proposed compensation for dealers warehousing inventory against customer dollar demand.
   This is neither a demonstrated edge for our market orders nor independently reproduced causal evidence.
4. **Build on it:** A bounded6E short from08:00 to14:00Frankfurt is a source-informed adaptation worth a cheap
   modern cost screen. It differs from the rejected price-conditioned post-London fade. Do not copy dealer spreads.

## Adaptation and dedup before outcomes

Use the modern14:00Frankfurt turning point; the reference-rate calculation/publication distinction matters.
Source SectionI keeps ECB calculation at14:15 but moves publication to16:00; SectionV.C identifies the newly
introduced WMR14:00 turning point. Our fixed08:00–14:00civil Frankfurt window is explicit, not a forecast-rate signal.
No auction, equity-return, month-end, price-threshold, order-flow or volatility-conditioning input is used.
All weekdays are considered, including any non-fixing holidays with quotes: deliberate broad calendar adaptation,
not a reconstruction of the authors' filtered event calendar. No exceptional-date return selection is permitted.

Prior `fx_fix_reversal.md` tested varying sides determined by15:30–16:03London prices, after London16:03 with
30/60-minute holds and an ATR stop. This proposal instead tests unconditional dollar-demand drift before a different
European benchmark. `hunt_v2_prefix_equity_source.md` concerns month-end relative-equity hedging; it stays paused.
The generic overnight/time-series scans and FX weekend-gap failure remain part of selection history.
No exact08:00–14:00Frankfurt unconditional6E-short rule was found in the inspected scripts/reports.

## Scope of this first stage

One standard6E reference, EUR125,000, tick0.00005=$6.25. Cost benchmark from the prior6E engine:
$7.25round-trip commission plus one tick on each side=$19.75. This is a research assumption, not a broker quote.
No M6E fees are borrowed from MNQ and no micro account result is inferred. The cheap reference screen has no stop;
it asks whether the source drift has enough gross edge to justify building a protected execution rule.
Endpoint omissions remain unknown, never zero-filled. Native contract IDs/roll timing are absent from these continuous
exports; a positive screen cannot establish executable fills, account risk or admission past that unresolved audit.
