## Information and execution gate

completed reversal uses the unknown future reversal endpoint. Scaling that range down does not establish an
available-at timestamp, first executable price, stop, timeout or permitted overnight policy. These missing causal
rules stop the research before gross screens; better price data do not cure the rule. This does not assert that
all smoothing is necessarily noncausal, or prove no genuine reversal strategy can exist.
