## Frozen rules and execution

Both directions. The original confirmed swing / HH-HL versus LH-LL structure-break logic is unchanged. One or two completed candles on EACH side confirm a strict swing, usable only when right-side confirmation closes. A close through the last opposing confirmed swing gives a reversal entry. Stop remains at the latest confirmed opposite swing. Target is now twice raw entry-to-stop distance; this changes the actual exit path, not a rescaling of old returns. No filters, side deletion, stop cap or new trend definition.

Four-hour uses the same CME-session full windows ending 21:00/01:00/05:00/09:00/13:00 CT, excludes the final three-hour stub, and holds until bracket exit including across daily close/weekends.

Hourly uses CT clock-hour context throughout the CME session, requiring all expected open minutes; the historical pre-June-28-2021 pause is excluded from expected minutes. Incomplete expected candles reset structure. Eligible signal closes are **09:00–14:00 CT** on normal cash sessions. Remaining open positions exit at the OPEN of the minute labelled **15:58 CT** (physical 15:57). On repo-defined early closes, exit at OPEN11:30 CT, and last qualifying signal is 10:00. Cash holidays/weekends/mourning dates are skipped. At least sixty labelled minutes must remain after a qualifying signal close. This RTH/flat convention was stated before returns, rather than selected from them. It is an intraday conversion, not an assertion that the screenshot specified these hours.

Primary fill is OPEN(signal minute+2), one physical minute after the completed close. The matched ideal-close benchmark uses the signal closing price, begins exit checks afterward and is not a guaranteed obtainable-close claim. One position; later signals while held are skipped. Active entry protection, worse observed gap-open fills, shared minute path and one-point target-through are retained. Intraday bracket walking ends before the scheduled time-exit open; the planned exit uses that open, not the previous close or a later bar extreme.

One MNQ scenario on NQ prices: $2/point, round-trip commission $1.24 plus one 0.25-point tick per side = **$2.24 per unit trade**. Pre-MNQ-launch history/native micro quotes/roll identity remain unverified. Realized risk/reward is not always 1:2 because time exits and gaps can end trades sooner or at worse prices. No account risk sizing is added.

## Why hourly win rate does not imply full 2R winners

Hourly R2: **293/408 resolved trades exit at the time cutoff**, 83 stop and only 32 reach the full 2R target. Hourly R1: 401/571 time exits, 134 stops, 36 targets. Therefore 48.28% winning hourly R2 trades are not all 2R wins. The median swing-stop distance is 110.25 NQ points ($220.50/MNQ), 95th percentile 301.14 points and maximum 776.25 points ($1,552.50/MNQ before gaps/costs). A single micro is not automatically suitable for a 50K evaluation daily limit or the shared book's drawdown budget.

## Recommendation

Park the four-hour 1:2 versions. Keep the hourly two-bar rule only as a **research lead**: it is positive across both sides and four years, but t 1.22 is weak and there is no demonstrated book contribution. Do not immediately add indicators or tune its session, swing size or stop cap.
