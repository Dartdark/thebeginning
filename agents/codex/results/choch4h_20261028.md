## Rule and clocks

NQ; close-stamped Central-time minute data. Full CME-session-aligned four-hour windows close at 21:00, 01:00, 05:00, 09:00 and 13:00 CT. The final 13:00–16:00 stub is not used as a four-hour signal/pivot candle. This anchor is our explicit test choice; the screenshot does not specify its chart anchor.

A strict pivot is higher/lower than its one or two neighboring candles on BOTH sides. It is usable only after right-side confirmation closes. Two successively higher confirmed highs and lows establish bullish structure; lower highs and lows establish bearish structure. A close through the last opposing swing gives the mirrored reversal entry, as specified in the plan. Each broken pivot is consumed once. Incomplete expected candles reset the structure instead of filling missing prices.

Short: bullish structure breaks below its latest confirmed low; stop at latest confirmed high. Long mirrors it. Raw-price target is one entry-to-stop distance away. No EMA/filter, arbitrary stop cap or time stop was added. Hold until bracket exit, including overnight/weekends. One position at a time, no pyramiding.

The close benchmark uses the signal close but checks exits only afterward; it is **not** a claim that the completed close can be obtained. Executable proxy uses the open of the minute labelled signal+2, one physical minute after the signal close. This avoids treating the immediately adjacent opening print as a guaranteed post-decision fill. Both use active entry-bar protection, worse observed gap opens and the shared repo execution path with one-point target-through.

Costs are a **MNQ cost scenario on NQ prices**: $2/point; $1.24 commission and one 0.25-point tick on each fill, $2.24 total per unit round trip. Pre-launch 2018 MNQ and native micro spread/roll execution are not verified. Stops remain at swing prices; 1:1 is before trading costs.

## Source finding and correction history

Audited design inputs: 1,769,357 minute rows; no duplicate keys, conflicts or malformed observed OHLCV rows. There are 4,932 complete candidate four-hour windows and 1,696 incomplete expected windows in the corrected run. Empty/missing bars are not imputed.

The key problem is **usually a missing 17:01 CT minute**, not an entirely absent opening hour. For example, 188 observed evening dates in 2018 have 59 of the expected 60 opening-hour minutes, and only five have all 60. In 2022, 225 dates have 59 and only 29 have all 60. Other opening-hour gaps also occur. Without that first minute, a 17:01–21:00 candle's high/low/open cannot be authenticated. Repeated missing candles prevent a continuous confirmed swing sequence, leaving the tiny late-2022 sample.

Timestamp-only complete-opening-hour counts by source year: 2018 5/256, 2019 6/258, 2020 5/259, 2021 4/259, 2022 29/258; 2023 249/258, 2024 254/259, 2025 254/258, 2026 201/201 observed evening dates.
