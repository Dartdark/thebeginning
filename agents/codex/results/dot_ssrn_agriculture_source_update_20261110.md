## Private WASDE forecasts: source-gated personal reserve
[SSRN5023323](https://ssrn.com/abstract=5023323), *Private Forecasts and Market Dynamic*, identity verified; exact full PDF unavailable. Related [2024 thesis](https://scholarworks.uark.edu/etd/5486/), [full mirror](https://www.mobt3ath.com/uplode/book/book-129335.pdf), pp.20–27: private world-ending-stocks forecast above prior WASDE implies short, below implies long; release-close to WASDE-close hold, overnight. Table4 values6.4819/5.1294 are coefficients on **subsequently realized forecast accuracy**, not unconditional trading returns. Ex-post best analysts are not preselectable. Thesis sample descriptions conflict.

Related [2026 journal article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/agr.70150) abstract reports soybean consensus returns positive/significant, corn indistinguishable from zero, before costs. Added author/sample means parity cannot be assumed. Licensed Bloomberg surveys/Barchart futures required.

[Cao–Robe full paper](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/284827/1/FUT_FUT22283.pdf), §3 p.256: survey database lacks exact publication times; authors recover them from Bloomberg News. Fixed three-day entry is unsafe. Need original forecasts, publication receipts, crop-year alignment, unrevised WASDE, native contracts, costs and untouched validation. Public USDA releases alone are insufficient.

## Intraday execution evidence, not alpha
- [Fast money around Federal Statistics Releases](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ajae.12356): full methods use first-two-second positions aligned with surprise direction and later average prices. Needs millisecond depth and news/survey timestamps. Not evidence for a retail trade initiated five minutes afterward.
- [WASDE Surprises and Futures Prices2026](https://higherlogicdownload.s3.amazonaws.com/ASFMRA/aeb240ec-5d8f-447f-80ff-3c90f13db621/UploadedImages/Journal/2026/014_Balsamo_ASFMRA_2026_Journal_vF.pdf): full source regresses pre-to-post announcement movements on eventual surprises. Information unavailable at the initial reference price cannot be used retrospectively as an entry signal.
- [Real-Time Tracking of Public Announcements in the Limit Order Book](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/fut.22577),2025: full source,18 grain announcement days,3,000-contract execution comparison. Savings versus uniform splitting, not round-trip returns. Assumes no permanent impact/replenishment; incomplete-order treatment and ten-minute estimate timestamps need reconciliation. Center/beginning labels do not establish a causal separately lagged trading implementation. Proprietary CME MBP data; no verified SSRN identity.
These sources support stricter information/execution gates, not new strategy slots.
