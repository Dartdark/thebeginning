## Treasury auctions: exact repository duplicate
[SSRN 6447502](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6447502), [official October 2026 full paper](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1188.pdf), §§3–4 and Tables 2–3: 1,684 cash-Treasury auctions, June 1991–July 2024, GovPX/BrokerTec minute data. Measure 180 minutes before auction close and after actual results release, excluding the announcement jump. Recent 2015–24 combined pressure is 0.631 yield bp for 10-year notes (10% significance), 0.906 for five-year (1%). No net futures strategy or frozen trading holdout is established. Same-window order flow explains outcomes, not an advance signal.

**Decision:** Do not add a new slot. [TIER_LIST](https://github.com/Dartdark/thebeginning/blob/codex/work/TIER_LIST.md#L165) already names this exact paper under D. Its recorded ZN 2018–26 calendar adaptation has pre-leg gross $15.5 versus $38.50 cost, no post-leg rebound, and both legs net −$63/day with 0/9 positive years. These are prior repository results, not new tests. The updated source figure modestly revises the older approximate 0.59bp; fixed 12:01 Central entry differs from actual historical result-release alignment, but neither distinction makes this a fresh discovery. Entry 21’s pre-refunding event is separate.

## Forecasting and Managing Volatility: known risk-method family
[SSRN 5013407](https://ssrn.com/abstract=5013407), [full JOIM 2025 publication](https://www.joim.com/wp-content/uploads/emember/downloads/p0776.pdf), pp.4–21. Allocate equity weight min(10%/forecast annual volatility, 1.5), remainder bills. Daily squared-return forecasts use 5/21 days; exponential smoothing uses .94 decay. Intraday measures use five-minute returns, with full realized variance adding the overnight gap. Evaluation August 2006–November 2023 emphasizes rolling-volatility target error, not alpha. The paper compares forecast lag and slower rebalancing. Exhibit 7 turnover and Appendix A.6's stated two-leg costs need reconciliation: 22% daily turnover at two 1bp legs implies roughly 1.11% annual drag, versus 0.56% shown. This is a parsed-source arithmetic flag; no independent backtest or screenshot validation was completed.

**Assessment:** Different from entry 10’s monthly inverse-variance exposure, but not a new alpha. Existing repository sizing and implied-plus-history experiments already failed to improve the funded plan. Better underlying variance prediction does not prove lower strategy losses or fewer breaches. A daily-close NQ adaptation is conditionally feasible, not exact S&P replication; session endpoints, rolls and full coverage still require verification. SPY_daily.csv’s inspected prices near 2252 in early 2017 do not establish native SPY total-return data merely because of the filename. No failed test is reopened.

Reported maximum timing/cross-sectional Sharpes are .56/.78. These are slide claims with unverified cost treatment, not a complete review of the April 2026 82-page paper. **Assessment:** Potential synthesis of existing carry, positioning and liquidity hypotheses, not established new independent exposure. Keep unnumbered and source-gated.

## Broader non-equity search: no promotion
- **Carbon, SSRN 1621340:** [accepted full manuscript](https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/0100505578/0100505578.pdf). Follow daily buyer-minus-seller initiated trade counts at close, exit next open; threshold variant requires absolute imbalance above 1,000. The 2009 EUA contract study reports 55 threshold trades, 2.03 after modeled spreads versus 6.45 using settlement/open facilities. Unfiltered after-spread result is negative. Text/table capacity scaling conflicts. Full-day signal with same-close fill is unresolved, and no modern holdout exists. Personal overnight reserve, not intraday. Authentic aggressor data and present access are unverified.
- **Temperature, SSRN 1520067:** [2009 full preprint](https://epub.uni-regensburg.de/9816/3/preprint.pdf). Long CDD/short HDD variants hold to expiry using climate-based pricing and probability filters. Actual trading evidence is 2002–06. Average prices 10–20 days before measurement are assumed entries. Margin-return denominator, omitted margin calls and losses exceeding 100% make account-survival claims untenable. No complete execution-cost validation. [JFE 2021 later evidence](https://gwern.net/doc/economics/2021-schlenker.pdf) finds prices incorporate warming and weather information, not a replication of the old trade.
- **Orange juice, SSRN 2080530:** [author 2012 draft](https://florent.mcisaac.fr/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/Orange_Weather.pdf), current-version parity unverified. Rolling temperature-return regressions select one-to-nine-day horizons, but future realized temperatures substitute for supposedly reliable forecasts. No issued-vintage archive; detrending and unfinished performance tables compound the problem. Reject accessible draft as executable evidence, rather than treating distinctness as strength.

## Public noiseband replication: qualified negative evidence only
[Public repository at audited commit](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy/tree/103e33556a8e0673b36694e7a60460a87b67609f), same SSRN 4824172 family, not a fresh entry. Its [ES report](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy/blob/103e33556a8e0673b36694e7a60460a87b67609f/reports/validation_es.md#L21-L79) reports recent weakness and 0.97 SPY/ES return correlation. Raw bars are absent. Shared-engine agreement cannot validate shared assumptions. Source inspection finds start-labelled bars whose close drives same-close fills, OHLC typical-price rather than trade VWAP, and historical adjusted levels incorporating future roll offsets. Numerical impacts were not computed. Protocols disclose prior full-sample baseline knowledge and call results pilots; recorded git ordering is not clean external preregistration. An October 16 half-session phantom-trade fix changes code without updating reports. Thus “feed, costs and stitching ruled out” is too strong. This corroborates low priority, not a verified causal backtest or universal proof that the strategy is dead. No external code was executed.

## Further intraday screens
- **ORB falsification, SSRN 7428398:** [primary report](https://github.com/mulhamfetna/trading-strategy-finder/blob/4bb816df595793c4cf1dfae0d20e834e858ebc58/docs/WS-ORB-REPORT.md). Nine futures, June 2010–August 2026; grid uses prior opening ranges, next-open entries and several stops/exits. Zero of 225 cells passes every criterion, but 39 are net positive and 138 underpowered. Uniform $25 cost and unresolved continuous rolls limit inference. Historical protocol ordering does not make all observed periods untouched. Cash-session variants flatten intraday; Globex variants can span the night. Negative evidence for a known family, not proof all ORBs are impossible.
- **Chinese commodity reversal, SSRN 3069253:** [full accepted manuscript](https://www.academia.edu/89635815/Momentum_and_reversal_strategies_in_Chinese_commodity_futures_markets). Thirty-one markets, May 2013–September 2017. Rank prior J-minute returns, buy three losers/short three winners, rebalance every K minutes, flat daily; highlighted J=K=10. Previous-day liquidity selects contracts. Attractive 3.78% monthly return is gross: commodity fees plus half-tick execution consume intraday profits. Existing US data do not replicate that cross-section. No promotion.
- **Lunch effect, SSRN 4934614:** [full author article](https://quantpedia.com/lunch-effect-in-the-u-s-stock-market-indices/), SSRN PDF unresolved. SPY short 11:00–12:00 Eastern, long 12:00–14:00, based on May 2010–May 2024 hourly history. Hours selected using the same history; no demonstrated net-cost futures holdout. Weak clock-effect reserve, not a qualified new current-data strategy.
- **FX fixing, SSRN 7388124:** newer listing’s independence/full paper unverified. [Established 3521370 accepted manuscript](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/177333/1/WRAP-foreign-exchange-fixings-returns-around-clock-Mueller-2023.pdf), Table X, reports customer-cost combined European legs negative for AUD/GBP. Dealer liquidity provision and gross CME seasonality do not establish accessible futures profits. This window is not entry 38’s separate US-session hypothesis.
- **Other source checks:** Donninger [2488539 full text](https://studylib.net/doc/28206401/ssrn-2488539) is a tuned 2010–14 ES gap/rebound reserve with VIX/VXV inputs and crisis concentration. Gold/silver [2652637 full PDF](https://c.mql5.com/forextsd/forum/174/does_technical_analysis_beat_the_market__evidence_from_high_frequency_trading_in_gold_and_silver.pdf) explores many spot MA rules without verified net costs or daily flattening. [1920401 macro jumps](https://www.academia.edu/18131980/Information_Driven_Price_Jumps_and_Trading_Strategy_Evidence_from_Stock_Index_Futures) trades 10:01–10:02 surprise direction in old full-size futures; reported 15.86–29.27% is cumulative across ten years, not annual. None supplies a strong fresh owned-OHLC-only edge.

## Intraday currency carry: existing S11, deeper source review
[SSRN 5230768](https://ssrn.com/abstract=5230768). Inspected [November 2024 Cambridge draft](https://www.jbs.cam.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/uncovered-interest-parity-in-high-frequency.pdf), 51 pages, not the latest 46-page April 2025 version. G9/USD midquotes over 1999–2023, with short-dated and one-month forwards. Intraday is 07:00–17:00 Eastern; interest accrues at 17:00 rollover. Traditional carry weights are linear deviations of forward premiums from their cross-sectional mean, not tercile ranks. Table II reports intraday 3.44% annual return, t=2.83. Table IV announcement/non-announcement contributions are close-to-close, not intraday event-only performance. No net strategy costs or frozen holdout established. Return-sign expressions and portfolio normalization need reconciliation before coding.

**Assessment:** Already S11 in DATA_NEEDED and the October 30 diversification review. Better source understanding, not a new discovery. Scheduled event calendars can be causal, but combining the time and event decompositions is a new strategy. Exact 17:00 Eastern exit equals 16:00 Central, after the repository's stated 15:59 flatten cutoff; an earlier exit is an adaptation. A 6E series cannot replace G9 spot/forward cashflows. Distinct from entry 38's Breedon–Ranaldo session flows, entry 23's between-meeting policy carry and the failed fixing reversal. Latest-version access, signal clock, normalized weights, data rights and executable quotes remain gates.

## Two simple-price reserves: insufficient novelty/validation
- **Correlated stress reversal, SSRN 5235835 / 5400407:** [complete author article](https://quantpedia.com/short-term-correlated-stress-reversal-trading/). Daily ETF pairs identify joint risk-asset declines or rising IEF with falling SPY/GLD/USO. Buy SPY at that close and exit next close; thresholds and asset choice are optimized within 2004/06–2025 history. No untouched holdout or complete execution-cost evidence established. Same-close information/fill needs a lag. Personal overnight hypothesis, not an intraday or current-data-qualified addition.
- **Two-year Treasury momentum, SSRN 6344979:** [complete author article](https://quantpedia.com/2-year-notes-momentum-extracting-term-structure-anomalies-from-fomc-cycles/). Monthly ROC/SMA variants over ratio-adjusted two-year futures, July 1990–January 2026, with multiple horizons and directional variants. Explicitly gross before costs. No untouched holdout shown. Article's changing-direction “long-short” is not market neutral; policy-cycle narrative does not establish a new cause of trend profits. Existing diversified trend and yield-curve momentum cover the family. No verified owned two-year contract history or live capital accounting.

## Further source and duplicate checks
### Duration rebalancing, SSRN 7601879
Already D26 and an existing scout record, not a new slot. Latest full paper remains unavailable. [Bloomberg methodology](https://data.bloomberglp.com/professional/sites/27/Fixed-Income-Index-Methodology.pdf), pp.45–46/80, establishes that the US Treasury snapshot shifted from 15:00 to 16:00 New York on January 14, 2021; early-close exceptions also vary. [NY Fed evidence](https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/09/treasury-trading-at-the-close/) documents greater month-end closing activity, not directional net profit. Existing repository ZN month-end tests lost after costs; their post-2021 13:59 Central exit precedes the actual new index snapshot by an hour. That mismatch does not authorize a fitted rescue. Existing feasibility records contain incomplete historical extension forecasts and unresolved receipt times; November 2020 endpoint selection was also problematic. Keep source/data-gated.

### Short-horizon commodity reversal, SSRN 6425598
Already a November 2 metadata-only scout record, with zero admitted trials. [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6425598) abstract distinguishes next-week reversal after speculator net trading from continuation in trading-orthogonal returns, especially under low volatility. Full source could not be obtained; conference access was restricted. Do not equate its actor-level signal with public COT or price-only residuals. Universe, regression fit, costs, holding rules and holdout remain unverified. Existing D50's universe belongs to another paper. No new count or intraday promotion.

### Commodity machine learning: lineage and causal-training gates
[Full July 2025 image-pricing manuscript](https://www.cfrn.com.cn/uploads/master/paper/20250710/686fbed4a9107.pdf), related to SSRN 5345159, uses 25 Datastream commodities, January 1973–January 2024, monthly extreme-quartile portfolios and future-characteristic supervised images. No dated train/validation/test schedule, retraining protocol or complete cost ledger was located. Equation 3, Figure 3 and Appendix A1 disagree on carry ratios/logs and sign. Non-overlapping labels alone do not prove causal training. Newer [7600983](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7600983) emphasizes two curve networks rather than the older broader set; latest full text remains unavailable, so older results cannot be assigned to it. [Expected Skewness 5368383](https://ssrn.com/abstract=5368383), [published record](https://scholars.cityu.edu.hk/en/publications/expected-skewness-forecast-combination-and-commodity-futures-retu/), has no recovered complete source: fit windows, forecast combination and positioning-release clocks remain gates. These overlap carry/curve/asymmetry families and need unavailable broad inputs. Keep unnumbered.

### Cross-market overnight time-series momentum
SSRN 4651331, 4765613 and 4978089 are related versions, not three distinct discoveries. [Published 2025 article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1042443125001295) describes US final-half-hour returns predicting foreign-market opening-half-hour returns across developed indices. Complete sample, costs and model-selection tables remain unavailable. A foreign opening interval is not its overnight opening gap. Build would require exact session calendars, latest completed US signals, tradable listing/FX/borrow assumptions and causal conditioning variables. Existing ES/NQ does not replace the foreign panel. Potential intraday holding, but no verified current-data candidate.

### Gold/silver session timing, SSRN 7257240
Full source unresolved. Public description uses hourly spot XAU/XAG in July 2024–August 2026 and fixed UTC sessions. US momentum is weak; favorable Asia/Europe variants depend on selected sessions and a short history. Hourly bars do not directly resolve a 14:30 UTC boundary, fixed UTC differs from DST market clocks, and Asian exposure can cross Pacific midnight. Spot evidence does not establish GC futures costs or eligible instruments. No promotion.

### Threshold session comovement, SSRN 4946188
[SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4946188), [published version](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10293523.2025.2503100). Full 2024/final text was not recovered. [March 2023 author precursor slides](https://jiwon-jung.github.io/files/Overnight_trading___AMS_2023_Sectional_meeting_slides.pdf) model SPY intraday against following FXI overnight returns, using threshold-event counts and Hawkes intensity; trading development was then future work. Both ETFs trade in the US, despite different home-market exposures. These modeling thresholds cannot be relabeled as verified final trading thresholds. SPY close information cannot automatically obtain simultaneous FXI closing fills. Owned ES/NQ cannot reproduce FXI. Source-gated personal ETF reserve, not demonstrated intraday strategy; existing cross-asset failures remain intact.
