## Treasury auctions: exact repository duplicate
[SSRN 6447502](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6447502), [official October 2026 full paper](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1188.pdf), §§3–4 and Tables 2–3: 1,684 cash-Treasury auctions, June 1991–July 2024, GovPX/BrokerTec minute data. Measure 180 minutes before auction close and after actual results release, excluding the announcement jump. Recent 2015–24 combined pressure is 0.631 yield bp for 10-year notes (10% significance), 0.906 for five-year (1%). No net futures strategy or frozen trading holdout is established. Same-window order flow explains outcomes, not an advance signal.

**Decision:** Do not add a new slot. [TIER_LIST](https://github.com/Dartdark/thebeginning/blob/codex/work/TIER_LIST.md#L165) already names this exact paper under D. Its recorded ZN 2018–26 calendar adaptation has pre-leg gross $15.5 versus $38.50 cost, no post-leg rebound, and both legs net −$63/day with 0/9 positive years. These are prior repository results, not new tests. The updated source figure modestly revises the older approximate 0.59bp; fixed 12:01 Central entry differs from actual historical result-release alignment, but neither distinction makes this a fresh discovery. Entry 21’s pre-refunding event is separate.

## Forecasting and Managing Volatility: known risk-method family
[SSRN 5013407](https://ssrn.com/abstract=5013407), [full JOIM 2025 publication](https://www.joim.com/wp-content/uploads/emember/downloads/p0776.pdf), pp.4–21. Allocate equity weight min(10%/forecast annual volatility, 1.5), remainder bills. Daily squared-return forecasts use 5/21 days; exponential smoothing uses .94 decay. Intraday measures use five-minute returns, with full realized variance adding the overnight gap. Evaluation August 2006–November 2023 emphasizes rolling-volatility target error, not alpha. The paper compares forecast lag and slower rebalancing. Exhibit 7 turnover and Appendix A.6's stated two-leg costs need reconciliation: 22% daily turnover at two 1bp legs implies roughly 1.11% annual drag, versus 0.56% shown. This is a parsed-source arithmetic flag; no independent backtest or screenshot validation was completed.

**Assessment:** Different from entry 10’s monthly inverse-variance exposure, but not a new alpha. Existing repository sizing and implied-plus-history experiments already failed to improve the funded plan. Better underlying variance prediction does not prove lower strategy losses or fewer breaches. A daily-close NQ adaptation is conditionally feasible, not exact S&P replication; session endpoints, rolls and full coverage still require verification. SPY_daily.csv’s inspected prices near 2252 in early 2017 do not establish native SPY total-return data merely because of the filename. No failed test is reopened.
