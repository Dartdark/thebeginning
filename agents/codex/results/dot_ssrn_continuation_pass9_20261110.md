## 47. Analyst forecasts before natural-gas storage announcements
**SSRN [2826684](https://ssrn.com/abstract=2826684), Gu/Kurov, JFM 2018.** [Publication](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/fut.21926), [WVU authorship confirmation](https://business.wvu.edu/academics/finance-department/finance-ph-d/finance-ph-d-alumni/chen-gu), [institutional dissertation record](https://researchrepository.wvu.edu/etd/8184/). Reviewed indexed March 25, 2018 [manuscript](https://assets.super.so/e46b77e7-ee08-445e-b43f-4ffd88ae0a0e/files/d9774aab-b384-4536-8ca4-6e5b87e3462f.pdf); direct download 403 and hosting-account ownership unverified. [Earlier manuscript](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/308561504_What_Drives_Informed_Trading_Before_Public_Releases_Evidence_from_Natural_Gas_Inventory_Announcements) identifies Kurov as uploader.

Signal is median of Bloomberg top-three analysts minus overall consensus; ranks use 24-month accuracy lagged one week. Negative buys nearby NG, positive shorts, zero skips. Entry release −90 minutes, exit −5; roll three trading days before expiry. July 2008–January 2016: 334 observations after 53 petroleum-overlap exclusions, 271 trades, gross Sharpe .84; 153 extreme-quartile events yield 1.26. Costs absent; extreme thresholds use full sample. Expanding forecast tests start 2010/2012/2014, but are not modern net-trading holdouts. 2016 source says forecasts close two hours before release; 2018 establishes only prerelease availability, not availability by entry.

**Build gate:** Verify contributor forecasts and prior-week rankings timestamped before the actual entry; use contemporaneous event schedules, native contracts, quotes/costs and independent postpublication evidence. Do not substitute actual storage surprises, revised EIA history or forecasts collected later that morning.

**Classification:** Personal research; **normal 06:00–07:25 Pacific same-day intraday timing fits**, not blanket Apex certification. Current-owned-data: **No**. [Existing NG intake](https://github.com/Dartdark/thebeginning/blob/codex/work/agents/codex/results/ng_corrected_intake_20261031.md) reports 2,959,522 bars 2017–2026, but clock/feed/roll/cost metadata remain unresolved, and no Bloomberg vintage panel is verified. This is prior intake evidence, not a new raw-row audit.

[EIA schedule](https://ir.eia.gov/ngs/schedule.html) has day/time exceptions; do not hard-code Thursday. [EIA revisions](https://ir.eia.gov/ngs/revisions.html) mean latest historical storage is not a forecast-vintage substitute. [Bloomberg product description](https://professional.bloomberg.com/products/bloomberg-terminal/research/economics/) establishes functionality, not matching export rights or user access. [MNG launched November 2023](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/faqs/micro-henry-hub-natural-gas-futures-and-options-frequently-asked-questions.html); scaling historical NG returns does not establish micro execution.

**Dedup:** No exact match in inspected codex/work TIER_LIST/MINED_INDEX. Distinct from unconditional 3575861. Important correction: [3575861 full review](https://github.com/Dartdark/thebeginning/blob/codex/work/agents/codex/results/ng_3575861_full_review_20261030.md) supersedes older source-blocked notes; later bid/ask-net −.90%/Sharpe −.16 closed that rule. ## Price-only intraday studies that do not supply a new strategy
- **SSRN 1107590, same-clock stock continuation:** [full primary paper](https://www.bauer.uh.edu/departments/finance/documents/Heston_Korajczyk_Sadka_paper_UH.pdf), § II.B/Table VII, explicitly negative after bid/ask costs across size/time groups. Different from Gao, but useful execution timing rather than standalone futures evidence.
- **SSRN 5039009, End-of-Day Reversal:** [April 2025 full paper](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/EOD.pdf), §§ 3.2/3.5. Rank individual stocks' prior-close-to 15:00 ET returns; buy losers/sell winners 15:30–16:00. Gross value/equal-weight means 3.78/6.86 bp/day; executable cost-efficient strategy left for future work. Index-futures fade is not replication.
- **November 6, 2026 countercheck:** [arXiv 2611.09286 full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09286), Tables 2–3. Preregistered sample excluding previously studied heavily traded stocks retains 3.05 bp in 2023–October 2026, concentrated in 2023. Full-index diagnostic only .74 bp, t .55 for 2023–2026 and .62 bp, t .66 for 2020–2026. Neither is net trading P&L; no preregistered mechanism passes.
- **SSRN 6918818, Gann ES:** full source 403; anchor confirmation, normalization, overlap, costs and Sharpe denominator unverified. Abstract's 5.03 does not qualify it. Low-priority source gate.
- **SSRN 4731947, Intraday Price Pressures:** October 2026 revision now reports 7.97% residual versus 5.34% raw reversal, unlike old 162.3% headline. Current full methods unavailable; stock factor cross-section not owned futures data.
- **SSRN 7217599, One-Minute Edge:** abstract claims statistical MNQ→QQQ prediction, not executable net alpha; removing freshest lag destroys development advantage. Not promoted.
- **Gold:** 6650958 exactly duplicates existing simulated Bhatti VWAP/EMA study; 7608245's abstract rejects SMC timing; 6941458 has short spot sample/unresolved tick attribution and unavailable advertised repository. No verified GC equivalent.

## Network momentum: full method, weak implementation case
**SSRN [4540651](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4540651)**; reviewed [August 2023 arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.11294v1), not byte-matched November SSRN revision.

64 Pinnacle ratio-adjusted futures, 1990–2022. Eight normalized return/MACD features; symmetric nonnegative graph from similarity, averaged 252–1260 day windows. Neighbor features feed pooled OLS; sign positions, inverse-vol sizing. Five-year expanding retraining, first 1990–99 training and 2000–04 test; last 10% training tunes graph. Reported OOS 2000–22, gross Sharpe 1.363 or 1.511 after scaling. Cost sweep is positive through 3 bp but −.67 at 5 bp. Statistical similarity is not an identified causal supply chain. Common formation/fill clock is not verified.

**Independent caution:** [Quantitativo's corrected replication](https://www.quantitativo.com/p/network-momentum) reduces its headline ≈ 2.12 to .97 after restricting to 48 US-session contracts to address asynchronous-close lookahead; own-momentum control 1.07. It did not rerun original Pinnacle data, so original is not proved invalid. [Another adaptation](https://market-graphs.com/praxis/papers/361154) reports .62 versus plain trend .80 on a different 40-contract 2020–2026 sample; not independently reproduced here. [Pinnacle availability](https://pinnacledata2.com/clc.html) is paid-product documentation, not user entitlement or redistribution permission. Personal overnight, no current-data qualification; retain as audit, not priority.
