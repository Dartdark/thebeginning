## Four paper questions

1. **About:** Cieslak,Morse,Vissing-Jorgensen, *Stock Returns over the FOMC Cycle*, SSRN2687614.
   Read working manuscript dated2018-02-12, not asserted identical to the2019Journal of Finance article.
2. **Findings — authors:** Table1 reports the equity premium concentrated in even cycle weeks in1994–2016;
   weeks2/4/6 dummy10.9bp(t3.24),17.6bp(t2.67) in2014–16. Figure4/Table7 strengthens the association after declines.
   Table1C even-week holding Sharpe0.92 versus0.45unconditional, before a verified modern implementation here.
3. **Why money:** Authors propose unexpectedly accommodating Fed communication and lower downside-risk premia
   on a biweekly information cycle. Causality, persistence and executable futures net edge are not independently verified.
4. **Build on it:** Test a public past-event phase signal using ES RTH data. This deliberately omits overnight returns
   and week0. It can fail even if the authors' full-day historical finding is correct. No private policy/news feed is used.
