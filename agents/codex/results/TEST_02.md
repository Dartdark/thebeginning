## Intrabar look-ahead: no, under the specified calculation mode

**The supplied code does not intrinsically use intrabar look-ahead.** With IOG false and normal close-only calculations, High, Low, Close and Volume refer to the decision bar after it completes. The buy is for the following opening tick. It does not buy at an earlier price inside the decision bar using its final High/Low/Close, and it does not reference a negative/future index. The current-bar intraday ATR is wrong for the daily rule, but its observations are known at that close.

Entry-bar exposure, an incorrect ATR horizon, volume semantics, missing holidays and price-path fill assumptions are different defects from look-ahead. Bar Magnifier supplies finer historical paths to the fill emulator; it does not by itself enable intrabar signal generation. Extra order-fill recalculations or changing IOG would require a new review of accumulator updates; the correction explicitly preserves close-only updates.
