## 1. What is it about?

A daily cash-equity allocation system trains PPO, A2C and DDPG agents. A growing training window is followed by
three months of validation; the highest-validation-Sharpe agent trades the following quarter. This selects one
policy rather than averaging three policies. The state includes cash, stock holdings, adjusted prices and
MACD/RSI/CCI/ADX for 30 Dow stocks. A turbulence threshold liquidates positions and pauses purchases.

The portfolio is long-only: selling held shares does not establish a short strategy. Modeled fees are 0.1% of
purchase/sale value, with instant closing-price execution assumed. These are stock/share units, not MNQ/MES costs.

## 3. Why might it make money?

The proposed mechanism is learned equity exposure/weighting, quarterly selection and cash withdrawal during
turbulence. No independently measured forced flow is identified. Stock-market drift, ordinary indicators and
the risk overlay can account for returns; the experiment does not isolate RL's incremental benefit over matched
passive allocations, simple timing policies and the same turbulence rule. Adaptive training itself is not proof
of a tradable edge. Updates using only matured past information can be causal; online retraining is not by itself leakage.

### Additional printed-accounting check

I visually checked uploaded p. 3: Equations 6–9 use old holdings in their claimed buy/sell reward expansion.
In the script's invented one-stock example, old holdings are zero, one share is bought at $100, its next mark is
$110, and the 0.1% entry fee is $0.10. Equation 4's NAV change is **+$9.90**; the printed Equations 6/9 give
**-$0.10**. The decomposition therefore cannot be implemented literally as the stated NAV reward.
This is a printed-equation inconsistency, not evidence that an unavailable runtime used that expansion.

Also, using today's closing indicators to fill at today's close needs a verified order/auction protocol. A turbulence
statistic must use only available observations and fold-local mean/covariance/threshold estimates. Neither an
adjusted history nor a hypothetical instant close fill is a verified executable price. A corrected engine would
be a separately specified experiment, not an exact replication claimed by assumption.

The four Apex homes cannot trade the source daily stock basket as written. There is no earned NQ intraday
conversion, personal allocation, Monte Carlo or BASE/ADD/REPLACE/EMPTY result. Smallest later step only with a
clarified source/protocol: reconcile a causal feature/decision/later-fill/cash/share trace, then the universe,
corporate actions, turbulence and seed/checkpoint rules before any new bounded return preregistration.
