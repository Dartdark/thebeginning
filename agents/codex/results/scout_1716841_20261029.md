# Goldman roll: DON'T TEST the published livestock protocol as a current candidate

Summary: The full source weakens its own attractive headline: livestock performance turns negative from 2008.
The published trade holds two delivery months across days and omits measured transaction costs.
No modern replication or account result was produced; no trial was spent.

## Four questions

1. **About:** Yiqun Mou studies scheduled commodity-index maturity rolls using calendar spreads. The position sells the index-held month and buys its destination month before the roll.
2. **Findings:** In the July 15, 2011 version, Table IV A reports livestock Strategy 1 annual excess return 4.99%, Sharpe 1.67 for 2000–March 2010. Section II.C, printed p.21, says returns declined toward zero after 2006 and livestock averages became negative from 2008. Section II.B, p.17, explicitly does not measure transaction costs. The abstract's maximum 4.39 Sharpe uses active-period annualization, not calendar-year capital availability.
3. **Why money:** The proposed mechanism is predictable index sales of the old month and purchases of the new month against limited arbitrage capital. Crowding and changing index designs can remove it.
4. **Build on it:** Retain the failure/crowding evidence; do not nominate a modern livestock trade from the old aggregate result. The published overnight protocol is incompatible with our Apex intraday restriction. A personal-account study would still need independently justified current flow, exact contracts and costs.
