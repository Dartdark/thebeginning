# dot: independent validation and version audit
Author: dot. 2026-11-10. Companion to the 41-entry SSRN index; this does not add 41 viable strategies or reopen tests.

## Summary
- Commodity curve hypotheses remain interesting, but later implementations materially weaken the historical headline-return case.
- Commercial-position flow has later statistical support, without a demonstrated executable release-timed net portfolio.
- FX session evidence does not establish modern profitable 6E execution.
- Business-cycle FX evidence remains 2019/2020-era, despite a 2026 SSRN metadata date.
- No returns were computed and no strategy was promoted.

## Basis Momentum and Relative Basis: implementation sensitivity
**Fan and Zhang, Commodity Premia and Risk Management, JFM 2024.** [Full publication](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/fut.22507), [institutional PDF](https://research-repository.griffith.edu.au/bitstreams/faa37914-895f-405d-847a-5d305455b127/download). SSRN identity not verified; included as independent validation, not an additional SSRN slot.

Source findings: § 2/Appendix A/Table 4 use 29 commodities, April 1982–September 2022, monthly equal-weight long/short halves. BM uses first-minus-second-nearby compounded 12-month returns; RB uses near minus far maturity-scaled log slopes. Traded outright contracts follow maximum OI, with the next maturity leading for three days before rolling. Gross-to-net annual BM is 2.6%→1.7% (net t 2.2, Sharpe .32), RB 2.0%→1.1% (t 1.5, Sharpe .21). Costs assume full 200% monthly turnover, $1.50 plus one tick per transaction. The paper describes dollar neutrality and 100% collateralization without a simple leg-weight equation.

**Independent assessment:** These are not calendar-spread returns, but neither are they unchanged replications: basket, sample, half-sorts and OI contract choice differ from original BM's top/bottom-four and RB's terciles. Do not subtract these figures from original returns and call the difference measured decay, leverage-normalized loss or transaction costs. Their within-paper gross/net difference is the valid cost comparison. Stop-loss improvements are research hypotheses, not proof of account-survival protection.

## Basis Momentum: regime dependence
**Baba-Yara and Bondatti, Commodity Returns: Lost in Financialization, SSRN [4345427](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4345427).** Reviewed [January 2023 EFMA full draft](https://www.efmaefm.org/0EFMAMEETINGS/EFMA%20ANNUAL%20MEETINGS/2023-UK/papers/EFMA%202023_stage-4455_question-Full%20Paper_id-318.pdf). [Author page](https://www.massimilianobondatti.com/research) lists March 2026 revision; latest full text remains unverified.

Source findings: 32 commodities, March 1986–August 2021, standard 12-month BM and top/bottom-four sorting. Outright returns use the second-shortest contract whose first-notice date clears the following month. Table 1 reports gross BM 18.77%/Sharpe .73 before 2004 versus 7.42%/.30 afterward; post 2004 t 1.08. No net-cost deduction. Across the factor panel, postpublication decay loses significance after controlling for the post 2004 period.

**Independent assessment:** Strong reason not to treat original BM headline returns as a current expected return. Not an isolated postpublication holdout, not causal proof that financialization caused deterioration, and not the original 21-market basket. Category: Personal evidence audit; no intraday or owned-data qualification.

## Commercial-position flow: support does not fix timing
**A Tale of Two Premiums Revisited, SSRN [3881922](https://ssrn.com/abstract=3881922), published 2023.** [Full journal source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/fut.22396).

Source findings: producer net-trading Q remains significant with fixed effects and disaggregated COT; smoothed hedging pressure AHP does not. Table 7 extension labeled 2007–2020 reports Q 4.78 (t 3.81), AHP −.34 (t −.84). Section 3 uses Tuesday-to-Tuesday returns, without a Friday-release adjustment or net trading-cost portfolio. The data section and extension text disagree about January versus December 2020 ending dates.

**Independent assessment:** Supports the liquidity-flow mechanism over the slow insurance component, not an executable Friday-afternoon strategy. Keep actual publication timestamps and postrelease prices as hard gates. This does not reopen the separately closed gold crowding screen: its 38 entries and 20.4% window coverage failed feasibility minima, not a gold-wide economic hypothesis.

## FX sessions: do not mix windows, instruments or costs
Existing **SSRN [3521370](https://ssrn.com/abstract=3521370)** [final 2024 paper](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jofi.13306) distinguishes fix-linked reversals from local-workday patterns. Its European strategy excludes the ECB-to-London-fix interval. Post-London is 11:00–17:00 ET, not the strict 08:00–16:00 NY rule. Table III's June 2006–December 2019 CME basket mean is .64 bp (t 2.20), using midquotes. Table II's 2014–2019 spot basket mean falls to .86 bp (t 1.62). Table X cost results concern AUD/GBP dealer-client spot, with postfix close 16:00, and negative customer returns.

**Independent assessment:** Gross futures pattern persistence is not net profitability of our same-day 6E hypothesis, while negative spot-customer results do not directly reject that exact futures rule. A 17:00 ET endpoint also fails applicable pre 17:00 flatten requirements without modification. SSRN 3069629 remains abstract-only; newer 3138756 post 2007 evidence remains full-source-gated. Neither is promoted.

## Business-cycle FX: newer timestamp, old evidence
**SSRN [2906600](https://ssrn.com/abstract=2906600)** shows July 2026 revision metadata, but [author research page](https://www.stevenriddiough.com/research) still identifies September 2019. A [December 6, 2019 accepted manuscript](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/bitstreams/a3531904-83e7-47ef-aac8-560c1e4b798c/download) retains December 1999–January 2016 real-time vintages and Table 4 GAP-CS net 3.46%, Sharpe .50 at 50% quoted spreads. [City's version](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/22867/1/BusCycleRisk.pdf) includes the appendix. Exact parity with inaccessible latest SSRN binary is unresolved. No author executable package or underlying dataset located on [Sarno's code page](https://sites.google.com/view/luciosarno/data-and-code).

**Independent assessment:** Preserve its causal-data strengths, but label 2019/2020 evidence, not a verified 2026 update. Manuscript CC BY-NC-ND licensing does not license underlying commercial data or any unlocated code. Personal category; broad vintages/forwards missing.

## Related BM corroboration with an unresolved FX formula issue
**Understanding the Performance of Currency Basis-Momentum, SSRN [4925173](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4925173), EFM 2025.** [Published full text](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/eufm.12555), [university PDF](https://pure.qub.ac.uk/files/636563351/Euro_Fin_Management_-_2025_-_Fan_-_Understanding_the_Performance_of_Currency_Basis_Momentum.pdf).

Appendix E.1/Table E1A: 27 GSCI constituents, December 1984–October 2020, equal-weight monthly terciles; BM12 earns .72% monthly, t 3.14, monthly Sharpe .15. Other horizons are insignificant. Bloomberg nearby prices generate signals; GSCI indices provide returns. Commodity net costs are not established. In the main FX section, printed Eq 6 equates expressions with apparently inconsistent signs; its long-cost formula uses negative forward-bid plus spot-ask, which credits spreads relative to midquotes. The printed PDF was visually checked.

**Independent assessment:** Commodity results corroborate a related implementation, not a matched original replication or independent post 2019 holdout. The FX discrepancy may be notation rather than implemented-code error. Do not declare the backtest disproved, but do not treat its net results as validated without code reconciliation. Personal/overnight; commercial inputs unverified. This supplementary audit is not a new deployable strategy slot.

## Policy slope: independent counterevidence, not a new lead
Neuhierl/Weber's SSRN2748290/2867577/2868930/2895997 are one paper family. Reviewed [January2018 full paper](https://bfi.uchicago.edu/wp-content/uploads/predictability.pdf) and [July2019 full paper](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/conferences/shared/pdf/20191007_mo_po_conf/Monetary_policy_communication_policy_slope_and_the_stock_market.pdf).

Weekly Wednesday changes in three-month fed-funds futures residualized on one-month changes forecast next-week equities; negative slope predicts higher returns. Baseline residualization uses the full sample. Appendix trains both stages on1988–1994 and evaluates1995–2007, OOS R²0.27%; descriptions of rolling versus fixed estimation are inconsistent. Extension after2007 changes tenors to six versus three months. Claimed weekly Sharpe0.0731→0.0900 is an analytical mean-variance calculation, not net trading P&L. Comparing spread against absolute market moves does not establish profitable implementation.

[Moench/Stein's published2019 comment](https://www.emanuelmoench.com/files/papers/Moench_Stein(JMEcomment2019).pdf) uses author-shared data. Slope t-statistics are−0.86 in1994–1998,−6.02 in1999–2001 and−1.24 in2002–2007. Error-difference gains largely occur in the middle period. The speech-tone result depends on two influential speeches and classification choices.

**Assessment:** Same-sample robustness criticism, not a modern replication, but enough to deprioritize the headline. Weekly Personal exposure only; native ZQ contracts and licensed equity data are unverified. Current free CME settlement delivery at midnight CT is not evidence of availability before that day's equity close; licensed contemporaneous feeds are a separate possibility. Do not declare every paper specification noncausal, and do not invent a sign-only ES rule. No tests or changes to prior pre-FOMC experiments.

## Decision impact
The index's leading curve papers are priority **data-and-modern-validation gates**, not immediately buildable recommendations. COT flow is distinct but still needs a release-timed implementation. Read the [native-contract source audit](dot_ssrn_curve_data_feasibility_20261110.md) before treating a continuous series or public price webpage as adequate data. All next steps remain research-only unless separately authorized.

## Author continuation and newer Relative Basis manuscript

The [Boons author page](https://sites.google.com/site/martijnboons/) links [Returns.pdf](https://drive.google.com/file/d/136AzSf2Cle_92OmN3YBj-GXEBDefRBMB/view?usp=sharing), a one-page update described as ending December 2025. Two cumulative-return panels compare basis-momentum, momentum and basis in first-nearby and spreading implementations. The outright BM curve rises strongly after 2022; spreading BM ends below its pre-pandemic high. No out-of-sample cutoff, updated universe, exact construction, costs, leverage, downloadable return series or code accompany the chart. Cost status is undisclosed, not verified gross or net.

**Assessment:** Author-maintained graphical continuation supports persistence, particularly outright. It corrects any blanket claim that no modern evidence exists, but is not independent replication, documented original-rule continuity or frozen prospective net-cost validation. Contrary studies using changed implementations do not invalidate this chart, nor does the chart erase their qualifications.

The [May 2025 Relative Basis draft](https://ifr.hkust.edu.hk/tc/public/uploads/upload/files/20250727/1a5308fcc8f0ab2ad6dd00436509570e.pdf), Tables 4–5 and Appendix A15, retains 24 commodities over January 1979–December 2019. The 0.81% monthly high-minus-low return (t=3.99), 0.685% alpha (t=3.48), and 0.633% alpha with traditional basis controlled (t=3.35) remain. The added BM alpha evidence uses overlapping history. A newer draft date is not newer performance or contemporary net execution validation.
