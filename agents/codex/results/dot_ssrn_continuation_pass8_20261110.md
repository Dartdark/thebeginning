## 44. Industrial metals and state-dependent equity returns
**SSRN [1660864](https://ssrn.com/abstract=1660864), Jacobsen/Marshall/Visaltanachoti.** Reviewed [September 2014 full draft](https://www.academia.edu/166230155/Stock_Market_Predictability_and_Industrial_Metal_Returns), §§ 2–3/Table 2; later July 2016 SSRN version is not matched.

Monthly S&P GSCI industrial-metals futures-price index predicts next-month stocks through a state-interacted regression. Hold S&P when predicted return exceeds prior-month risk-free rate, otherwise cash. CFNAI-MA3 below −.7 defines contraction; metals' predictive sign changes across states. First genuinely realtime forecast is April 2001, respecting actual CFNAI publication delays, sometimes two months. Through June 2013, realtime OOS R² 7.11% and annual certainty-equivalent gain 4.09%; these are not net Sharpe figures. No explicit transaction-cost analysis located.

**Assessment/build gate:** Better causal macro handling than contemporaneous NBER labels. Pin exact newer specification, estimation window, executable equity/ES timing and postpublication validation before coding. Requires the exact metals index and vintage CFNAI panel, not copper alone or revised macro data. Personal monthly allocation overlay, not an independently validated diversifying sleeve. No verified current inputs or intraday translation. Bounded repo checks found no exact implementation; generic cross-asset failures remain untouched.

## 45. Cross-asset timing of separate FX carry legs
**SSRN [2399282](https://ssrn.com/abstract=2399282), Lu/Jacobsen.** IDs 2398102/2560968 are alternate records of one study. Reviewed [February 10, 2016 author-uploaded full draft](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/272248002_Cross-Asset_Return_Predictability_Carry_Trades_Stocks_and_Commodities), §§ 3–4.3.

G10 FX against USD; equal-weight one/two/three highest- and lowest-rate currencies inferred from spot/forward quotes. One-month forwards settle at month-end spot. Three-month-lagged CRB return predicts high-yield long-leg profit; two-month-lagged equity-index return predicts low-yield short-leg profit. Execute a leg only when forecast profit is positive. Expanding regression requires 120 observations; OOS April 1998–December 2015. Costs cross Barclays/Datastream bid–ask spreads. Timed long-leg Sharpes .31–.76; short-leg −.14–.14. Lag choice follows in-sample findings, and no post 2015 validation is established.

**Assessment/build gate:** Transparent and distinct from simply buying carry, but modest results and weak short leg. Personal monthly, not intraday. Broad forward/bid–ask/CRB inputs are not verified owned. A 6E-only or futures substitution changes settlement, carry and universe. Does not establish portfolio independence.

## 46. Crypto Carry: dated-futures convergence
**SSRN [4268371](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4268371), Schmeling/Schrimpf/Todorov.** Reviewed [March 24, 2023 BIS working paper 1087](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry.pdf).

At 28 days before CME BTC expiry, buy spot/short futures for positive basis, reverse both legs for negative basis; hold to expiry. Table 5 combined strategy Sharpe .59 on 30 monthly observations, March 2019–January 2022. Trading costs and financing spreads excluded, financing modeled at LIBOR. No frozen holdout demonstrated. Uses Skew spot/constant-maturity basis and raw CME Bloomberg prices for robustness; constant-maturity indices are not executable contracts. Despite offsetting spot gains, futures margin calls can liquidate the hedge: 10× leverage would have liquidated the futures leg in over half the sampled months.

**Version gate:** [Management Science 2026](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05069) main text was not inspected. Its [public appendix](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/suppl/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05069/suppl_file/mnsc.2024.05069.sm1.pdf) has later samples through July 2024; carry-measure correlations are not realized portfolio-return correlations. [Author summary](https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/crypto-carry-market-segmentation-and-price-distortions-digital-asset-markets) describes basis compression around spot ETFs. Do not call .59 a current journal estimate.

**Assessment/build gate:** Lower-priority Personal capital-intensive relative value, multi-day; no intraday eligibility. Native expiries, executable spot/settlement matching, fees, custody, borrowing, collateral and liquidation paths are indispensable. BTC_1min_2018.csv actually inspected: date/time/OHLC/volume beginning December 17, 2018, no contract/maturity ID. The existing script treats it as rolled history. ETH file columns were not verified. Neither establishes faithful convergence-trade data. No direct carry duplicate found in current repo checks.

## Perpetual funding is a different trade
Christin/Routledge/Soska/Zetlin-Jones, [The Crypto Carry Trade, August 2023 full author-linked paper](https://gerbil.life/papers/CarryTrade.v1.2.pdf), has no verified SSRN ID. Linear version buys spot and shorts equivalent perpetual each eight-hour interval; inverse hedge adjusts notional by funding. 18 coins/36 contracts, August 2020–June 2023. BTC tether headline Sharpe 8.76 falls to 1.312 in May 2022–June 2023; coin-settled 4.93 falls to .007. Retrospective subsamples, no frozen holdout. Costs/margin capital omitted; spot index may not be executable. Bankruptcy can strand the hedge. Those figures must not be attributed to the CME paper.

Current [Binance funding documentation](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/360033525031) calculates funding during the running interval. The paper's known-next-period convention requires historical verification before using a conditional funding filter. Realized funding is not necessarily known at entry.

[Vision Dataset Terms](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md), August 2026, permit personal nonproduction historical research but restrict live proprietary execution under that grant; download-date rights matter. README MIT text is not a blanket data license. [Journal replication gate](https://services.informs.org/dataset/mnsc/download.php?doi=mnsc.2024.05069) requires assent/contact information and restricts use to replication without further permission.
