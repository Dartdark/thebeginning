**What does it find?** The PDF reports1161development trades,t5.10;256OOS1trades,t3.09;604OOS2trades,t1.58.

and small neighboring parameter changes lose. Its later window fails even the ordinary1.96bar as well as its
protocol's4.15threshold. Positive later P&L is not the same as confirmed evidence; failure to reach a threshold
also does not prove the true premium vanished. Rules fixed in advance is an author claim, not an independently
audited preregistration/variant ledger supplied here.

**Why might it make money?** The note explicitly allows a generic equity overnight premium rather than the gold
opening-price mechanism. High win rate is partly an exit-shape result when wins are capped but losses are not.
This review identifies no independently established forced buyer or causal reason for R²'s incremental value.

**Can we build on it?** Yes as a bounded reproduction/adaptation question; the user authorized and we completed
one. It has positive design merits and remains unverified independently, but fails advancement gates. Existing
weak Overnight Drift/broad-night-long results and failed sentiment-filter work remain prior negative evidence.

## What could make this wrong

Our R², sample windows, roll guard, reentry trigger, target reset and order timing are disclosed assumptions.
The PDF's page4withholds its selected filters and exits; its exact860OOStrades cannot be reproduced from this note.
Five-minute target execution is replayed with one-minute paths and1pointthrough; there are no queue/quote fills.
Charging market slippage without moving the reference target is a conservative reference-price convention,
not a claim of exact actual-fill-anchored profit targets. Fixed25pointwins with uncapped time exits can become
less attractive as nominal volatility rises; that is an untested structural concern, not a fitted explanation here.
Missing-session handling and actual contract rolls need raw identity/path audits before any account simulation.
