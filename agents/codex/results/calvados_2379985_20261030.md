## Rule (as tested)

**What is it about?** Chrilly Donninger's *VIX Futures Basis Trading: The Calvados-Strategy 2.0*,
January 2014, Revision 1 dated January 21, studies shorting individual VIX futures whose premium to spot is large
relative to their recent volatility and remaining maturity. The preferred version adds a short ES hedge.
It is a multiday volatility-risk-premium trade, not an intraday NQ entry rule.
[Uploaded source](../inputs/calvados_2379985_20261030/paper.pdf), [text](../inputs/calvados_2379985_20261030/paper.txt).

**What does it find?** For January 12, 2010–January 17, 2014, the selected RMS-normalized rule reportedly returns
**460.3% unhedged with 16.7% maximum drawdown**, or **273.7% hedged with 11.3% drawdown**.
These are the author's gross, selected, leveraged results on an initial $500,000, not our reproduction.
The author explicitly excludes costs. Median smoothing and long-volatility reversal generally worsened results.
The short overlapping revision period is not independent validation. Our later fixed-one-VX component loses money.

**Why does it make money, if it does?** The proposed mechanism is compensation for supplying volatility/crash
insurance: futures can trade above spot and decline as the premium converges. Spot VIX cannot be bought and carried
like a stock; this is not a locked-in arbitrage. The source cites basis predictability and reports selected simulations,
but does not measure forced order flow. Short ES can offset some ordinary equity sensitivity of short VX;
it cannot eliminate nonlinear volatility jumps, overnight gaps or margin calls.

**Can we build on it?** We obtained actual dated VX histories and completed the cheap unhedged screen below.
It fails, so this component earns no historical-validation or account stage. The preferred ES-hedged version is
separate and untested: it still requires dated ES prices, executable rolls and a frozen integer hedge/rebalance policy.
Only a conditional personal-account research lead remains. The multiday source rule does not fit any of our four
intraday Apex plans as written. No NQ, VIXY or continuous-contract proxy was used to manufacture an answer.

### Frozen component

- Fixed one full VX contract, multiplier $1,000 per VIX point; only monthly expiry identities.
- After both same-date VIX close and confirmed VX settlement, compute
  `R = (VX settle - VIX close)/(RMS10 daily VX price changes * trading days to settlement)`.
  RMS uses ten squared same-contract differences without demeaning, not a sample standard deviation.
- While flat, select the largest R among contracts with 15–93 trading days remaining, ties earlier expiry;
  short if R > 0.12. The 15-day entry minimum is visible in the source figures, absent from the prose.
- Keep that contract until its own R < 0.10 or maturity < 10 trading days. No continuous best-contract switching.
  Cover and any eligible replacement occur at the following session's recorded Open, each separately costed.
- Start flat January 2, 2018. Liquidate at December 30, 2022 trade-date Open, generally December 29 evening CT
  under GTH naming; remain flat through final settlement. No later liquidation price enters this test.
- December 5, 2018: execute pending orders, mark held VX, suppress fresh spot-dependent decisions because spot
  is missing, and retain known-calendar exits. No stale VIX forward-fill.
- No ES hedge, price stop, source leverage, compounding, fractional contracts or micro backfill.

Fees are an assumed **$5 per fill**; slippage is **one 0.05-point VX outright tick, $50 per fill**.
Primary round trip $110; stress uses two ticks per fill, $210 round trip. This is not the NQ fee schedule.
Recorded daily Opens are later-session research reference prices; native executable opening fills are unverified.

## What could make this wrong (data, fills, look-ahead, multiple testing)

- **Source selection:** five normalization families, thresholds, median filters, hedge coefficients, long/short choices,
  leverage and revised sizing were explored. The total search count is unknown. Author-selected returns are not a
  statistical promotion gate. The 2010–14 period excludes 2008 and all crises after January 2014.
- **Equation error:** Eq7 implies the hedge coefficient `b1 + b2*TTS`; printed Eq8 instead multiplies `b1*b2*TTS`.
  PDF-image inspection confirms a source typo, not an OCR repair. With `b1 = −0.6, b2 = 0.006`, source discussion
  and sign crossover support the additive interpretation. Signed short-VX/short-ES synthetic math checks pass;
  they do not validate a hedged strategy. Daily/infrequent adjustment thresholds and integer rounding remain unspecified.
- **Inputs:** 66 monthly dated VX files from a pinned public archival copy, 4,740 eligible candidate rows, complete
  warm-up and next-session Opens. No continuous futures or synthetic second maturity. Far-dated invalid raw rows
  remain unchanged and outside the registered eligibility window; eligible and held price windows pass metadata checks.
  Original Cboe-byte equality, revision history and exact session-Open convention remain unverified. Raw data is
  scratch-only; acquisition URLs, hashes and receipts are committed. See [acquisition](../inputs/calvados_2379985_20261030/acquisition.md).
- **Actual ES acquisition follow-up:** [dated ES feasibility](../inputs/calvados_2379985_20261030/es_hedge_gate/es_hedge_feasibility.md)
  records owned-file checks, official CME/DataMine attempts, vendor access limits and successful public archive reads.
  A pinned public archive advertises all 21 quarterly ES identities needed through ESH2023. Three complete samples
  have coherent OHLC and overlapping contract intervals. Thus dated ES files were found; they are not yet admitted
  hedge inputs. Vendor, trade-versus-quote price type and timezone/bar convention remain undocumented, official
  settlement fields are absent, and a complete paired-roll/required-clock census was not established. Sample comparisons
  strongly favor UTC start labels advanced one minute against our CT close labels, but residual mismatches remain.
  This evidence supports further metadata investigation, not silent clock certification or a hedge return claim.
  [Reproducible metadata script](../../../analysis/codex_calvados_es_metadata_audit_20261030.py). No raw ES files are redistributed.
- **Calendar/clock:** actual filenames retain Tuesday holiday-adjusted expiries. TTS counts CFE dates strictly after
  the decision through expiry inclusive, including the exceptional December 5, 2018 shortened VX session. A frozen
  secondary calendar with that primary correction supplies dates only. Cboe moved settlement reference 15:15→15:00 CT
  effective October 26, 2020; signals wait for both confirmed settlement and spot, then fill a later session.
- **Execution/tails:** daily settlement marks miss intraday adverse excursions. Daily Open plus assumed ticks does
  not establish executable fills, spread capacity, variation margin, broker eligibility or survivability. One full VX
  is large relative to the micro portfolio; no prelaunch VXM history is invented. The source's 60% sizing convention
  is not a 60% maximum loss or margin requirement.
- **Implementation adaptations:** next-session Open, fixed one VX, monthly-only universe, held-contract persistence,
  missing-spot handling and terminal liquidation are frozen interpretations, not exact source reproduction. A further
  source hedge test needs its own completed data gate and committed protocol. There was no threshold, stop or hedge
  rescue after seeing this failure. Independent Claude verification remains pending.
