## QA findings and limits

The acquisition script validates all history pagination/row totals, byte identities, ordered unique dates and numeric
fields. Five histories have no nonpositive NAV/shares/assets and no identity error above 1%. Their maximum absolute
relative errors are UGL 0.0010%, GLL 0.2573%, UCO/SCO 0.0140% and KOLD 0.1289%.

**BOIL is not admitted.** There are 1,178 identity mismatches above 1%, 82 zero-share rows and 40 flagged 2018–22 rows.
The secondary collector explicitly rounds `Shares Outstanding (000) ×1000` to a whole share. This can destroy precision
when historical split-restated share counts are small; the original sponsor CSV is required to locate the full source
of the problem. No original-file error is established, and no inferred shares/assets repair was applied.
Flagged rows are saved separately; the entire BOIL dataset remains quarantined even where an individual row passes.

Primary data comparison is still required for every fund/index. The source's split-adjustment assertions cannot be
accepted solely because NAV×shares agrees: both fields can be co-adjusted. AUM history is not a timestamped intraday
net-flow record. The historical availability/revision clocks, mandate changes, benchmark and contract/roll weights,
and actual creations/redemptions remain missing. Current UCO/SCO benchmark is a balanced WTI index, not a certified
continuous front-month CL exposure. Current holdings must not be projected backwards.

Current [UGL objective](https://www.proshares.com/our-etfs/leveraged-and-inverse/ugl) supports investigating a daily
leveraged gold-futures mechanism, but no price predictability has been tested. This does not rescue the failed NQ
Late-Day Flow sizing rule or imply that fund exposure adjustment is unoffset forced futures demand.

VVIX/SKEW repeated OHLC values are source-cache placeholders; close-only files prevent accidentally using them as
intraday bars. VIX9D launched October 2013; earlier observations are back-calculated
([Cboe methodology](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/indices/governance/Volatility_Index_Methodology_Selected_SPX_Target_Expected_Volatility_Term_Indices.pdf)).
None of these indices supplies signed dealer positions or native VX futures term structure, and daily archives do
not resolve pre-close availability. No publication timestamp has been invented; `available_at` stays blank.
