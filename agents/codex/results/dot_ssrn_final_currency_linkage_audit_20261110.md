# dot: final currency-linkage source check
Author: dot. November 10, 2026. Unnumbered exclusion; no new qualified count.

[SSRN 4564504](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4564504), Jeanneret and Sokolovski, *Looking Under the Hood of Commodity Currency Predictability*. [Management Science publication](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2024.07249), October 23, 2026. Riddiough is a discussant, not an author.

[Current 47-page appendix](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/suppl/10.1287/mnsc.2024.07249/suppl_file/mnsc.2024.07249.sm1.pdf), dated May 29, 2026, supplies monthly one-month FX forecasting tests. Expanding OLS starts with 24 observations and sets coefficient signs inconsistent with contemporaneous commodity beta to zero. January 1985–April 2020 sample. Table A.7 has negative OOS R² for all five importer currencies. Table A.11 includes bid/ask costs in existing carry-return predictive regressions: commodity-price coefficient .337 (SE .130) falls to insignificant .189 (.115) after excluding six least-traded commodity currencies. This is not verified net OOS P&L of a newly managed trading strategy.

[September 2023 full predecessor](https://www.wu.ac.at/fileadmin/wu/d/ri/isk/VSFX_2021/Papers/20230910_Commodity_Prices_and_Currencies_Jeanneret_Sokolovski_SSRNlink.pdf) identifies WM/Reuters spot and one-month forwards through Datastream and IMF commodity terms-of-trade indices, with lagged three-year export weights. Revised-paper parity is not assumed.

Assessment: country-specific commodity prices are conceptually different from trade-network and volume signals, but liquidity sensitivity and missing verified net implementation prevent promotion. A 2026 publication date is not a modern trading sample. Need current main-paper portfolio construction, point-in-time universe and index vintages, historical receipts, lawful data and actual execution.
