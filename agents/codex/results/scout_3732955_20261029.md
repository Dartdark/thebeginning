## What is it about?
Closing-auction price impact, resiliency and differences between Nasdaq and NYSE.

## What does it find?
The current author page describes greater NYSE depth and a temporary impact component dissipating over3–5days,
with profitable source strategies. Those claims are unverified here. The older “Information Content and Timeliness”
title and earlier Wu-only SSRN3440239 are related-version leads, not separate independent discoveries.
Do not combine numbers or rules from an older summary with the current article without a source-version audit.

## Why does it make money, if it does?
Potential compensation for absorbing mandatory benchmark demand, and subsequent relaxation of that pressure.
We have not verified whether the return sorts use a contemporaneously published imbalance, a final auction
quantity, historical short availability, or an entry feasible after the observation. These details decide tradability.

## Can we build on it?
A source-parity audit could support either an intraday pressure rule or a multi-day personal basket.
A3–5day equity reversal is incompatible with Apex intraday rules as written. An intraday futures adaptation
would require evidence of transmission and its own frozen protocol, not an assumed transfer of stock alpha.
