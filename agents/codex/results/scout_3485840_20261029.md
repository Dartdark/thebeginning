## What is it about?
Closing-auction participation, temporary price pressure and the interpretation of official closing prices.

## What does it find?
The2010–2018 stock sample's mean absolute auction deviation is8.12bp, versus0.93bp for the capital-weighted
aggregate absolute deviation (printed pp.13–15). The ETF appendix reports QQQ/SPY deviations of1.68/1.83bp.
Section3.4 finds rapid but incomplete after-hours reversal among stocks actually trading then.
These are source statistics, not expected strategy profits. The differences in units/universe matter.

## Why does it make money, if it does?
The proposed compensation is supplying liquidity to benchmark-sensitive investors while accepting execution
uncertainty and inventory risk. Its regression return starts at the auction print, which is not a guaranteed
entry for someone who first observes that final print. The after-hours result uses interval VWAPs and excludes
missing trades; neither is a guaranteed market-order fill.

## Can we build on it?
Yes, as support for a separately timed real-signal test: pre-auction imbalance known before an auction order,
or a post-auction entry at a later executable quote. Do not splice those into one backtest or give the later
entry the already-completed auction price. A cross-stock long/short result need not reach NQ/ES; a personal
stock/ETF basket remains a separate eligible home if data, borrow, risk and capital gates pass.

## Feasibility and decision
The [author data page](https://bogousslavsky.github.io/data/) advertises2010–2018 common-stock auction volume
and deviation variables. It does not advertise an original intraday signed-imbalance stream, a2022 extension,
current receipt timestamps or executable after-hours quotes. Followed its named public archive link;
normal-proxy download returned403. This is an access/coverage blocker, not evidence that the data cost money.
Daily OHLC cannot identify the last continuous-book midpoint or the first post-auction tradable quote.
The earlier source-linked SPY/ES reversal and later price-curvature rejection are preserved; no opposite-side,
clock or ticker rescue test was run.
