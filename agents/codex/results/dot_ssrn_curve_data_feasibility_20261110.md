# dot: native-contract data feasibility
Author: dot. 2026-11-10. Documentation review only; no data purchased, downloaded, tested or admitted.

## Decision
Continuous OHLCV does not supply simultaneous first/second/third/deferred maturities. No comprehensive free, no-signup native-contract panel was verified. These sources are potential acquisition routes, not evidence of owned usable data or a recommendation to purchase.

## Required fields
Basis Momentum needs paired contemporaneous contract histories; Relative Basis needs three maturities; curve momentum needs a maturity strip. Preserve contract identifiers, delivery month, settlement status, first-notice/last-trade dates, volume/OI publication availability, and explicit selection/roll rules. A continuous series with roll flags still omits unselected maturities.

## Documented sources
- **CME DataMine Standard EOD:** contract delivery year/month, settlement, OHLC, volume and OI. Earliest pit history varies: CBOT 1972, COMEX 1975, CME 1982, NYMEX 1986; some fields start November 2010. “Contract Day” is a daily-option field, not a futures expiry calendar. Inactive contracts can lack settlements. Licensed access; a historical notice of free midnight files does not establish free deep-history entitlement. [Schema](https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457225547), [access FAQ](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/faqs/access-to-cme-group-settlement-data-faq.html).
- **Norgate:** individual contracts distinct from continuous products, selective approximately 100-market coverage, generally around 1980/inception. OHLC, settlement-based Close, volume/OI, dated symbols and notice/trading dates. Historical volume/OI can be rescaled after contract-size changes. Windows subscription required for retrieval. Public listed pricing checked today: USD 148.50/6 months or 270/12 months. Personal noncommercial license permits conditional private cloud processing, not sharing source data; exports/backups generally must be deleted after subscription expiry, while derived research results may remain. Exact basket completeness still unverified. [Package](https://norgatedata.com/futurespackage.php), [data semantics](https://norgatedata.com/data-package-faq.php), [metadata interface](https://pypi.org/project/norgatedata/), [license FAQ](https://norgatedata.com/faq.php).
- **Databento GLBX.MDP3:** CME-group contracts since June 2010. Statistics distinguish preliminary/final settlement, cleared volume/OI and reference dates; volume/OI may arrive after session end. Contracts with neither volume nor OI can lack settlements. Definitions include identity and last-eligible-trading expiration. Paid usage-based history, not anonymous public data; license rights remain dataset-specific. Later-period candidate only, not original long-history replication. [Specifications](https://databento.com/docs/datasets/glbx-mdp3), [definitions](https://databento.com/docs/schemas/instrument-definitions), [pricing](https://databento.com/pricing).
- **ICE EOD/MFT:** delivery-strip identity, OHLC, settlements, volume/OI; OI is added the following business day. Subscription plus separately purchasable older EOD history. Full-curve Settlement Snapshot has only previous-calendar-year onward history, and its older snapshots are explicitly excluded from purchasable Back History. Older strip completeness and expiry reference data unresolved. [Official guide, §§ 2–5](https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/futures/ICE_Futures_Data_MFT_User_Guide.pdf).
- **CFTC COT:** genuinely public free positioning history, not price curves. Legacy futures-only from 1986, combined from 1995, disaggregated/TFF history from 2006. Pre-September 1992 observations are not weekly; grain units changed 1998. Match actual publication availability rather than Tuesday position date. [Archives](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/HistoricalCompressed/index.htm), [release schedule](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/ReleaseSchedule/index.htm), [public-domain policy](https://www.cftc.gov/WebPolicy/index.htm).

## Avoid false shortcuts
CME's own [continuous series](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/cme-group-continuous-price-series.html) selects front/active contracts rather than preserving the whole curve. Public website prices are reference viewing data; [website terms](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/market-data-explanation-disclaimer.html) restrict extraction, compilation, redistribution and AI-related use without authorization. Do not scrape those pages as a supposedly license-free research panel.

## Practical next decision
First specify the exact universe, years, contract-selection and release-time requirements. Then verify a licensed sample against that specification before any acquisition decision. COT's free positioning leg makes it more accessible than full-curve research, but validated price returns and timestamp mapping remain separate gates. No current-data qualification is granted by this audit.
