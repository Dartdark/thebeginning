## Baltic Dry Index
[SSRN 1747345](https://ssrn.com/abstract=1747345), conference duplicate [1787757](https://ssrn.com/abstract=1787757). [January 2011 manuscript transcription](https://www.academia.edu/106409072/The_Baltic_Dry_Index_as_a_Predictor_of_Global_Stock_Returns_Commodity_Returns_and_Global_Economic_Activity) inspected; later revision parity unverified.
Three-month BDI log change predicts monthly returns, May 1985–September 2010, with recursive 120/180-month estimation. Crucially, commodity targets are Moody's, Reuters and CRB spot indexes. Appendix A excludes futures indexes. Portfolio Sharpe/utility results concern stock allocation.

Assessment: no established executable commodity-futures strategy. Demand predictability is not automatically an unpriced futures signal. A native-roll, costed monthly adaptation would be new research. [Baltic's 2018 definition change](https://www.balticexchange.com/en/data-services/Circulars/market-announcements-/category-a/2018/circular-08-18---changes-to-the-baltic-dry-index--bdi-.html) removed Handysize and changed weights to 40/30/30; historical definitions, licensing and clocks matter.

## Satellite oil-demand nowcasting
[SSRN 6316019](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6316019), *Can Satellites Predict Oil Demand?* [ECB full paper](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3198~e3858c52a3.en.pdf).
Sentinel-5P NO₂ begins July 2018. Daily signal combines NRTI days 0–7 and OFFL days 8–27, then monthly averages. OFFL uses later weather information; eight-day lag is explicit. January 2020–December 2024 ten-country evaluation predicts oil-demand RMSE, not futures returns. Estimation-through-t−1 wording versus two-month target delays needs label-availability reconciliation.

Assessment: promising distinct data method, not a trading strategy. Need authentic product/target vintages and a separate causal futures signal. No source holding horizon or net evidence. [Copernicus terms](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions) permit open Sentinel access subject to legal notice; this does not establish unlimited processing resources or present ownership of a usable dataset.
