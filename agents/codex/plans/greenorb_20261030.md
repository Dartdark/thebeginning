## Frozen rule and clocks

- NQ 1-minute exports in CT, valued at exactly one MNQ, $2/point, 0.25-point tick. MNQ did not exist for all early
  design dates; those are explicitly hypothetical micro-valued NQ observations. No continuous-contract/native match claimed.
- OR is wall NY 09:30–09:45, equivalent to close-stamped CT columns511..525. Three five-minute candles end at
  CT08:35/08:40/08:45; all strictly green allow long, all strictly red allow short, otherwise no entry.
- VWAP is observed 1-minute typical-price volume VWAP after prior 16:00 CT reset. Include prior evening and current
  prefix only; incomplete overnight prefixes remain flagged proxy observations. No RTH replacement, missing-bar price
  fill, inferred true-zero volume or retrospective signal repair.
- First qualifying five-minute close strictly outside OR and on correct VWAP side, allowed direction only, from
  NY09:50 through12:00 inclusive. Signal-close to opposite OR stop must be strictly below1% of signal close.
- Fill at next minute OPEN, adverse2ticks; target1.5R anchored to that slipped fill, rounded outward to native tick.
  Stop fixed at opposite OR. Entry-bar protection active. Target requires at least1tick through, pays no slippage.
  Stops pay adverse2ticks, adverse opening gaps fill from open. Candle path source convention:
  up/doji O-L-H-C; down O-H-L-C. Path is an assumption, not reconstructed ticks.
- Remaining position exits at wall NY15:58 OPEN (close-stamped CT14:59 bar OPEN, column899), before that bar's wick.
  One trade/day at most, no reentry, no percentage-risk sizing, no limit-entry experiment.
- Incomplete required CT511..899 OHLCV paths remain unknown; exclude before outcomes and report them. No fabricated
  early-close liquidation or carry through the halt. Fewer than1,000 resolved design days stops as inadequate data.
- Costs: $1.24 MNQ RT commission, market/stop2ticks each. Author fee-only sensitivity $1.04. Stress4ticks per
  market/stop fill rebuilds entry and R target on identical signals. These are cost sensitivities, not extra signal variants.
