# TEST_01: pre-specified audit of the Combined Vault Break filter

Recorded before running strategy results. Branch: `codex/tests`.

## Rule and mechanism

Rebuild the existing long-only `candidates24.vault_break24` with `Grid24` and
`grid.execute`: 30-minute decision close from 10:00 through before 14:30 CT,
midnight open + 0.30 times prior 15-weekday daily ATR, above midnight VWAP,
40-point target, 75-point stop, 14:30 flat, at most three trades per day.
Preserve the tool's early-close schedule and next-minute market entry.
Combined permits decision closes through 12:30 inclusive and requires
(close - the SAME 30-minute midnight VWAP) / ATR <= 0.50.

Proposed mechanisms: later trades have less time to reach the target; distant
VWAP entries may reverse. Neither identifies a forced participant. These are
claims to audit, not explanations established by a higher average trade.
Closest overlap: Vault Break itself (including its funded single-print sizing).

## Variants and chronology

Exactly four fixed configurations: Base, Runway alone, Stretch alone, Combined.
Three filter trials; the supplied agent already tried the single filters before
Combined, so this is a replication of a selected rule, not fresh preregistration
of an unseen discovery. No threshold search or settings changes after results.
Design: 2018-2022. Freeze Combined as supplied; reveal 2023+ once in this run.
Both periods and every available year use the same data dates and costs.
Claim-cost recalculation is an accounting audit, not a strategy variant.

fill, no target slippage, one-point target trade-through). Also show the stated
$17.25 commission convention and distinguish trade versus daily drawdown.
Whole = longs; shorts have zero trades and no estimable statistics.
Report full metrics, yearly and design/holdout results, removed-base trades'
mean and t, and new trades created by changed execution state. Do not assume
executed filtered trades are a subset: rerun signals through the engine.

Run 5,000 matched-count random deletions of base trades in each period, judging
TOTAL net and drawdown separately and jointly; additionally randomize signals
and rerun execution on the holdout to check state effects. Use 5,000 ten-day
block bootstraps and daily permutations, plus paired bootstrap of Combined
minus Base. Random-time/random-side controls preserve exits and daily counts.
Monte Carlo at 2 MNQ = 0.20 NQ and actual evaluation size 8 MNQ = 0.80 NQ;
show one-year $2,000 EOD-trailing hit risk, not just historical drawdown.

Use the existing funded Lab and Boot simulator with report-derived current plan
components, including VB 2/1 single-print sizing, ES MOC 2 MES and Relative
Strength longs 1 MNQ. Test Combined as a replacement for the same-sized VB

1,000 paired 20-day-block histories; show full and 2023+ evidence and uncertainty.
