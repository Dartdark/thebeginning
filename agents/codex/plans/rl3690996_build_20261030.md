## Frozen candidates and controls

Per instrument: four candidate allocation series, PPO, A2C, DDPG and a preceding-quarter Sharpe selector among them.
Three fixed controls are counted too: stocks equal-weight, equal-weight plus turbulence, positive 63-day momentum
plus turbulence; futures RTH long, RTH long plus turbulence, MACD-sign plus turbulence. Four candidate variants,
three diagnostic controls, seven return definitions per adaptation; no parameter selection from design results.
Every definition, failed candidate and training seed is retained in the run record. No scout budget is reset or used.

Primary seed 3690996, 8,192 training steps per algorithm/quarter, CPU and single Torch thread. PPO: MLP 64/64,
n_steps256, batch64, n_epochs5, lr.0003, gamma.99, gae.95. A2C: MLP64/64, n_steps64, lr.0007, gamma.99, gae.95.
DDPG: MLP64/64, lr.001, buffer50,000, starts512, batch64, tau.005, gamma.99, train every4 steps/one gradient,
OU noise sigma.1. No hyperparameter search. Seeds17/29 are reserved only if primary design passes, not replacements
for an unfavorable seed. Stable-Baselines3 2.4.1, Gymnasium1.0.0, Torch2.5.1 CPU.

Training is expanding. Each trade quarter fits on data before the preceding 63 eligible trading sessions, validates
on those 63 sessions, and trades only after validation ends. Models retrain quarterly. Validation policies start
flat with the same capital/risk. Highest annualized daily Sharpe wins; ties prefer PPO then A2C then DDPG.
Training uses chronological episodes drawn only from the fit period; no transition may finish outside fit dates.
Past data may initialize indicators, but no future observations fit transforms, turbulence or policy parameters.

## Daily-stock adaptation

Raw author-public panel, SHA/hash receipts from prior review. Exclude DD/RTX before outcomes; remaining28 names
are fixed. Training begins after features are available (2011 onward); design2018–2019. Reserved2020 through final
raw date only if design passes. This limited available source history does not establish canonical2023+ validation.
Initial capital$10,000, cash-funded long-only, 10% target-weight cap per stock; no dividends/borrow or leverage.
Action positive part normalized to <=100% total weight, then individually clipped at10%, leaving excess in cash.
State: cash fraction, holdings weights and causal return/MACD/RSI/CCI/ADX features. Fixed scales, no fitted scaler.
Targets sized in integer raw shares from close-t equity/prices; execute at next-session raw open with5bp adverse
slippage plus10bp commission each side. Sell first; buy orders are clipped to fee-inclusive arrival cash. This
arrival-quote sizing/fill remains a daily-bar execution proxy, not certified opening-auction routing. Record clamps.
Factor-ratio splits adjust shares before next open; unresolved corporate-action cash is a certification limit.
Turbulence: current close return vector vs previous252 returns, fixed half diagonal covariance shrinkage; threshold
95th percentile of previous252 turbulence scores. Risk-off targets zero; no backward fill or full-sample threshold.

## Intraday MNQ adaptation

One-minute owned NQ bars, five-minute RTH decision closes08:35–14:55 CT, fill next minute's open, final target0 at
14:55 (exit14:56). Only complete observed08:31–15:00 sessions; census every exclusion, do not fill missing bars.
This is conditional observed-grid evidence, not a claim missing days have zero return. Past2017 trains first fold;
design2018–2022; historical2023+ only if design passes. Early-close/incomplete sessions have no invented fills.
Actions map to -1/0/+1 MNQ using thresholds -1/3,+1/3. Later150K-size diagnostic is2MNQ, not fractional lots.
Stops: fixed initial .5 prior15-session RTH ATR, tick-rounded, capped by remaining$250 session loss budget per MNQ.
Stops are active in the entry minute, fill at stop or worse opening gap plus1 adverse tick. Daily loss latch and
final flatten override model actions; after a stop the model may re-enter while its daily budget remains positive.
Market fills pay .62 commission per contract per side plus1 tick (.50USD) adverse price each side; target reversals
pay both closed/open legs. Evaluate minute adverse equity and retain complete fills; no EOD-only Apex claim.
State: causal technical features, prior ATR, turbulence, position/open P&L, daily P&L and session-clock terms.
Turbulence uses past252 five-minute returns/scores with the same95% past-only rule. Risk-off target0; fixed
controls with overlay share the same stops/loss-budget/flatten rules. Training episodes are complete past sessions.
