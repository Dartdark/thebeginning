## Signal

Use the shared close-stamped CT minute grid. CME-session anchor 17:00 CT: complete 240-minute windows ending 21:00,01:00,05:00,09:00,13:00; the final 13:00–16:00 session stub is not a 4-hour signal/pivot bar. Expected maintenance/weekend gaps are excluded from minute-path completeness checks. Any incomplete expected four-hour window resets pivot/structure state. Complete bars are not filled across missing data. Weekend closed windows are skipped; other unverified holiday gaps remain unknown, not zero.

A swing high/low is strictly higher/lower than all L neighboring bars on EACH side. It becomes available at the close of the Rth subsequent complete 4h bar. Require the confirmation neighborhood to have consecutive full windows apart from the deliberate session stub/weekend closures; invalid expected bars reset the neighborhood. Equal extrema do not qualify. Keep the last two confirmed highs and lows. When new pivots arrive, HH+HL establishes bullish state, LH+LL bearish state. A bullish state with previous completed close >= latest confirmed low and current close < that low produces one short; bearish state with previous close <= latest confirmed high and current close > that high produces one long. Stop is the latest confirmed opposite swing high for short / low for long. The break flips state. Consume each broken pivot once per direction; no repeated entries at a recycled level. Both sides mirror. No EMA/filter/ATR rule added.
