## The single proposed input question and why it does not advance

**Participant/cause:** physical-gold ETF authorized participants may create/redeem shares against bullion and may use futures or OTC hedges while obtaining or delivering that bullion. This differs from the daily leverage-reset arithmetic of UGL/GLL. It does not mean every basket creates a new directional GC order: an AP may already hold bullion, hedge elsewhere, internally offset orders or have completed the hedge before the published holdings change.

**Known prior counterpart:** at7f0800f, `checks/candidates/gc_combo/GC_COMBO_v2_community.txt:13` explicitly defines voteC as GLD ETF holdings changing by at least one creation basket yesterday, with direction+1/−1/0 supplied by Data3. `analysis/gc_combo_test.py` consumes that encoded vote and tests individual votes and the blend without each vote. The current TIER_LIST's GC_COMBO entry records its MC check and lack of plan gain. Thus a next-session GC trade/filter in the direction of yesterday's GLD holdings change is already selected prior research. It cannot be presented as an untested ordinary-ETF mechanism or reopened merely by dropping other votes, changing size or imposing another clock.

**Input availability actually established:** the7f tree has the already used encoded `checks/candidates/gc_combo/GCCOMBO.txt` and source code consuming it. The scoped `data/` filename inventory did not establish an underlying original creation-order/confirmation panel, AP hedge inventory, basket-settlement obligations or historical receipt/vintage records for2018–22. The existing encoded GLD vote is not a substitute for those prospective inputs. A date/time in a prepared indicator file cannot establish its original publication or hedge-execution deadline.

**Time and size:** no authenticated predecision basket-order size, remaining unhedged quantity, mandatory GC side, contract month or execution deadline was established. No trade clock, threshold, magnitude relative to liquidity or native-cost gross effect is invented. Owning continuous GC price filenames does not repair this participant/input problem. Low correlation or a new book contribution was not measured.

A genuinely distinct later question would require original, available-before-entry basket/order evidence and the applicable primary protocol, plus a supported bridge from an outstanding obligation to the specific futures hedge. Realized holdings after settlement, a current prospectus alone or a daily price move cannot supply that bridge.
