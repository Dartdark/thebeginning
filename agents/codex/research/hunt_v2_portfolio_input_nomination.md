## Risk mechanism before any values

The participant is this trader allocating finite personal-book risk, not an invented external forced seller. A new LDF ticket adds NQ delta to an already active morning relative-strength position. When that same-side earlier position is underwater, the market has moved against its actual entry while that exposure remains outstanding. A second ticket can deepen the same-direction loss concentration. Active quantity alone ignores that path/cost-basis information. The prediction needed for a qualifying filter is stronger than “smaller size lowers DD”: the omitted LDF tickets must lose money and their omission must improve a current plan versus BASE and EMPTY.

That is a falsifiable risk/conditional-return hypothesis. Independent late reset demand could instead rescue the morning position, making the removed tickets profitable. RS may be uninformative once fixed-clock price movement is controlled. No exposure amount, common loss rate, count, sign advantage or book benefit is measured here. The rule does not flatten, hedge, resize or change the stop/exit of the existing RS position.

LDF is a source-clock choice, not a favorable-result choice: its fixed late decision permits an already observed morning position state. Current personal19 contains both RS both-sides and LDF at1MNQ. Their source orders remain unchanged. Define gross mark sign as `RSside * (completed_decision_close - RS_actual_entry) < 0`; using gross price P&L avoids an artificial negative state caused solely by commission. Zero is not negative. No fitted currency-loss cutoff is needed.
