# Owned-data-first academic strategy search

User instruction2026-10-29: search primarily for hypotheses we can actually backtest with data already owned.
A worthwhile data-dependent lead is recorded once and passed over; it must not dominate the cycle or repeatedly
trigger acquisition attempts/alerts. This updates search priority, not evidence, preregistration or cost gates.

## Before spending a full review or a trial

1. Refresh the owned filename/schema inventory and prior data audits. Map every proposed feature and traded
   instrument to an actual owned field, information clock and design-period history. File names are not proof of
   row completeness, contract identity, historical availability, data rights or native micro fills.
2. Search the compatible hypothesis families below, then deduplicate exact economic definitions against
   TIER_LIST, WATCHLIST, TODO, results and scout history. Different ticker/window/title alone is not novelty.
3. Prefer papers with obtainable full text, executable timing, costs and out-of-sample evidence. Use recent
   2–3year research and publicly visible download rankings as discovery aids when available, not quality proof.
4. If a required input is absent, make a bounded owned/free availability check only when warranted, save the
   specific promising lead/prerequisites and move on. An unchanged blocked lead is not unfinished eligible work.
5. An eligible hypothesis triggers autonomous development, committed preregistration/code, design gates and
   every earned current-portfolio comparison. Do not stop to announce a lead or ask whether to test.

## Owned inputs and search implications

| Input family | Suitable research directions | Limits to check first |
|---|---|---|
| ES/NQ/YM/GC/CL/ZN/6E/BTC named1-minute exports | Intraday/session timing, scheduled-flow price reactions, cross-market hypotheses | Exact sessions/holes/bar clocks, contract/roll identity and native costs; file years are not coverage certification |
| NQ order-flow exports | New source-supported flow hypotheses with actual matching fields | Publication/capture timing and schema eras; many imbalance/big-print definitions already failed |
| VIX/VXN daily | Lagged volatility-state or risk-scaling hypotheses | Daily close available later; not an intraday IV surface, VIX term structure or variance-swap payoff |
| GEX/GEXOI exports | Only hypotheses matching the actual index/field/period | NDX data are not signed SPX dealer books; limited history cannot silently replace2018–22 design |
| COT legacy/financial archives and COT_NQ | Lagged publicly available positioning/risk-premium hypotheses | Tuesday position date differs from publication; not observed CTA orders or an index-trader supplement |
| Owned auctions, earnings and acquired macro calendars | Scheduled-event hypotheses with verified definitions/timestamps | Full paper event universe, as-of availability, holiday exceptions and release overlap |

Absent until demonstrated otherwise: paired delivery-month histories, full historical auction imbalance feeds,
signed SPX dealer inventory, intraday option surfaces and futures curves. Do not infer these from continuous
prices, OHLC, unsigned OI or daily VIX. Keep source/data-blocked leads, including weekly basis reversal, parked
until genuinely new inputs arrive; do not let them displace compatible research.

## Skip in routine scouting

Moving-average/indicator papers (MACD, RSI, Hurst), surveys/behavioral questionnaires, single-country emerging
market studies, regulation/policy-only papers and crypto pump-and-dump. Do not force an index-futures strategy
from those subjects. Explicit later user requests still take precedence.
