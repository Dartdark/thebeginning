## Mechanism judgment

VIX and VIX3M represent different implied-volatility tenors. VIX>VIX3M indicates front-loaded implied variance demand/expectations; it does not itself measure a tradable futures curve, dealer gamma, net futures inventory or an actual margin call. Urgent downside insurance demand and constrained closing sellers could leave an overnight risk concession after a down day. That contingent mechanism is plausible enough for a fixed existing-leg filter, but unmeasured. Near-term scheduled news or informed selling can instead justify the premium and produce continued losses. Passive liquidity providers may require compensation rather than face an obligation to buy at a known minute.

No participant amount, compulsory NQ order, liquidation deadline, available hedge size, new entry time or predicted dollar edge is established here. Retaining the original whole-contract parent avoids inventing these. This belongs to the independent-risk-input/filter priority, not a discovery of a scheduled forced order. The other agent/root must establish the actual parent, same-session versus prior-session index knowledge, Sunday/holiday reuse, missing-input treatment and executable clocks before a TEST can reach values.
